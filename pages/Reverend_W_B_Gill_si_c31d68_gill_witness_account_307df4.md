## Introduction

Reverend William B. Gill’s account became the centre of the 1959 Boianai UFO controversy because it combined an unusually detailed personal testimony with reports from many other observers at the Anglican mission in Papua New Guinea. Gill was not initially a committed believer in UFO claims; he described himself as cautious and demanded evidence before accepting extraordinary explanations. His later report was therefore influential because supporters saw it as the account of a reluctant witness whose views changed after a direct experience.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59fsr.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, FSR article...</span></span></span>

The importance of Gill’s testimony lies not only in what he claimed to have seen, but in how the event unfolded socially. Mission residents, teachers and local workers gathered to watch the objects, discussed what they were observing, signed statements, and later repeated many of the same core details. At the same time, critics argued that group expectations, environmental conditions and interpretation errors could have shaped the reports. The Boianai witnesses remain significant because the case sits at the difficult boundary between a well-recorded human experience and an unresolved explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59letters.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, the letters...</span></span></span>

## Gill's role and changing views on UFO reports

Before the main June 1959 observations, Gill was approached by mission staff and local residents who reported unusual lights in the sky. His early attitude was sceptical. Rather than immediately accepting claims of unknown craft, he recorded observations and considered more conventional possibilities, including atmospheric or electrical phenomena. His surviving correspondence shows that he was interested in investigating the reports but remained cautious about claims of visitors from space.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59fsr.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, FSR article...</span></span></span>

Gill’s own written report later became one of the strongest reasons the Boianai case attracted attention. In the document prepared after the events, he described himself as the principal observer and recorder, while also distinguishing between his personal impressions and the observational details he had collected. This distinction mattered because it showed an attempt to separate what witnesses saw from conclusions about what the object might have been.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-au-a703-580-1-1-part2-a703-580-1-1-part-2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufotransparency.com.</span></span></span>

The change in Gill’s position was gradual rather than a simple conversion to a UFO belief system. He continued to express uncertainty about the origin of the objects, but he became convinced that the specific events he had witnessed could not easily be dismissed. This combination of personal conviction and continued caution contributed to his reputation among UFO researchers as a more measured witness than many contemporary claimants.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59fsr.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, FSR article...</span></span></span>

## The 26 and 27 June observations

### The first major gathering

On the evening of 26 June 1959, Gill and others at Boianai observed a bright object that appeared to approach and remain visible over the area. The sighting developed from an individual observation into a group event as mission residents came outside to watch. Accounts associated with Gill’s report describe a large object with unusual lights and a surface or structure that some observers interpreted as a craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59fsr.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, FSR article...</span></span></span>

A major feature of the witnesses’ descriptions was the apparent presence of figures associated with the object. Observers reported seeing shapes that looked like people on or near the upper part of the object. These descriptions became the most debated element of the case because they moved the report beyond an unexplained light in the sky into what later UFO literature classified as an “entity” encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59fsr.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, FSR article...</span></span></span>

Gill’s notes and later accounts emphasised that several people were watching at the same time rather than relying on a single memory. Reports connected with the case state that around 38 people witnessed the events, with a group signing a statement afterwards. The witnesses included mission personnel and local residents, creating a mixture of observers with different backgrounds.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59fsr.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, FSR article...</span></span></span>

### The reported interaction on 27 June

The following evening produced the most famous part of Gill’s testimony. Witnesses again reported seeing an object above Boianai, and Gill described apparent figures on the object. According to his account, he raised his hand and waved, after which one of the figures appeared to respond with a similar movement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59fsr.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, FSR article...</span></span></span>

The reported exchange attracted particular attention because it suggested deliberate interaction rather than a passive observation. Some witnesses also attempted to signal with a torch, and Gill interpreted movements of the object as possible responses. Supporters of the case argue that these details make the report unusual because several observers appeared to perceive a similar sequence of events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59fsr.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, FSR article...</span></span></span>

However, the interpretation of these moments remains disputed. A perceived wave or response depends heavily on how observers interpreted distant shapes, lighting effects and movement in the sky. The event’s strongest evidence is therefore not a physical trace, but the consistency and timing of multiple human observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59letters.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, the letters...</span></span></span>

## How multiple witnesses described the event

The Boianai witnesses were important because their accounts did not originate solely from Gill. Mission residents reportedly gathered together during the sightings, and statements were collected afterwards. This gave investigators material beyond a single eyewitness narrative, although the quality of witness evidence still depended on memory, perception and later retelling.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59fsr.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, FSR article...</span></span></span>

## Why the Gill witnesses drew attention

The lasting interest in Gill’s account comes from the combination of credibility, documentation and uncertainty. Gill was viewed by many investigators as a careful observer, his report was written close to the time of the events, and multiple witnesses supported important parts of the story. These features made Boianai one of the more frequently discussed mass-witness UFO cases of the twentieth century.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-au-a703-580-1-1-part2-a703-580-1-1-part-2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufotransparency.com.</span></span></span>

At the same time, the witnesses did not provide physical evidence that could independently confirm what they saw. The Australian Air Force investigation considered the observers reliable but suggested that natural explanations, including bright planets and atmospheric conditions, could account for at least some aspects of the sightings. The unresolved disagreement is therefore not mainly about whether people reported something unusual, but about what best explains those reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59letters.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, the letters...</span></span></span>

Gill’s account remains significant because it illustrates both the strength and limitation of eyewitness evidence. A respected witness, detailed records and multiple observers can make a case compelling, but they do not automatically determine the cause of an event. The Boianai testimony continues to be studied precisely because it preserves a detailed record of what people believed they saw while leaving the final explanation contested.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/papua59letters.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufo Phenomenon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo Phenomenonufo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Father Gill sighting, Papua - New Guinea, 1959, the letters...</span></span></span>
