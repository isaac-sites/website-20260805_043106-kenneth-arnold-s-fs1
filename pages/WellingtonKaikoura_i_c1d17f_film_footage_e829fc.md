## Introduction

The Kaikōura film footage is the most recognisable piece of visual evidence from the Wellington/Kaikōura incident of 1978. Recorded from a Safe Air Argosy cargo aircraft on the night of 30–31 December 1978 by television cameraman David Crockett, the 16mm colour film captured bright lights that appeared to move in relation to the aircraft and became the image most associated with the case. The footage gave the incident a lasting place in UFO history because it transformed a series of pilot and radar reports into a physical recording that could be examined frame by frame. However, the film did not provide a definitive identification of the lights. It remains a disputed record: some analysts have argued that it shows distant but ordinary light sources affected by aviation perspective and camera optics, while others maintain that the movements and brightness remain difficult to explain.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ngataonga.org.nz/search-use-collection/search/F1910/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ngataonga.org.nz">[Ngā Taonga Sound &amp; Vision]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ngataonga.org.nz</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ngā Taonga Sound &amp; Vision UFO: A TRUE STORY</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ngā Taonga Sound &amp; Vision UFO: A TRUE STORY</span></span></span>

## The 30–31 December filming encounter

The famous footage was not originally intended as a UFO investigation. After earlier Kaikōura sightings involving Safe Air crews had attracted public attention, a television team travelled aboard a routine cargo flight to reconstruct the story for a news report. Reporter Quentin Fogarty and cameraman David Crockett joined the aircraft, which was operated by Captain Bill Startup and First Officer Robert Guard. During the flight, unusual lights were again reported, and Crockett filmed several of them through the aircraft window.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ngataonga.org.nz/search-use-collection/search/F1910/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ngataonga.org.nz">[Ngā Taonga Sound &amp; Vision]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ngataonga.org.nz</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ngā Taonga Sound &amp; Vision UFO: A TRUE STORY</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ngā Taonga Sound &amp; Vision UFO: A TRUE STORY</span></span></span>

The resulting film showed bright points of light against the night sky. In some sequences, the light appeared to change size, intensity or position as the camera operator adjusted focus and zoom. Supporters of the UFO interpretation have pointed to these apparent changes as evidence that the object was manoeuvring near the aircraft. The footage also became more influential because the lights were observed by several people on board, including the flight crew and television personnel, rather than being a recording made by an unknown observer.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ngataonga.org.nz/search-use-collection/search/F1910/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ngataonga.org.nz">[Ngā Taonga Sound &amp; Vision]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ngataonga.org.nz</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ngā Taonga Sound &amp; Vision UFO: A TRUE STORY</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ngā Taonga Sound &amp; Vision UFO: A TRUE STORY</span></span></span>

The film was later examined as part of wider [investigations]({{ 'investigations-f80150/' | relative_url }}) into the incident. The Royal New Zealand Air Force investigation files included material relating to the December 1978 encounters, including witness statements, radar-related documentation and analysis connected with the television footage. The files were released publicly decades later, allowing researchers to examine the official response alongside the original claims.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://disclosurearchives.com/government-archives/new-zealand-nzdf-ufo-files" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: disclosurearchives.com">[Disclosure Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">disclosurearchives.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on disclosurearchives.com.</span></span></span>

### The footage as documentary evidence

The film’s greatest strength is that it preserves an actual event rather than relying only on memory. A recording can be reviewed, analysed and compared with other information from the same time period. This made the Kaikōura footage unusual among UFO reports of the era, when most cases depended almost entirely on witness descriptions.

At the same time, a recording of a light in the sky is not automatically a recording of an identifiable object. The film does not contain a clear outline, surface detail, reference point for scale or independent measurement of distance. What it records with certainty is the appearance of bright lights on film; determining their physical source requires interpretation. This distinction became the foundation of the later debate.

## Camera limits and optical effects

One of the main difficulties in interpreting the Kaikōura footage is that night-time aerial filming is technically challenging. A camera looking through an aircraft window is affected by several variables: reflections from the glass, vibration, movement of the aircraft, lens characteristics, exposure settings and the absence of nearby objects that provide scale.

These factors matter because a distant light source can appear to move dramatically when viewed from a moving aircraft. Changes in the aircraft’s heading, altitude or viewing angle can alter the apparent position of a light without the light itself moving in an unusual way. Investigators who favour conventional explanations have argued that the footage may show distant sources such as fishing boats, with apparent motion produced by perspective and camera effects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://zfilesuap.com/en/cases/kaikoura-1978" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zfilesuap.com">[The Z Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zfilesuap.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">kaikoura 1978</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Z FilesKaikoura Lights (1978) · Z 檔案...</span></span></span>

The most prominent conventional interpretation developed from analysis by New Zealand scientific authorities. The Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR) examined the case and argued that some of the visual reports could be explained by a combination of ordinary sources, including lights from fishing vessels, astronomical objects and optical effects. Later commentary on the official investigation has highlighted that analysts considered the camera system itself an important factor when interpreting the apparent behaviour of the filmed lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://zfilesuap.com/en/cases/kaikoura-1978" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zfilesuap.com">[The Z Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zfilesuap.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">kaikoura 1978</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Z FilesKaikoura Lights (1978) · Z 檔案...</span></span></span>

The footage was also examined by American optical physicist Bruce Maccabee, who approached the material from a different perspective. He argued that the characteristics of the filmed light were inconsistent with some proposed explanations and concluded that the film represented an unidentified phenomenon rather than a simple misidentification. His analysis became one of the most frequently cited arguments in support of the footage’s significance, although it was disputed by other investigators.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaik%C5%8Dura_lights" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Kaikōura lights</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Kaikōura lights</span></span></span>

The disagreement illustrates a wider problem in analysing unusual aerial footage: the same visual features can be interpreted in opposite ways. A changing light may represent an object changing position, or it may represent a stationary or distant source being viewed through changing optical conditions.

## Competing interpretations of the footage

The Kaikōura film remains controversial because different analysts prioritise different parts of the evidence. Those who regard it as strong evidence of an unidentified object emphasise the combination of professional witnesses, filming during an active sighting, and the apparent relationship between the light and the aircraft.     var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
