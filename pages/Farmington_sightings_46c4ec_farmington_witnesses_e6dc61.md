## Introduction

The [Farmington sightings]({{ 'farmington-sightings-1950/' | relative_url }}) of March 1950 became famous because the reports did not come from a single observer but from a large number of people watching the same portion of sky. Residents, local officials, newspaper employees, former military personnel and visitors to Farmington, New Mexico, described groups of bright objects moving above the town, with the largest wave occurring on 17 March. The strength of the case lies in its unusually broad witness base: many people reported seeing something unusual at roughly the same time. Its weakness is that the evidence remains almost entirely observational, with no recovered material, photographs accepted as authentic, radar records or physical measurements that independently confirm what the witnesses saw.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/500316farmington_report2.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportMarch 17, 1950...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 17, 1950</span></span></span>

## Local witnesses and community reports

The best-known episode occurred on 17 March 1950, when reports spread through Farmington during the late morning. Contemporary newspaper accounts described residents stopping in streets, looking upward and contacting the *Farmington Daily Times* to report unusual objects. The newspaper reported that calls came in from multiple observers and that many residents believed they had seen a large formation of strange aerial objects. Estimates of the number varied widely, from a few objects to several hundred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/press/farmingtondailytimes18mar1950.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: the newspapers, the Farmington case in the Press, 1950March 18, 1950...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 18, 1950</span></span></span>

The range of people reporting the event was one reason the sighting gained attention. The witnesses included ordinary residents, business owners, newspaper staff and people with military backgrounds. This variety mattered because it reduced the impression that the report came from one isolated individual or a single misunderstanding. Later investigators interested in UFO reports highlighted Farmington as a multiple-witness and multiple-object case, noting that people across the town appeared to have been observing the same general phenomenon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufology.patrickgross.org/books/mcdonaldhcsa68lone.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufology.patrickgross.org">[Ufology]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufology.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Professor James E. ### Clayton J. Boddy and other named observers

Among the frequently cited witnesses was Clayton J. Boddy, business manager of the *Farmington Daily Times* and a former Army engineer officer. According to contemporary reporting, Boddy described seeing several moving objects that were followed by what appeared to be a much larger group. He estimated the objects were high in the sky and included a red object that appeared different from the others.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/press/farmingtondailytimes18mar1950.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: the newspapers, the Farmington case in the Press, 1950March 18, 1950...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 18, 1950</span></span></span>

Harold F. Thatcher, head of the local Soil Conservation Service office, provided one of the more technical eyewitness accounts. He attempted a rough triangulation estimate, using his understanding of measurements and perspective, and concluded that the object would have appeared to be travelling at an extremely high speed if it had been a conventional aircraft. His account is significant because it shows an effort by a witness to apply a measurement method rather than relying only on impression. However, such estimates still depended on assumptions about distance, size and identification of the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/press/farmingtondailytimes18mar1950.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: the newspapers, the Farmington case in the Press, 1950March 18, 1950...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 18, 1950</span></span></span>

Edward Brooks, a former B-29 tail gunner, was another often-mentioned witness. He stated that the objects’ movements did not resemble aircraft behaviour as he understood it from his wartime experience. Other witnesses similarly described unusual manoeuvres, including rapid changes in direction and apparent interactions between objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/press/farmingtondailytimes18mar1950.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: the newspapers, the Farmington case in the Press, 1950March 18, 1950...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 18, 1950</span></span></span>

## Descriptions of objects and movements

Witness descriptions shared several recurring features. Many observers reported bright, metallic or silvery objects that appeared disc-shaped. Some described groups moving together, while others reported individual objects moving differently from the main formation. The objects were commonly described as travelling without visible exhaust trails or engine noise.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/press/farmingtondailytimes18mar1950.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: the newspapers, the Farmington case in the Press, 1950March 18, 1950...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 18, 1950</span></span></span>

A particularly memorable element was the reported movement of the objects. Several witnesses claimed that they appeared to accelerate quickly, change direction sharply or move in ways they considered impossible for aircraft of the period. Some accounts described objects appearing to approach each other before separating or changing course. These reports helped create the image of the “Farmington UFO Armada” as a coordinated aerial display rather than a simple sighting of a single unknown object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/press/farmingtondailytimes18mar1950.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: the newspapers, the Farmington case in the Press, 1950March 18, 1950...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 18, 1950</span></span></span>

However, descriptions of motion are among the most difficult parts of any sky observation to evaluate. Without reliable information about distance, object size and viewing angle, an observer may struggle to distinguish between rapid movement and changes caused by perspective. A small object at high altitude can appear to move dramatically when crossing a viewer’s field of vision, while atmospheric conditions can alter brightness and apparent shape.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/press/farmingtondailytimes18mar1950.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: the newspapers, the Farmington case in the Press, 1950March 18, 1950...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 18, 1950</span></span></span>

The Farmington reports also included disagreement among observers. Some people described hundreds of objects, while others reported far smaller numbers. Differences in counting are not unusual in mass sightings: people entering an event at different times, watching from different locations or focusing on different parts of the sky may produce very different estimates.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/500316farmington_report2.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportMarch 17, 1950...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 17, 1950</span></span></span>

## The limits of eyewitness testimony

The number of witnesses is the strongest feature of the Farmington case, but it does not automatically identify the objects. A large number of people can confirm that an unusual sight was present without providing a definitive explanation for what caused it. Eyewitness testimony is valuable evidence of perception, but it is weaker evidence for the physical nature of an object unless supported by independent records such as photographs, instruments or controlled measurements.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Project BLUE BOOK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesProject BLUE BOOK - Unidentified Flying Objects &#124; National Archives...</span></span></span>

Several conventional [explanations]({{ 'explanations-77e10d/' | relative_url }}) have been discussed over the years. One line of investigation involved airborne material, including reports that some observers may have mistaken floating material for objects in the sky. A later explanation associated the event with the breakup of a high-altitude balloon, suggesting that fragments could have reflected sunlight and appeared as moving discs. This explanation has been debated among UFO researchers, with supporters arguing that it could account for the appearance of many bright objects and critics questioning whether it matches all reported movements.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Report_on_Unidentified_Flying_Objects/Chapter_6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: en.wikisource.org">[Wikisource]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">en.wikisource.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Report on Unidentified Flying Objects/Chapter 6</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Report on Unidentified Flying Objects/Chapter 6 - Wikisource, the free online library...</span></span></span>

The historical record therefore presents a mixed picture. The community response and number of observers make Farmington unusual among early UFO reports, while the absence of physical evidence limits what can be concluded. The witnesses provide strong evidence that many people in Farmington saw unusual objects or lights in the sky in March 1950; they provide less certainty about whether those observations represented extraordinary technology, a natural or human-made phenomenon, or a combination of factors.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufology.patrickgross.org/books/mcdonaldhcsa68lone.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufology.patrickgross.org">[Ufology]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufology.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: Professor James E. McDonald statement at the HCSA 1968July 29, 1968...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 29, 1968</span></span></span>

The enduring importance of the witness accounts is not that they settle the identity of the objects, but that they preserve one of the most notable examples of a mass aerial observation in the early flying-saucer era: a case where the central evidence was a whole community looking upward at the same time.

    return encodeURIComponent(String(value || '').replace(/\s+/g, ' ').trim()).replace(/%20/g, '+');
  }
  function buildUrl(query, reference, marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
