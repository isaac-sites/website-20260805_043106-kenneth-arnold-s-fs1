## What Vorilhon said happened in 1973

Before the alleged contact, Claude Vorilhon was known primarily as a journalist involved with motor racing. He published a car magazine and had a public identity separate from the religious movement he later created. According to accounts of his life, he reported the encounter shortly before publishing his first book describing the message he said he had received.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra%C3%ABl" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

Vorilhon’s account places the event on 13 December 1973 at Puy de Lassolas, an extinct volcanic area near Clermont-Ferrand. He said he saw a bright object descend and encountered a humanoid being who communicated with him in French. The being, whom he later identified as Yahweh, was described as a representative of the Elohim—a technologically advanced civilisation that Vorilhon said had created life on Earth through scientific methods.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rael.org/rael/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rael.org">[RAEL.ORG]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rael.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Last Prophet, Rael</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Last Prophet, Rael</span></span></span>

According to his later writings, the encounter was followed by a series of meetings over several days. Vorilhon claimed that these communications explained that traditional religious figures had misunderstood messages from the Elohim and that humanity’s creation was the result of advanced genetic engineering rather than supernatural creation. These ideas became the core of what he called the “Message” and were published in his 1974 book *Le Livre qui dit la vérité* (*The Book Which Tells the Truth*).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra%C3%ABl" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

## How the 1973 encounter became Raëlism

The immediate consequence of the claimed contact was not simply a UFO report but the creation of a new belief system. Vorilhon adopted the name Raël and began presenting himself as a messenger chosen to prepare humanity for contact with the Elohim. In 1974 he formed the organisation initially known as MADECH, an early version of what became the Raëlian Movement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra%C3%ABlism" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

[The movement]({{ 'the-movement/' | relative_url }})’s central claims combined extraterrestrial ideas with reinterpretations of religious traditions. The Elohim were presented not as gods in a supernatural sense, but as highly advanced beings whose technology appeared miraculous to ancient humans. This approach placed Raëlism within the broader category of UFO religions, movements in which alleged encounters with non-human beings become the basis for spiritual teachings or social programmes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eap-iea.org/index.php/eap/article/view/814" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eap-iea.org">[eap-iea.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eap-iea.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Religions – Beginnings and Main Forms &#124; Etnoantropološki problemi / Issues in Ethnology and AnthropologyApril 12, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 12, 2010</span></span></span>

A major theme of the message was the importance of science and human technological development. Later Raëlian positions, including support for human cloning research and criticism of traditional religious authority, grew from the worldview Vorilhon said he received during the 1973 contact.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://portal.research.lu.se/en/publications/new-religious-movements-and-science-raels-progressive-patronizing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: portal.research.lu.se">[Lund University]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">portal.research.lu.se</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">nd UniversityNew religious movements and science: Rael&#x27;s progressive patronizing parasitism - Lund University...</span></span></span>

## What evidence exists for the contact claim?

The strongest evidence for the 1973 event is documentary evidence of Vorilhon’s own testimony: his books, interviews, and the development of an organisation built around his account. These sources show that he consistently promoted the story and that it had a significant cultural impact. They do not, however, independently verify the physical reality of the encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra%C3%ABl" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

No publicly confirmed physical trace of the alleged spacecraft, the claimed extraterrestrial being, or any technology from the encounter has been produced. Unlike some UFO cases involving multiple witnesses, radar records, photographs, or material samples, the 1973 Vorilhon case is primarily a single-witness contact narrative supported by later writings and followers’ acceptance of his testimony.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/raelian-movement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: encyclopedia.com">[Encyclopedia.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">encyclopedia.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Raelian Movement &#124; Encyclopedia.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Raelian Movement &#124; Encyclopedia.com</span></span></span>

The location itself is known and the broader historical fact that Vorilhon made the claim is well documented. The unresolved question is whether the reported experience represented an external event, a misidentified phenomenon, a personal experience interpreted through the UFO culture of the period, or another explanation. The available evidence does not allow the extraterrestrial interpretation to be independently demonstrated.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eap-iea.org/index.php/eap/article/view/814" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eap-iea.org">[eap-iea.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eap-iea.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Religions – Beginnings and Main Forms &#124; Etnoantropološki problemi / Issues in Ethnology and AnthropologyApril 12, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 12, 2010</span></span></span>

## Skeptical interpretations and unresolved questions

Skeptical analyses of the Raëlian origin story generally focus on the lack of corroborating evidence and on the way the narrative developed into a religious system. Critics argue that the account reflects themes common in twentieth-century contactee culture, where advanced extraterrestrials often replace earlier supernatural figures while retaining ideas of revelation, chosen messengers, and future transformation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eap-iea.org/index.php/eap/article/view/814" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eap-iea.org">[eap-iea.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eap-iea.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Religions – Beginnings and Main Forms &#124; Etnoantropološki problemi / Issues in Ethnology and AnthropologyApril 12, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 12, 2010</span></span></span>

Some critics have also examined differences between versions of Vorilhon’s story over time, arguing that details presented in later publications should be treated cautiously because they come from the claimant and movement itself rather than independent investigators.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://raelian-truth.blogspot.com/2019/05/rael-facts-his-book-vs-interview.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: raelian-truth.blogspot.com">[raelian-truth.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">raelian-truth.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">rael facts his book vs interview</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Raelian &quot;TRUTH&quot;: RAEL FACTS: HIS BOOK vs. INTERVIEWMay 3, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 3, 2019</span></span></span>

Supporters of Raëlism, by contrast, regard the encounter as a genuine meeting and see Vorilhon’s message as a scientific explanation of humanity’s origins rather than a traditional religious revelation. The movement continues to frame the 1973 event as the founding moment of a mission to prepare humanity for future contact with the Elohim.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rael.org/rael/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rael.org">[RAEL.ORG]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rael.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Last Prophet, Rael</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Last Prophet, Rael</span></span></span>

## Why the 1973 contact remains significant


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

The lasting importance of the Claude Vorilhon contact lies in its influence on modern UFO culture and new religious movements. The case illustrates a transition from earlier “contactee” stories, in which individuals claimed communication with benevolent beings from space, into organised movements with doctrines, symbols, leadership structures, and long-term social goals.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eap-iea.org/index.php/eap/article/view/814" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eap-iea.org">[eap-iea.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eap-iea.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Religions – Beginnings and Main Forms &#124; Etnoantropološki problemi / Issues in Ethnology and AnthropologyApril 12, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 12, 2010</span></span></span>
