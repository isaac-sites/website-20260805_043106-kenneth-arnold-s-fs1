## The 1954 Coniston sighting and the photographs

### A schoolboy encounter in the Lake District

The incident took place near Little Arrow Farm, close to Coniston and the Old Man of Coniston mountain. Stephen Darbishire, then a pupil at Ulverston Grammar School, was walking with his eight-year-old cousin Adrian Meyer and carrying a Kodak box camera. According to the original account, the boys intended to photograph birds but instead noticed a strange object in the sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/project-red-book-project-red-book-vol-5-no-6/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

Darbishire described the object as a silvery, reflective craft with features resembling the classic “flying saucer” designs popular in 1950s UFO culture. He said it appeared to have portholes, a hatch-like feature, and projections beneath the body. He claimed to have taken two photographs before the object disappeared rapidly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/project-red-book-project-red-book-vol-5-no-6/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

The photographs showed a blurred disc-like object that resembled the “scout ship” images promoted by American UFO figure George Adamski. This resemblance helped make the case famous, because Adamski’s claimed spacecraft photographs had become internationally known after the publication of *Flying Saucers Have Landed* in 1953.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/secret-files/alex-birch-ufo-photos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[drdavidclarke.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Alex Birch UFO photos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Alex Birch UFO photos</span></span></span>

## How the photograph became a national UFO story

The Darbishire photograph arrived during a period when flying saucer reports were attracting major public interest. Newspapers covered the case extensively, and the photographs were presented by some UFO researchers as the first major British flying saucer image.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://isaackoi.com/ufo-history/ufo/19540215-stephen-darbishire-photo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: isaackoi.com">[Isaac Koi Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">isaackoi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">19540215 stephen darbishire photo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Isaac Koi Archive1954.0215 Stephen Darbishire photo &#124; Isaac Koi Archive...</span></span></span>

The publicity went beyond newspapers. Stephen and his father were invited to Buckingham Palace, where they met a representative connected with the Duke of Edinburgh. RAF Squadron Leader Sir Peter Horsley, who had an interest in UFO reports, interviewed the family and later discussed the case in relation to wider flying saucer investigations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://isaackoi.com/ufo-history/ufo/19540215-stephen-darbishire-photo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: isaackoi.com">[Isaac Koi Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">isaackoi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">19540215 stephen darbishire photo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Isaac Koi Archive1954.0215 Stephen Darbishire photo &#124; Isaac Koi Archive...</span></span></span>

### Reasons some researchers considered it significant

Supporters of the case have pointed to several features:

### The problems with the photographic evidence

The main weakness is that the original photographic material has not been available for a modern, independent forensic examination comparable with contemporary image analysis standards. Much of the debate relies on historical accounts, reproductions, and statements made decades after the event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://magoniamagazine.blogspot.com/2013/12/darbishire.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: magoniamagazine.blogspot.com">[magoniamagazine.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">magoniamagazine.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">MAGONI A ARCHIVE: UFO Hoaxing and the Story of Stephen Darbishire</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MAGONI A ARCHIVE: UFO Hoaxing and the Story of Stephen Darbishire</span></span></span>

Questions were also raised about the similarities between Darbishire’s description and the imagery already associated with Adamski-style flying saucers. Critics argued that the object’s appearance may have reflected popular UFO imagery of the period rather than an independently observed craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/project-red-book-project-red-book-vol-5-no-6/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

Another issue concerns Darbishire’s own later comments. Over the years he gave differing accounts of whether the photographs were genuine, fabricated, or something more complicated. This inconsistency became central to sceptical interpretations of the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/secret-files/alex-birch-ufo-photos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[drdavidclarke.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Alex Birch UFO photos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Alex Birch UFO photos</span></span></span>

## The hoax explanation and Darbishire’s later statements

The most common sceptical explanation is that the photograph was a schoolboy prank. Later accounts reported that Darbishire eventually described the photographs as a hoax or prank, although the exact meaning of his changing statements has remained debated among UFO researchers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/2019/03/Issue-01-26.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on skepticalinquirer.org.</span></span></span>

A possible explanation proposed by critics is that the image could have been created using a small model or constructed object photographed against the landscape. Such methods were relatively simple and were already familiar in photography and amateur trick imagery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://magoniamagazine.blogspot.com/2013/12/darbishire.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: magoniamagazine.blogspot.com">[magoniamagazine.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">magoniamagazine.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">MAGONI A ARCHIVE: UFO Hoaxing and the Story of Stephen Darbishire</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MAGONI A ARCHIVE: UFO Hoaxing and the Story of Stephen Darbishire</span></span></span>
