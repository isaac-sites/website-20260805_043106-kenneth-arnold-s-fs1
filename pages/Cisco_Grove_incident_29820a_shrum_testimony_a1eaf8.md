## Introduction

Donald Shrum’s account is the central human testimony behind the [Cisco Grove incident]({{ 'cisco-grove-incident-1964/' | relative_url }}) of 1964. On the night of 4 September 1964, the 28-year-old bow hunter said he became separated from his companions in California’s Tahoe National Forest and later experienced a prolonged encounter involving a strange illuminated object, three unusual figures, and a reported struggle for survival while he was trapped in a tree. The importance of Shrum’s testimony lies less in proving what the figures were and more in the unusual combination of a detailed first-person narrative, limited corroboration of the initial light sighting, and the absence of physical evidence that could independently confirm the most extraordinary parts of the story.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/cisco-grove-1964/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Cisco Grove · California · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Cisco Grove · California · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

His testimony has remained a point of debate: supporters emphasise his consistency and apparent reluctance to seek publicity, while sceptics point to the lack of independent confirmation for the encounter itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/aliens/encounters/cisco-grove-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">cisco grove encounter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe 1964 Cisco Grove Encounter - UFO InsightDecember 31, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2018</span></span></span>

## Shrum’s night in the forest

Shrum was hunting with friends near Cisco Grove when the group became separated after darkness fell. According to later investigative summaries of his statement, he chose to spend the night in a tree rather than travel through unfamiliar terrain. From his position, he noticed a moving white light among the trees and initially interpreted it as a possible helicopter or rescue effort. After attempting to attract attention, he reported that the light approached and that he realised it did not resemble any aircraft he recognised.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/cisco-grove-1964/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Cisco Grove · California · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Cisco Grove · California · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

The setting shaped the entire experience. Shrum was alone, outdoors at night, physically exposed, and attempting to interpret unfamiliar sights and sounds under stressful conditions. These circumstances are important in assessing the testimony because they create both sides of the evidential debate: they could contribute to confusion or misperception, but they could also explain why a genuinely unusual event would be remembered with such intensity. The case therefore depends heavily on how much confidence can be placed in a single witness describing events in an extreme environment.

Shrum’s reported encounter developed into a long confrontation rather than a brief sighting. He said that after the object arrived, figures approached his position and repeatedly attempted to reach him while he remained in the tree. He described using fire, thrown objects, and his bow and arrows as defensive measures. The account recorded by UFO investigators included claims that arrows struck one of the figures with a flash and that a vapour emitted by the entity caused him to lose consciousness temporarily.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/PDFs/HUMCAT/HUMCAT_Index_1964.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center for UFO Studies\</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">64-1 1964* Casitas Dam, California Type A Hearin...</span></span></span>

## Descriptions of the figures and object

The most unusual elements of Shrum’s testimony concern his descriptions of the alleged visitors. He reported three figures: two humanoid forms wearing light-coloured or metallic-looking clothing and a third figure that appeared mechanical or robotic. The latter was described as having reddish-orange glowing eyes and other features that led Shrum to interpret it as an artificial being rather than a living creature.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/cisco-grove-1964/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Cisco Grove · California · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Cisco Grove · California · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

According to the account preserved by investigators, the figures did not communicate verbally with him. Instead, the interaction was described through movement and physical actions: approaching the tree, attempting to dislodge him, and repeatedly returning after being driven away by fire. This made the testimony unusual even within UFO reports of the period because Shrum’s claim centred on a sustained physical confrontation rather than observation alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/PDFs/HUMCAT/HUMCAT_Index_1964.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center for UFO Studies\</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">64-1 1964* Casitas Dam, California Type A Hearin...</span></span></span>

The object itself was also described in extraordinary terms, although details vary between later retellings. The most consistent element is Shrum’s report of a bright moving light associated with a larger unknown craft or presence. Civilian investigators recorded the case as involving a reported landing and entities, while later summaries generally treat the craft description as part of Shrum’s personal account rather than independently verified evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://isaackoi.com/ufo-history/ufo/19640904-cisco-grove-incident/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: isaackoi.com">[Isaac Koi Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">isaackoi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">19640904 cisco grove incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Isaac Koi Archive1964.0904 Cisco Grove incident &#124; Isaac Koi Archive...</span></span></span>

## The witness evidence: what supports Shrum’s account?

The strongest supporting element in the Shrum story is that another member of the hunting party, Vincent Alvarez, reportedly saw an unusual light during the same night. This does not confirm the claimed beings or the confrontation at the tree, but it provides some support that an unexplained light was present in the area.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/cisco-grove-1964/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Cisco Grove · California · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Cisco Grove · California · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

Investigators from civilian UFO organisations, including NICAP and APRO-associated researchers, considered Shrum an important witness because of the level of detail in his report and his willingness to repeat the account. The case was also notable because Shrum was not initially a public figure seeking attention; later writers have emphasised that he was reluctant to discuss the event publicly for many years.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/aliens/encounters/cisco-grove-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">cisco grove encounter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe 1964 Cisco Grove Encounter - UFO InsightDecember 31, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2018</span></span></span>

However, the supporting evidence has clear limits. No publicly confirmed physical trace has established the presence of unknown beings, and the reported damage to arrows, clothing, or other objects has not produced independently verified evidence of an extraordinary cause. The central event — the encounter with the figures — remains based almost entirely on Shrum’s testimony.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/aliens/encounters/cisco-grove-encounter" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">cisco grove encounter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe 1964 Cisco Grove Encounter - UFO InsightDecember 31, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2018</span></span></span>

## Questions around memory and testimony

Shrum’s account presents a classic challenge in evaluating unusual witness reports: sincerity and accuracy are not the same thing. A witness may genuinely report an experience while still being mistaken about its cause. In the Cisco Grove case, factors such as darkness, isolation, fear, fatigue, and the difficulty of judging unfamiliar objects in a forest environment are relevant considerations for sceptical interpretations.

At the same time, the account has features that make it difficult to dismiss simply as a casual misidentification. Shrum described a prolonged sequence of events with specific details, including his defensive actions, the behaviour of the figures, and his physical situation in the tree.
