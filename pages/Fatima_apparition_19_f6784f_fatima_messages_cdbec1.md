## Introduction

The messages associated with the Fátima apparitions of 1917 became the central reason the event developed from a local religious claim into a worldwide subject of devotion, historical study, and controversy. The most discussed element is the so-called “Secret of Fátima”, a three-part message that the children said they received on 13 July 1917. According to the later writings of Lúcia dos Santos, the message concerned a vision of hell, warnings about war and persecution, a call for devotion to the Immaculate Heart of Mary, and a final vision that was not published until 2000.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://fatima.org/the-secret-in-three-parts-the-first-part/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.org">[Fatima Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center The Secret in Three Parts: The First Part &#124; The Fatima Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fatima CenterThe Secret in Three Parts: The First Part &#124; The Fatima CenterFebruary 13, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 13, 2017</span></span></span>

The importance of the messages lies less in a single prediction than in how they were interpreted across the twentieth century. Supporters saw them as a spiritual warning connected with world conflict, communism, and the suffering of the Church. Critics and historians have focused on the way the messages were recorded decades later, the influence of changing historical circumstances on interpretation, and disputes over whether the complete third secret was released.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/it/bollettino/pubblico/2000/06/26/0407/01462.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: press.vatican.va">[Vatican Press]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">press.vatican.va</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Press CONFERENZA STAMPA DI PRESENTAZIONE DEL DOCUMENTO &quot;IL MESSAGGIO DI FATIMA</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Vatican PressCONFERENZA STAMPA DI PRESENTAZIONE DEL DOCUMENTO &quot;IL MESSAGGIO DI FATIMA&quot;June 26, 2000...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 26, 2000</span></span></span>

## The July 1917 message

The reported message of 13 July 1917 is traditionally known as the “Secret of Fátima”. According to Lúcia’s later memoirs, the secret was not a separate prediction given over several visits but a single revelation divided into three parts. The first two parts were written down and published in the early 1940s, while the final part remained sealed for decades.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.papa2017.fatima.pt/en/pages/the-secret-of-fatima" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: papa2017.fatima.pt">[Papa Francisco - Fátima 2017]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">papa2017.fatima.pt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Papa Francisco</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fátima 2017THE SECRET OF FATIMA...</span></span></span>

### The first two parts: judgement, war, and the Immaculate Heart

The first part of the secret described a vision of hell shown to the children. In Catholic interpretation, this was understood as a warning about the consequences of rejecting God rather than as a prediction of a specific historical event. The second part introduced the themes that would dominate later Fátima discussions: the conversion of sinners, devotion to the Immaculate Heart of Mary, and warnings of further conflict if humanity did not change course.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://fatima.org/the-secret-in-three-parts-the-first-part/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.org">[Fatima Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center The Secret in Three Parts: The First Part &#124; The Fatima Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fatima CenterThe Secret in Three Parts: The First Part &#124; The Fatima CenterFebruary 13, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 13, 2017</span></span></span>

The war element became especially significant because the message was recorded during the First World War. Lúcia’s account later connected the warning of another war with a future “sign” and with the idea that failure to respond to the message would bring further suffering. After the Second World War and the rise of Soviet communism, many Catholics interpreted these themes through the political struggles of the twentieth century.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://fatima.org/the-secret-in-three-parts-the-second-part/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.org">[Fatima Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center The Secret in Three Parts: The Second Part &#124; The Fatima Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fatima CenterThe Secret in Three Parts: The Second Part &#124; The Fatima Center...</span></span></span>

The reference to Russia became one of the most debated parts of the message. Supporters of the Fátima interpretation linked it to the 1917 Russian Revolution and later Cold War tensions, while historians have noted that later readings were shaped by events that unfolded after the original reports. The historical difficulty is that the meaning of the message depends heavily on later interpretation rather than on a universally agreed prediction with a single measurable outcome.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://fatima.org/the-secret-in-three-parts-the-second-part/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.org">[Fatima Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center The Secret in Three Parts: The Second Part &#124; The Fatima Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fatima CenterThe Secret in Three Parts: The Second Part &#124; The Fatima Center...</span></span></span>

## Later publication and interpretation

The history of the three secrets is largely a history of gradual disclosure. Lúcia revealed the first two parts in her memoirs in 1941, following requests from church authorities. The third part was written down separately and remained unpublished for more than half a century.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://fatima.org/silencing-of-sister-lucia/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.org">[Fatima Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center Silencing of Sister Lucia &#124; The Fatima Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fatima CenterSilencing of Sister Lucia &#124; The Fatima CenterMarch 8, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 8, 2017</span></span></span>

### Why the third secret became controversial

The third secret became one of the most discussed religious documents of the twentieth century because of the long period in which its contents were unknown. The expectation surrounding the unreleased text led to many theories about whether it contained a prediction of catastrophic events, a crisis within the Church, or some other hidden warning.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.papa2017.fatima.pt/en/pages/the-secret-of-fatima" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: papa2017.fatima.pt">[Papa Francisco - Fátima 2017]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">papa2017.fatima.pt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Papa Francisco</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fátima 2017THE SECRET OF FATIMA...</span></span></span>

The Vatican released the text of the third part in 2000 as part of the document *The Message of Fátima*. The published vision described an “Bishop dressed in White”, understood by the Vatican as representing the Pope, travelling through a devastated city and facing martyrdom together with other religious figures and lay people.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/travels/2000/documents/hf_jp-ii_spe_20000513_sodano-fatima.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vatican.va">[Vatican]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vatican.va</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Address of Cardinal Angelo Sodano regarding the third part of the secret of Fátima at the conclusion of the solemn Mass of John Pa...</span></span></span>

The Vatican’s interpretation connected the vision with twentieth-century persecution of Christians and, in particular, with the attempted assassination of Pope John Paul II on 13 May 1981, the anniversary of the first reported Fátima apparition. Cardinal Joseph Ratzinger, who later became Pope Benedict XVI, argued that the text should be read symbolically rather than as a simple prediction of future events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/it/bollettino/pubblico/2000/06/26/0407/01462.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: press.vatican.va">[Vatican Press]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">press.vatican.va</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Press CONFERENZA STAMPA DI PRESENTAZIONE DEL DOCUMENTO &quot;IL MESSAGGIO DI FATIMA</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Vatican PressCONFERENZA STAMPA DI PRESENTAZIONE DEL DOCUMENTO &quot;IL MESSAGGIO DI FATIMA&quot;June 26, 2000...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 26, 2000</span></span></span>

## Religious meaning and historical debate

For many Catholics, the Fátima messages are important because they function as a call to prayer, repentance, and concern for human suffering rather than as a timetable of future events. The Vatican has emphasised that private revelations such as Fátima are distinct from the central teachings of Christianity and are interpreted within the wider tradition of the Church.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/it/bollettino/pubblico/2000/06/26/0407/01462.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: press.vatican.va">[Vatican Press]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">press.vatican.va</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Press CONFERENZA STAMPA DI PRESENTAZIONE DEL DOCUMENTO &quot;IL MESSAGGIO DI FATIMA</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Vatican PressCONFERENZA STAMPA DI PRESENTAZIONE DEL DOCUMENTO &quot;IL MESSAGGIO DI FATIMA&quot;June 26, 2000...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 26, 2000</span></span></span>

### The official Catholic interpretation

The official interpretation presents the secrets as a symbolic warning. The first part highlights the seriousness of moral choices; the second connects conversion with peace; and the third presents suffering and persecution through imagery associated with martyrdom. In this reading, the message’s purpose is spiritual renewal rather than forecasting a specific political sequence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/travels/2000/documents/hf_jp-ii_spe_20000513_sodano-fatima.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vatican.va">[Vatican]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vatican.va</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Address of Cardinal Angelo Sodano regarding the third part of the secret of Fátima at the conclusion of the solemn Mass of John Pa...</span></span></span>

The connection with Pope John Paul II became particularly influential because the assassination attempt occurred on a Fátima anniversary and the Pope himself attributed his survival to the protection associated with Our Lady of Fátima. ### Continuing disputes over the third secret

Some Fátima commentators have argued that the 2000 publication did not represent the entire third secret, suggesting that additional text or interpretations remain undisclosed. These arguments focus on questions about earlier references by Lúcia and others, as well as expectations created during the decades before publication.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://fatima.org/reply-to-the-hoax/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.org">[Fatima Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center Reply to the Hoax &#124; The Fatima Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fatima CenterReply to the Hoax &#124; The Fatima Center...</span></span></span>

## Why the messages remain central to Fátima

The three secrets shaped Fátima’s historical importance because they provided a framework through which later generations interpreted major events of the twentieth century. The themes of war, suffering, conversion, and hope allowed the messages to be connected with events ranging from the First World War to the Cold War and the experiences of persecuted religious communities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://fatima.org/the-secret-in-three-parts-the-second-part/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.org">[Fatima Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center The Secret in Three Parts: The Second Part &#124; The Fatima Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fatima CenterThe Secret in Three Parts: The Second Part &#124; The Fatima Center...</span></span></span>

At the same time, the history of the secrets shows the difference between a religious interpretation and a historical demonstration. For believers, the messages form part of a continuing spiritual tradition. For historians and sceptics, their significance lies in how communities recorded, preserved, and interpreted extraordinary claims over time. The lasting debate around Fátima is therefore not only about what the children reported in 1917, but also about how later societies assign meaning to ambiguous prophetic texts.

    return encodeURIComponent(String(value || '').replace(/\s+/g, ' ').trim()).replace(/%20/g, '+');
  }
  function buildUrl(query, reference, marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
