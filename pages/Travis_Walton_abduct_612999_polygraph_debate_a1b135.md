## Introduction

Polygraph testing became one of the most debated pieces of evidence in the Travis Walton abduction case because supporters and critics drew very different conclusions from the same record. Walton and several members of the logging crew underwent examinations after the 1975 incident, and favourable interpretations were often presented as evidence that the witnesses were sincere. However, Walton’s own testing history included disputed results, including an early examination that an examiner interpreted as deceptive. The disagreement matters because polygraphs do not determine whether an event happened; at most, they attempt to assess whether a person is being deceptive about answers to specific questions under specific testing conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

## Tests taken by Walton and the crew

### The early law-enforcement examinations

After Walton disappeared on 5 November 1975 and his co-workers reported the incident, investigators treated the situation initially as a missing-person case. The first concerns were not whether an extraterrestrial event had occurred, but whether the crew had harmed Walton or was concealing information about his disappearance. Polygraph examinations connected with this phase focused largely on whether the men had injured or killed Walton rather than proving the details of the alleged encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travis_Walton_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton incident</span></span></span>

This distinction became central to later arguments. Supporters of Walton’s account pointed to the crew’s favourable results as evidence that the men were not fabricating a disappearance or covering up violence. Critics responded that passing questions about whether someone committed a crime does not establish that the unusual explanation offered by the witnesses is true. A person could, in principle, answer truthfully about not harming Walton while still being mistaken, exaggerating, or misleading about other parts of the story.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

### Walton’s own polygraph examinations

Walton himself took multiple polygraph tests under different circumstances. The most controversial was an examination conducted on 15 November 1975 by examiner John J. McCarthy, arranged through UFO investigators and funded through media interest in the case. McCarthy concluded that Walton showed signs of deception during the test, a result that became a major point for critics of the abduction claim.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

Later examinations were interpreted by supporters as favourable to Walton. These conflicting outcomes allowed both sides to claim that the testing supported their position: advocates highlighted the tests that appeared to confirm his honesty, while sceptics emphasised the failed or unfavourable examination and questioned the circumstances of the positive results.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

## Why the results produced conflicting interpretations

The disagreement over Walton’s polygraphs was driven partly by different assumptions about what a successful test would prove.

Supporters argued that a group of people maintaining a false story would be unlikely to produce a pattern of favourable examinations over time. They viewed the crew’s results as an important credibility factor, particularly because multiple witnesses maintained their accounts for decades.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

Critics took a narrower view. They argued that even a genuine polygraph “pass” could only indicate that a person appeared truthful when answering particular questions. It could not verify the existence of an unknown craft, confirm Walton’s claimed experiences inside the object, or eliminate possibilities such as mistaken belief, memory distortion, or deliberate deception.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

The question wording also became a major issue. Some examinations focused on whether the crew had harmed Walton, while others focused more directly on whether Walton had experienced an abduction. Because different tests addressed different claims, comparing them as though they were measuring the same thing created further confusion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travis_Walton_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton incident</span></span></span>

### Supporters’ reading of the evidence

Those defending Walton’s account often present the polygraphs as part of a larger credibility argument rather than as standalone proof. They point to several factors:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The logging crew’s willingness to undergo testing after the disappearance.
* The absence of a confession from the original witnesses.
* The long-term consistency of their central account.
* The difficulty, in their view, of maintaining a coordinated deception involving several people.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

</div>

From this perspective, the polygraphs are valuable because they appear to show that the witnesses were not simply inventing a story for immediate gain. Supporters argue that the combination of witness testimony and favourable examinations makes the case unusual compared with many single-witness UFO reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

### Critics’ reading of the evidence

Sceptical investigators have generally treated the polygraph record as weak or ambiguous evidence. They have highlighted Walton’s unfavourable early test, questioned the conditions of some later examinations, and argued that positive results were given greater publicity than negative ones.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

Critics have also pointed out that a polygraph result cannot distinguish between several possibilities. A person may believe a story is true and still produce a misleading result, while a person attempting deception may sometimes avoid detection. For this reason, they argue that the tests cannot resolve the central question of whether Walton’s claimed abduction actually occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travis_Walton_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton incident</span></span></span>

## The limits of deception testing

Polygraph examinations remain controversial because they measure physiological responses associated with stress and questioning rather than directly detecting lies. Their interpretation depends heavily on the examiner, the structure of the questions, the testing environment, and the assumptions made when evaluating responses. In the Walton case, these limitations became especially important because the tests were used to address different claims at different times.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

The strongest conclusion that can reasonably be drawn from the polygraph record is therefore limited: some examiners interpreted certain tests as supporting Walton and the crew’s credibility, while another examiner interpreted an early Walton examination negatively. The results do not provide a definitive answer about whether the reported abduction occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

The enduring dispute over Walton’s polygraphs reflects a broader problem in extraordinary claims: credibility evidence can influence how people judge a testimony, but it cannot substitute for independent physical evidence. In the Walton case, the tests remain an important part of the historical debate, but they have never settled the disagreement between supporters who see them as corroboration and critics who see them as inconclusive.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/cases/travis-walton-case-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Travis Walton case &#124; UAP Case Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
