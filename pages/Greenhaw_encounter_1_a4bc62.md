## The 1973 Roadside Encounter in Falkville

### Greenhaw’s account of the night

According to Greenhaw’s account, he received a late-evening telephone call from a distressed woman who claimed that she had seen a UFO land in a field near Falkville. Greenhaw, who was the town’s police chief, travelled to the area to investigate. He initially found no obvious evidence of a craft or landing site, but while driving along a nearby road he noticed a figure illuminated by his vehicle’s lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://thehorrorcollection.com/alabama-metal-man-1973-cryptid-or-hoax/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thehorrorcollection.com">[The Horror Collection]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thehorrorcollection.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Horror Collection Alabama Metal Man: Alien, Hoax, or Something Far Stranger?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Horror CollectionAlabama Metal Man: Alien, Hoax, or Something Far Stranger?June 16, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 16, 2026</span></span></span>

Greenhaw described the figure as human-shaped but unlike any person he had seen. He said it appeared to be covered in a bright, reflective material resembling polished metal or foil, with unusual proportions and a stiff, mechanical manner of movement. Some retellings of his statement describe an antenna-like feature on the head and a lack of visible facial features.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.scribd.com/document/731742835/Blum-Beyond-Earth-Man-s-Contact-with-UFOs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Blum Beyond Earth Man s Contact with UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Sightings: A 1973 Overview &#124; PDF &#124; Unidentified Flying Object...</span></span></span>

## The Photographs: The Case’s Main Evidence

The most important physical evidence connected to the encounter is the series of Polaroid photographs taken by Greenhaw. The images show a bright, reflective figure against a dark background, but their quality is limited by the technology available at the time and the nighttime conditions. They do not provide clear detail of the figure’s construction, identity, or surroundings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://isaackoi.com/ufo-history/ufo/19731017-falkville-alabama-photos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: isaackoi.com">[Isaac Koi Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">isaackoi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">19731017 falkville alabama photos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Isaac Koi Archive1973.1017 Falkville, Alabama photos &#124; Isaac Koi Archive...</span></span></span>

UFO investigators examined the photographs, including analysis associated with the Mutual UFO Network (MUFON). Some interpretations focused on unusual features in the images, while other analysts argued that photographic artefacts or ordinary objects could account for apparent anomalies. Later discussions of the case have noted that claimed “saucer” shapes in some photographic material were not part of Greenhaw’s original report of what he photographed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.phantomsandmonsters.com/2013/12/the-falkville-metal-man.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: phantomsandmonsters.com">[Phantoms &amp; Monsters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">phantomsandmonsters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Phantoms &amp; Monsters The Falkville Metal Man</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Phantoms &amp; MonstersThe Falkville Metal ManDecember 12, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 12, 2013</span></span></span>

## The Hoax Explanation and the Protective-Suit Theory

The main sceptical explanation is that the “Metal Man” was a human wearing a reflective suit or costume. Investigators have suggested that a fire-resistant suit, including older types of protective equipment with metallic surfaces, could resemble the figure seen in the photographs. This explanation attempts to account for the reflective appearance, the unusual shape, and the limited photographic detail.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://appalachianhistorian.org/the-falkville-metal-man-jeff-greenhaw-and-alabamas-silver-suited-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: appalachianhistorian.org">[Appalachianhistorian.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">appalachianhistorian.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Falkville Metal Man: Jeff Greenhaw and Alabama’s Silver-Suited Mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">24, 2026...</span></span></span>

The timing of the case also attracted attention. The encounter occurred during a period in 1973 when UFO reports involving humanoid figures were receiving considerable publicity, including the widely reported Pascagoula, Mississippi incident earlier that year. Some investigators argued that the Falkville story may have been influenced by the wider cultural atmosphere surrounding UFO reports at the time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.phantomsandmonsters.com/2013/12/the-falkville-metal-man.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: phantomsandmonsters.com">[Phantoms &amp; Monsters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">phantomsandmonsters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Phantoms &amp; Monsters The Falkville Metal Man</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Phantoms &amp; MonstersThe Falkville Metal ManDecember 12, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 12, 2013</span></span></span>

## Witness Credibility and the Limits of the Evidence

Greenhaw’s role as a police chief has often been presented by supporters as a reason to take the report seriously. He was not an anonymous witness, and his account involved a situation in which he was acting in an official capacity by responding to a reported disturbance. Contemporary coverage recorded his description of the encounter and his claim that he had photographed the figure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.scribd.com/document/731742835/Blum-Beyond-Earth-Man-s-Contact-with-UFOs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Blum Beyond Earth Man s Contact with UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Sightings: A 1973 Overview &#124; PDF &#124; Unidentified Flying Object...</span></span></span>

At the same time, professional status does not by itself prove the accuracy of a perception. A witness can sincerely report an unusual experience while still misidentifying what they observed, especially in poor lighting, under surprising circumstances, and without opportunities for close examination. The key evidential weakness in the Greenhaw case is that the photographs do not clearly identify the material, scale, or nature of the figure.

The encounter also became entangled with later stories about Greenhaw’s personal difficulties, including claims about problems following the publicity. These details contributed to the case’s mythology, but they do not directly resolve the central evidential question of what appeared on the road that night.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://thehiddentruth.co/1973-police-chief-photographs-spaceman-from-ufo-va-109/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thehiddentruth.co">[thehiddentruth.co]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thehiddentruth.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1973 police chief photographs spaceman from ufo va 109</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">1973: Police Chief Photographs Spaceman From &#124; The Hidden TruthJuly 4, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 4, 2022</span></span></span>

## Why the Greenhaw Encounter Still Attracts Attention

The lasting appeal of the Falkville Metal Man case comes from the unusual combination of ordinary and extraordinary elements. A small-town police chief responding to a call, a rural Alabama road, and a handful of instant photographs create a scenario that feels more concrete than many UFO reports. Yet the same features that make the story memorable also expose its limitations: the event happened quickly, visibility was poor, and the available evidence is ambiguous.

For UFO researchers, the case remains an example of a report where witness testimony and physical documentation exist but do not produce a definitive conclusion. For sceptics, it illustrates how a striking image can encourage extraordinary interpretations when ordinary explanations have not been fully excluded.
