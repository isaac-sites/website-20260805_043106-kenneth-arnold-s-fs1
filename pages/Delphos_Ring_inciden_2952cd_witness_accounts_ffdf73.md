## Introduction

The Delphos Ring incident of 1971 is remembered largely because of the combination of a close-range sighting and a reported physical trace, but the event began with one person’s account: 16-year-old Ronald “Ronnie” Johnson, who said he encountered a brightly illuminated object while tending sheep on his family’s farm near Delphos, Kansas, on 2 November 1971. His report described a low-hovering, multicoloured object, unusual sounds, a brief visual effect after the object departed, and a later discovery of a strange ring-shaped mark on the ground.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/types-of-ufos/close-encounters-of-the-second-kind/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center for UFO Studies Close Encounters of the Second Kind</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesClose Encounters of the Second Kind - Center for UFO Studies...</span></span></span>

The importance of Johnson’s testimony lies not only in the detail of the account but in how it connected with other observations. His parents, Durel and Erma Johnson, reported seeing a bright object moving away after Ronnie alerted them, while another local resident, Lester Ensbarger, reported seeing a bright light in the Delphos area at around the same time. These accounts did not provide independent confirmation of every detail of Johnson’s close encounter, but they helped establish that multiple people reported unusual lights during the same period.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/apro-bulletin-apro-bulletin-1971-11-00-november-december/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

## Ronald Johnson’s close encounter

### A farm sighting at close range

Ronald Johnson’s account began while he was outside with the family’s sheep at approximately 7:00 p.m. According to later case summaries and investigation reports, he heard a rumbling sound and saw a luminous object in a small grove of trees about 75 feet away. He described the object as roughly nine feet wide and about ten feet high, with a domed upper section and intense blue, red, and orange illumination. Investigators who recorded his statement classified the report as a close encounter because Johnson claimed the object was near the ground and close enough for him to observe its structure and effects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/types-of-ufos/close-encounters-of-the-second-kind/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center for UFO Studies Close Encounters of the Second Kind</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesClose Encounters of the Second Kind - Center for UFO Studies...</span></span></span>

Johnson’s description included several sensory details that became central to later evaluations of the case. He said the object hovered only a few feet above the ground, with a bright glow beneath it. After several minutes, he reported that the base became brighter and the object moved away at an angle, producing a change in sound from a lower rumble to a higher-pitched noise. He also reported a temporary loss of vision following the departure, after which he recovered and went to get his parents.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/PDFs/JUFOS/1991_NS_vol3_JUFOS.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesISSN: 0730-5748...</span></span></span>

The account was unusual for its level of detail, but it remained a single-person observation for the most dramatic part of the event. No other witness claimed to have seen the object at close range hovering near the trees. This distinction is important when assessing the strength of the testimony: the case contains corroboration of unusual lights, but not independent confirmation of Johnson’s full description of the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/types-of-ufos/close-encounters-of-the-second-kind/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center for UFO Studies Close Encounters of the Second Kind</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesClose Encounters of the Second Kind - Center for UFO Studies...</span></span></span>

### The transition from sighting to physical trace

After Ronald called his parents outside, the family reportedly observed a bright object moving away in the sky. They then investigated the area where Johnson said the object had been and found a circular mark on the ground. Early accounts described the area as a bright grey-white ring with an unusual surface texture. The family also reported a numb sensation after touching material within the ring.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/apro-bulletin-apro-bulletin-1971-11-00-november-december/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

This sequence shaped later interpretations of the case. Supporters of the incident’s significance argue that Johnson’s account was strengthened because the physical trace was found immediately after the sighting. More cautious investigators separate the two questions: whether Johnson and his parents genuinely saw unusual lights, and whether the ground feature was actually caused by the object described. The witness accounts provide the foundation of the case, but the interpretation of the ring remains the more disputed issue.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/PDFs/JUFOS/1991_NS_vol3_JUFOS.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesISSN: 0730-5748...</span></span></span>

## Family and local witness reports

### Durel and Erma Johnson’s observation

Ronald’s parents, Durel and Erma Johnson, were not witnesses to the close encounter itself. Their testimony began after Ronnie ran to the house and asked them to look outside. They reported seeing a bright light moving away in the southern sky. Contemporary UFO investigators treated this as supporting evidence because it placed the family’s observation within the same timeframe as Ronald’s account.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/types-of-ufos/close-encounters-of-the-second-kind/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center for UFO Studies Close Encounters of the Second Kind</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesClose Encounters of the Second Kind - Center for UFO Studies...</span></span></span>

The parents’ role is significant because they were not initially describing a prepared story or a planned investigation. Their observation followed their son’s immediate report, and investigators later interviewed them as part of the wider case file. However, their testimony had limits: they saw a distant light rather than the object Ronald described at close range, meaning it could not independently verify the shape, size, or behaviour of the object on the ground.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/types-of-ufos/close-encounters-of-the-second-kind/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center for UFO Studies Close Encounters of the Second Kind</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesClose Encounters of the Second Kind - Center for UFO Studies...</span></span></span>

### Lester Ensbarger and the wider local reports

A separate local witness, Lester Ensbarger of Minneapolis, Kansas, was reported to have told authorities that he saw a bright light descending in the Delphos area at approximately 7:30 p.m. on the night of the incident. This account became part of the case record as a possible additional observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://projectaquarius.mufon.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/December_November_1985.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: projectaquarius.mufon.com">[Project Aquarius]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">projectaquarius.mufon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Aquarius MUFOH UFO JOURNAL</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project Aquarius MUFOH UFO JOURNAL</span></span></span>

Ensbarger’s report is often described as corroboration, but it should be interpreted carefully. It supported the claim that unusual lights were seen in the area that evening, yet it did not establish that he saw the same object described by Ronald Johnson. The timing and location made the report relevant, but the lack of a detailed description prevented it from serving as a complete independent confirmation of the close encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://projectaquarius.mufon.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/December_November_1985.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: projectaquarius.mufon.com">[Project Aquarius]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">projectaquarius.mufon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Project Aquarius MUFOH UFO JOURNAL</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project Aquarius MUFOH UFO JOURNAL</span></span></span>

## Assessing testimony and corroboration

### What makes the witness accounts notable

The witness evidence in the Delphos case stands out because it combines different levels of observation:

* **Ronald Johnson:** a close-range witness who provided the detailed description of the object, sounds, movement, and immediate effects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/types-of-ufos/close-encounters-of-the-second-kind/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center for UFO Studies Close Encounters of the Second Kind</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesClose Encounters of the Second Kind - Center for UFO Studies...</span></span></span>

Early investigators generally described the Johnson family as credible local residents. Reports associated with the investigation included comments from Ottawa County Sheriff Ralph Enlow indicating that he considered the family reliable and did not regard the incident as an obvious hoax.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.scribd.com/doc/307201180/FSR-CH-1972-N-9" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FSR-CH 1972 N 9 &#124; PDFFebruary 1, 1972...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 1, 1972</span></span></span>

### The unresolved role of testimony

The witness accounts remain the human core of the Delphos Ring incident. They explain why the case attracted attention before laboratory testing and why later researchers continued to revisit it. The reports from Ronald Johnson and the other witnesses created a chain of events: a close encounter claim, a family observation of a departing light, a local report of another unusual light, and the discovery of the ring.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/apro-bulletin-apro-bulletin-1971-11-00-november-december/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

What the testimony cannot settle on its own is the ultimate identity of the object. The accounts establish that the Johnson family experienced something they considered extraordinary, and that several people reported unusual lights that night. Whether that event represented an unknown physical phenomenon, a misidentified natural occurrence, or something more unusual remains the central unresolved question of the Delphos case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/PDFs/JUFOS/1991_NS_vol3_JUFOS.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesISSN: 0730-5748...</span></span></span>
