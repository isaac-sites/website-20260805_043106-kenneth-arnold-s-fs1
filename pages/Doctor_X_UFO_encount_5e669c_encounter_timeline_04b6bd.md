## Introduction

The 1968 “Doctor X” UFO encounter is remembered less as a conventional sighting report than as a sequence of linked events: a late-night observation of unusual lights, a close encounter involving a reported beam of light, and claims of sudden physical changes afterwards. According to accounts published by UFO investigators, the event took place during the night of 1–2 November 1968 in southern France and involved a physician who kept his identity private. The reported timeline begins with a child waking during a storm and ends with the witness recording sketches and notes before later noticing unexpected changes in his health.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://isaackoi.com/ufo-history/ufo/19681102-qdr-xq-healing-encounter/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: isaackoi.com">[Isaac Koi Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">isaackoi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">19681102 qdr xq healing encounter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Isaac Koi Archive1968.1102 &quot;Dr. X&quot; healing encounter &#124; Isaac Koi ArchiveNovember 2, 1968...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 1968</span></span></span>

## The night of the reported sighting

### A storm, a child at the window, and the first lights

The reported incident began in the early hours of 2 November 1968. In the accounts later circulated by investigators, Doctor X was awakened by his young child during a storm. The doctor initially interpreted flashes outside as possible lightning, but the child’s behaviour drew his attention back towards the window. He reportedly moved through the house and looked outside to investigate the source of the unusual illumination.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/ufos/waves/dr-x-1960s-ufo-invasion" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">dr x 1960s ufo invasion</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe “Dr. X” Encounter And The 1960s French UFO Invasion - UFO InsightJune 18, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 18, 2018</span></span></span>

The timing is a key feature of the case because the event was described as occurring before dawn, in difficult viewing conditions. According to Vallée’s account, the doctor was already dealing with a painful leg injury sustained several days earlier while chopping wood, meaning the reported sighting was linked from the beginning to a physical condition that later became central to the story.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/jacques-vallee-ufos-the-psychic-solution-1977/6915111" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: slideshare.net">[www.slideshare.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">slideshare.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">www.slideshare.net Jacques Vallee</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Vallee - UFOs - The Psychic Solution (1977) &#124; PDF...</span></span></span>

### The reported observation from the house

According to the witness narrative, Doctor X saw two luminous objects outside the property. Later descriptions characterised them as disc-shaped objects with unusual light patterns and horizontal structures that were interpreted as antenna-like features. The objects were said to move close together and then merge into a single luminous form before directing an intense light towards the house.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/2a-epoca-anomalia-2a-epoca-no-05-2005/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

The reported sequence was not described as a distant observation of lights in the sky. Instead, it was presented as a close-range encounter, with the objects appearing near the home and interacting with the surrounding environment. Some accounts placed the final object at roughly the scale of a large structure, although these estimates came from witness interpretation rather than measurements taken at the time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/2a-epoca-anomalia-2a-epoca-no-05-2005/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

## The objects and unusual light effects

### From two lights to one luminous object

The most distinctive part of the sighting is the reported transformation of the objects. In later retellings, Doctor X described two separate luminous forms that approached each other until they became a single object. The encounter was said to involve repeated flashes, increasing brightness, and a final intense beam directed towards the witness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/2a-epoca-anomalia-2a-epoca-no-05-2005/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

This progression shaped how later investigators classified the case. Rather than treating it simply as an unidentified light in the sky, they considered it a close encounter because the witness described apparent structure, movement, and a direct effect on him. Vallée highlighted this aspect when discussing the case as one of the reports where an observed phenomenon was followed by personal and physical consequences.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://isaackoi.com/ufo-history/ufo/19681102-qdr-xq-healing-encounter/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: isaackoi.com">[Isaac Koi Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">isaackoi.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">19681102 qdr xq healing encounter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Isaac Koi Archive1968.1102 &quot;Dr. X&quot; healing encounter &#124; Isaac Koi ArchiveNovember 2, 1968...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 1968</span></span></span>

### The beam, disappearance, and immediate reaction

The reported climax occurred when the luminous object allegedly projected a powerful beam towards Doctor X. Accounts differ slightly in wording, but they describe a sudden bright illumination, a loud sound or shock-like event, and the object’s rapid disappearance. Some versions describe a lingering glow or upward-moving light after the main object vanished.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufology.patrickgross.org/htm/x.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufology.patrickgross.org">[Ufology]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufology.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: UFOs A to Z: X...</span></span></span>

After the event, Doctor X reportedly returned inside, documented what he believed had happened by making sketches and notes, and woke his wife. The existence of those notes and drawings became an important part of later retellings because investigators viewed them as a record created close to the reported event rather than a story developed only years afterwards.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufology.patrickgross.org/htm/x.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufology.patrickgross.org">[Ufology]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufology.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: UFOs A to Z: X...</span></span></span>

## The events reported after the encounter

### The claimed recovery of the leg injury

The most famous part of the Doctor X timeline occurred immediately after the sighting. According to the published accounts, his wife noticed that he was no longer limping while he was describing the event. When they examined his injured leg, the swelling and pain that had followed the earlier wood-chopping accident were reportedly gone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.scribd.com/document/731742835/Blum-Beyond-Earth-Man-s-Contact-with-UFOs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Blum Beyond Earth Man s Contact with UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Sightings: A 1973 Overview &#124; PDF &#124; Unidentified Flying Object...</span></span></span>

UFO researchers later treated this as the defining feature of the case, describing it as a possible “healing” associated with a close encounter. However, the medical evidence available publicly is limited.
