## Introduction

The Navy pilot encounters associated with the USS *Theodore Roosevelt* (CVN-71) in 2014–2015 form the operational backdrop for the “Gimbal” video. The 34-second infrared recording was not an isolated sighting; it emerged from a period in which F/A-18 aviators assigned to the Roosevelt carrier strike group reported repeated unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP) during training flights off the U.S. East Coast. The importance of these reports lies less in proving a particular explanation and more in showing how trained military observers, advanced sensors, and aviation safety procedures interacted when crews encountered objects they could not immediately identify.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/33371/here-are-the-detailed-ufo-incident-reports-from-navy-pilots-flying-off-the-east-coast" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Here Are The Navy Pilot Reports From Encounters With Mysterious Aircraft Off The East CoastMay 13, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 13, 2020</span></span></span>

## The Roosevelt deployment timeline

### Training flights before the famous video

The Roosevelt encounters developed during a period of intensive carrier aviation preparation. In late 2014 and early 2015, Carrier Air Wing 1 and associated Navy squadrons were conducting training activities before the carrier’s scheduled global deployment. Reports from aviators placed many of the encounters in training areas off the U.S. East Coast, including the W-72 warning area near Virginia and North Carolina.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/33371/here-are-the-detailed-ufo-incident-reports-from-navy-pilots-flying-off-the-east-coast" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Here Are The Navy Pilot Reports From Encounters With Mysterious Aircraft Off The East CoastMay 13, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 13, 2020</span></span></span>

These were not casual observations by individual witnesses. The reports came from F/A-18 Super Hornet crews operating with military radar, infrared targeting systems, and standard aviation procedures. According to accounts from pilots including Lieutenant Ryan Graves, the frequency of the contacts led crews to treat them as an operational issue rather than a one-off curiosity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapinvestigations.com/navy-pilot-uap-encounters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapinvestigations.com">[UAP Investigations]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapinvestigations.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP InvestigationsNavy Pilot UAP Encounters: What Roosevelt Squadron Pilots Reported -March 29, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 29, 2026</span></span></span>

The operational environment matters because Navy training ranges are designed for complex air operations. Aircraft crews routinely encounter friendly aircraft, drones, weather effects, and sensor artefacts. The significance of the Roosevelt reports was that some contacts reportedly persisted through multiple detection methods and could not be immediately matched to known aircraft or training activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/33371/here-are-the-detailed-ufo-incident-reports-from-navy-pilots-flying-off-the-east-coast" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Here Are The Navy Pilot Reports From Encounters With Mysterious Aircraft Off The East CoastMay 13, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 13, 2020</span></span></span>

### The carrier’s 2015 deployment

The USS *Theodore Roosevelt* departed for its 2015 around-the-world deployment in March 2015. The carrier strike group moved through U.S. Sixth Fleet and Fifth Fleet areas before conducting operations in support of Operation Inherent Resolve and other missions. The ship later completed an eight-and-a-half-month deployment, arriving at its new homeport in San Diego in November 2015.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cpf.navy.mil/Newsroom/News/Article/2745348/theodore-roosevelt-to-complete-world-deployment/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cpf.navy.mil">[Navy Civilian Payroll]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cpf.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Navy Civilian PayrollTheodore Roosevelt to Complete World Deployment &gt; U.S. Pacific Fleet &gt; NewsNovember 19, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2015</span></span></span>

This timeline is important because the best-known Roosevelt UAP recordings occurred before the carrier entered its overseas operational phase. The Gimbal footage is associated with the training period before deployment, when aircrews were preparing for carrier strike operations and conducting high-tempo aviation exercises.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.iufodb.com/gimbal/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: iufodb.com">[Internet UFO Database]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">iufodb.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Internet UFO DatabaseGimbal UFO Video 2015June 30, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 30, 2024</span></span></span>

## Pilot observations and testimony

### What the aviators reported seeing

The Roosevelt-related reports involved several types of observations rather than a single encounter. Pilots described objects detected by aircraft systems and, in some cases, objects that appeared unusual during closer investigation. One widely discussed report involved an F/A-18 crew encountering an object described by witnesses as a cube-like shape inside a translucent sphere. Another involved objects tracked through aircraft sensors and later associated with the Gimbal and GoFast videos.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/33371/here-are-the-detailed-ufo-incident-reports-from-navy-pilots-flying-off-the-east-coast" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Here Are The Navy Pilot Reports From Encounters With Mysterious Aircraft Off The East CoastMay 13, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 13, 2020</span></span></span>

Ryan Graves, an F/A-18 pilot with Strike Fighter Squadron 11 (VFA-11), later described the encounters publicly, explaining that pilots were concerned primarily with the aviation safety implications of unknown objects operating near military aircraft. His account helped shift discussion away from the traditional “mystery sighting” framing and towards questions about airspace awareness, reporting procedures, and potential technological risks.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/28453/navy-f-a-18-pilot-shares-new-details-about-ufo-encounters-during-middle-east-deployment" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Navy F/A-18 Pilot Shares New Details About UFO Encounters During Middle East Deployment...</span></span></span>

Other pilots involved in the Roosevelt period, including Lieutenant Danny Accoin, described attempts to investigate contacts using aircraft systems. Their accounts highlighted a recurring difficulty: a contact might appear on one system but not another, or might not produce enough information for immediate identification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://legalclarity.org/navy-uap-key-encounters-hearings-and-disclosure/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: legalclarity.org">[LegalClarity]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">legalclarity.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Legal Clarity Navy UAP: Key Encounters, Hearings, and Disclosure</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Navy UAP: Key Encounters, Hearings, and Disclosure - LegalClarityJuly 2, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2026</span></span></span>

### Why pilot testimony matters — and where it stops


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

Military aviators are valuable witnesses because their training involves identifying aircraft, judging distances, and interpreting instrument information under demanding conditions. In the Roosevelt case, the credibility issue is therefore not simply whether pilots saw something unusual; it is how their observations should be interpreted alongside incomplete sensor data.

</div>

Pilot testimony can establish that experienced personnel encountered something they could not identify at the time. It cannot, by itself, determine whether the cause was an advanced foreign system, a conventional aircraft viewed under unusual conditions, a sensor-related effect, or another explanation. This distinction has remained central to official assessments of UAP cases.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapinvestigations.com/navy-pilot-uap-encounters-nimitz-to-roosevelt/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapinvestigations.com">[UAP Investigations]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapinvestigations.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Investigations Navy Pilot UAP Encounters</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Investigations Navy Pilot UAP Encounters</span></span></span>

## How witness reports fit the record

### From routine training reports to the Gimbal footage

The Gimbal video became significant because it provided a physical record connected to the broader Roosevelt encounters. Recorded by an F/A-18 Super Hornet’s ATFLIR (Advanced Targeting Forward-Looking Infrared) system in January 2015, the clip shows an infrared object that appears to rotate as the sensor tracks it. The recording was later released by the U.S. government and formally acknowledged as genuine Navy footage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.iufodb.com/gimbal/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: iufodb.com">[Internet UFO Database]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">iufodb.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Internet UFO DatabaseGimbal UFO Video 2015June 30, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 30, 2024</span></span></span>

### The unresolved question: unknown object or incomplete identification?

The central dispute surrounding the Roosevelt encounters is not whether Navy personnel reported unusual events. That part of the record is well established. The dispute concerns what those events represented.

Several possibilities remain discussed:

* **Conventional aircraft or equipment:** Some analysts argue that unusual viewing angles, infrared effects, distance estimates, or incomplete situational awareness could make ordinary objects appear extraordinary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08773" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Reconstruction of Potential Flight Paths for the January 2015 Gimbal UAPJune 15, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 15, 2023</span></span></span>

## Why the Roosevelt pilots changed the UAP discussion

The lasting impact of the Roosevelt encounters came from changing the type of question being asked. Earlier UFO debates often focused on individual sightings and personal credibility. The Roosevelt reports shifted attention toward a modern military problem: how should armed forces respond when trained personnel and advanced sensors detect objects that cannot immediately be classified?

The case also helped normalise reporting within the U.S. military. Rather than treating unidentified objects as automatically belonging to folklore or speculation, Navy officials increasingly framed them as potential airspace awareness and safety issues. The Roosevelt pilots’ accounts became part of a broader movement that encouraged more formal UAP reporting procedures and greater public discussion of military encounters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapinvestigations.com/navy-pilot-uap-encounters-nimitz-to-roosevelt/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapinvestigations.com">[UAP Investigations]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapinvestigations.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Investigations Navy Pilot UAP Encounters</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Investigations Navy Pilot UAP Encounters</span></span></span>

For the Gimbal case specifically, the pilot encounters provide the human and operational foundation. The video is the most visible piece of evidence, but the surrounding 2014–2015 Roosevelt operations explain why that short clip became a major reference point in the modern UAP debate. The enduring question is not simply what the pilots saw, but how military aviation systems should handle events that remain unidentified after professional investigation.
