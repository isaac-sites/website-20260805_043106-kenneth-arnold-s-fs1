## Introduction

The Nimitz Tic Tac encounter became widely discussed not only because of the later-released infrared video, but because several trained Navy personnel gave consistent firsthand accounts of what they saw and how they encountered it. The central witnesses were Commander David Fravor and Lieutenant Commander Alex Dietrich, two F/A-18 pilots who reported a close visual encounter with a white, wingless object during a training mission in November 2004. Their accounts were later joined by other aircrew and ship personnel who described radar contacts, mission tasking and follow-on observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/witnesses/david-fravor/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">David Fravor &#124; UAP Witness Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">David Fravor &#124; UAP Witness Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

## The Fravor and Dietrich encounter

The most influential eyewitness account comes from Commander David Fravor, then commanding officer of Strike Fighter Squadron 41, and Lieutenant Commander Alex Dietrich, who was flying another F/A-18F in the same area. Both crews were sent to investigate unusual activity detected during a Navy exercise involving the USS Nimitz Carrier Strike Group.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/witnesses/david-fravor/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">David Fravor &#124; UAP Witness Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">David Fravor &#124; UAP Witness Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

According to their accounts, the pilots descended towards an area of ocean where they saw disturbance on the water surface and a white object moving above it. Fravor described the object as approximately shaped like a “Tic Tac” mint: smooth, elongated, without visible wings, exhaust, rotors or other obvious propulsion features. Dietrich has similarly described seeing a white object that did not resemble a conventional aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/tic-tac-ufo-sighting-uap-video-dave-fravor-alex-dietrich-navy-fighter-pilots-house-testimony/?ftag=CNM-00-10aab4i" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cbsnews.com">[CBS News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cbsnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CBS NewsThe story behind the &quot;Tic Tac&quot; UFO sighting by Navy pilots in 2004 - CBS News...</span></span></span>

A key detail in their testimony is that the observation was not a distant glimpse. The pilots reported watching the object closely while manoeuvring their aircraft nearby. Fravor stated that he attempted to approach the object, and he described its movement as unusual compared with aircraft he was familiar with. Dietrich has emphasised that the encounter was brief and that visual impressions from an airborne cockpit are not the same as a complete technical measurement of an object’s performance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/tic-tac-ufo-sighting-uap-video-dave-fravor-alex-dietrich-navy-fighter-pilots-house-testimony/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cbsnews.com">[CBS News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cbsnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CBS NewsThe story behind the &quot;Tic Tac&quot; UFO sighting by Navy pilots in 2004 - CBS News...</span></span></span>

The two pilots’ accounts are significant because they were independent observers operating in separate aircraft. They also had different levels of experience, meaning the testimony was not based on one individual’s interpretation alone. However, their reports remain observations rather than measurements: they could describe appearance, movement and relative behaviour, but they did not have instruments recording every parameter needed to determine speed, distance or physical characteristics.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/witnesses/david-fravor/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">David Fravor &#124; UAP Witness Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">David Fravor &#124; UAP Witness Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

## Why trained pilots changed the debate

The credibility of the Nimitz witnesses is one of the main reasons the case attracted attention. Fighter pilots are trained to identify aircraft, judge relative motion and operate under demanding conditions. Supporters of the case argue that these skills make their observations more valuable than ordinary eyewitness reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/witnesses/david-fravor/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">David Fravor &#124; UAP Witness Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">David Fravor &#124; UAP Witness Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>

The witnesses also had professional incentives to avoid exaggeration. At the time of the incident, UAP reports were not a normal topic of public discussion within military aviation, and the pilots did not immediately present their encounter as a major mystery. The account became widely known years later, after renewed public interest in military UAP investigations and the release of Navy footage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/tic-tac-ufo-sighting-uap-video-dave-fravor-alex-dietrich-navy-fighter-pilots-house-testimony/?ftag=CNM-00-10aab4i" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cbsnews.com">[CBS News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cbsnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CBS NewsThe story behind the &quot;Tic Tac&quot; UFO sighting by Navy pilots in 2004 - CBS News...</span></span></span>

Several factors strengthen the evidential value of the testimony:

* **Multiple observers:** Fravor and Dietrich were accompanied by weapons systems officers in their aircraft, meaning more than one person reported seeing the object during the intercept phase.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/tic-tac-ufo-sighting-uap-video-dave-fravor-alex-dietrich-navy-fighter-pilots-house-testimony/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cbsnews.com">[CBS News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cbsnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CBS NewsThe story behind the &quot;Tic Tac&quot; UFO sighting by Navy pilots in 2004 - CBS News...</span></span></span>
* **Military operating environment:** The witnesses were flying advanced fighter aircraft during a structured training exercise rather than observing casually from the ground.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/tic-tac-ufo-sighting-uap-video-dave-fravor-alex-dietrich-navy-fighter-pilots-house-testimony/?ftag=CNM-00-10aab4i" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cbsnews.com">[CBS News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cbsnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CBS NewsThe story behind the &quot;Tic Tac&quot; UFO sighting by Navy pilots in 2004 - CBS News...</span></span></span>
* **Consistency over time:** Public interviews years after the event have broadly preserved the same core description: a white, smooth object with no obvious conventional aircraft features.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uaprad.org/witnesses/david-fravor/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uaprad.org">[UAPRAD]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uaprad.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">David Fravor &#124; UAP Witness Research &#124; UAPRAD</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">David Fravor &#124; UAP Witness Research &#124; UAPRAD...</span></span></span>
* **Connection with other evidence streams:** Their encounter occurred within a wider sequence involving reports from USS Princeton personnel and a later infrared recording made by another Navy aircraft. These evidence streams are related but should not be treated as identical; the video did not capture the original visual encounter described by Fravor and Dietrich.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.strangerthanfiction.org/2026/05/tic-tac-ufo-nimitz-sensor-data/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: strangerthanfiction.org">[Stranger Than Fiction]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">strangerthanfiction.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Stranger Than Fiction The Tic Tac Encounter &#124; Stranger than Fiction</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stranger Than FictionThe Tic Tac Encounter &#124; Stranger than FictionMay 8, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 8, 2026</span></span></span>

At the same time, witness expertise does not remove all uncertainty. Human perception remains limited, even for trained observers. Judging distance, size and speed from an aircraft cockpit can be difficult, especially during a short, unexpected encounter. ## What witnesses could and could not prove

The Nimitz pilots’ testimony is strongest as evidence that trained Navy personnel encountered something they could not confidently identify. It does not, by itself, prove a specific explanation for the object. The accounts support the existence of an unusual observation; they do not establish whether the cause was an unknown aircraft, a sensor-related misunderstanding, a classified system, or something beyond current explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://disclosurefiles.org/cases/nimitz-tic-tac-uf-26003/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: disclosurefiles.org">[Disclosure Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">disclosurefiles.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nimitz tic tac uf 26003</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Disclosure FilesUSS Nimitz 2004 &quot;Tic-Tac&quot; UAP Encounter // DISCLOSURE FILESJune 2, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 2, 2026</span></span></span>
