## Introduction

The physical evidence record of the Alan Godfrey encounter is unusual because the case contains more documentation than many UFO reports, but relatively little material evidence that can be independently tested. The strongest surviving evidence is documentary: a police officer’s contemporaneous report, his sketch of the object, the recorded investigation of the location, and later research by UFO investigators. These records help establish that Godfrey reported a serious experience while on duty, but they do not by themselves prove what caused it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

## Police report and contemporaneous sketch

### What was recorded at the scene?

One of the most important features of the Godfrey case is that the initial account came from a serving police officer making a report connected to his professional duties. On 28 November 1980, Alan Godfrey reported seeing a large illuminated object on Burnley Road near Todmorden while searching for stray cattle during a night shift. According to case records, he initially mistook the object for a vehicle before approaching and identifying it as something unusual.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

Godfrey’s written report and later accounts described the object as a flattened, diamond-like shape with a rotating lower section. A key part of the documentary record is that he made a sketch in his notebook during the encounter rather than producing a drawing years later. This has made the sketch one of the most frequently discussed pieces of evidence because it provides a near-contemporary record of his perception of the object’s shape and features.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

The sketch does not independently confirm the object’s nature. A drawing made by a witness records what that person believed they saw, not necessarily the physical characteristics of the thing observed. However, in evaluating the reliability of the report, the timing of the sketch matters: it reduces the possibility that later retellings alone shaped every detail of the account.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

### The importance of official documentation

The existence of a police report distinguishes the Godfrey case from many informal sightings that survive only through later interviews. The report provides evidence that the event was reported promptly and that Godfrey treated it as a genuine incident rather than a story created after public attention developed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

At the same time, official documentation should not be confused with official confirmation. A police record demonstrates that a report was made and investigated; it does not establish that the reported object was an unknown craft or that extraordinary explanations are correct. This distinction is central to assessing the case fairly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

## Site observations and physical claims

### The reported road trace

The most discussed physical claim in the Godfrey case concerns the location where he said the object hovered. Accounts from investigators describe Godfrey returning to the area and observing a patch of road that appeared unusually dry despite wet conditions, with a swirling pattern on the surface. This observation became an important part of the case narrative because it suggested a possible interaction between the object and the environment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

However, the evidential strength of this claim is limited. The observation was not preserved as a forensic sample, photographed under controlled conditions, or analysed with modern environmental testing. The record depends largely on witness descriptions and later investigation accounts. As a result, the road feature remains an interesting reported anomaly rather than a confirmed physical trace.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

### Other physical claims connected to the encounter

Godfrey also reported personal effects that became part of later investigations, including damage to his boot and an unexplained mark on his leg after the reported encounter. These details contributed to the later abduction interpretation of the case, but they present the same evidential problem: there is limited independent documentation showing when or how these changes occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

The physical record therefore differs from cases involving recoverable objects, photographs, radar recordings, or multiple independent witnesses. In the Godfrey encounter, the main physical claims are observations associated with the witness and location rather than objects that can be examined by outside investigators.

## Investigations and evidence limits

### Later investigators and competing interpretations

The case attracted attention from UFO researchers including Jenny Randles and later writers who examined the police documentation, witness testimony, and circumstances surrounding the encounter. Their work helped preserve and publicise the evidence trail, particularly the importance of the original report and sketch.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

This creates two separate evidence categories within the case:

* **Contemporary evidence:** the police report, the initial sketch, and the immediate account of [the sighting]({{ 'the-sighting-789627/' | relative_url }}).
* **Later interpretive evidence:** investigations, expanded memories, and explanations developed after the event.

The first category is stronger because it is closer in time to the reported incident. ### What evidence is missing?

The biggest limitation of the Godfrey case is the absence of independent physical confirmation. There is no publicly available material object from the encounter, no photograph of the alleged craft, and no confirmed instrument record showing an unexplained object at the location. The case also relies primarily on a single main witness for the central sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

This does not mean the documentation has no value. The case remains significant because it shows how a well-documented report can still leave major questions unresolved. A credible witness and a detailed record can establish that an unusual experience occurred, while still leaving open different explanations for what that experience represented.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/todmorden-godfrey-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Todmorden · PC Alan Godfrey · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

The evidence trail of the Alan Godfrey encounter is therefore strongest as a record of a puzzling reported event: substantial enough to support serious historical investigation, but incomplete enough that the cause of the experience remains disputed.
