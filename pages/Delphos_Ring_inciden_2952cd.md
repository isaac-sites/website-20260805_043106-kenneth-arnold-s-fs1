## What happened on the night of 2 November 1971?

Ronald Johnson was tending sheep on his family’s property near Delphos, Kansas, at around 7:00 p.m. when he reported hearing a vibrating or mechanical sound and seeing a brightly illuminated object near a group of trees. He described the object as roughly nine feet in diameter and about ten feet high, with a domed shape and intense blue, red, and orange light. According to his account, the object hovered only a few feet above the ground before rising away at an angle. The bright light reportedly affected his vision temporarily.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/types-of-ufos/close-encounters-of-the-second-kind/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center for UFO Studies Close Encounters of the Second Kind</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesClose Encounters of the Second Kind - Center for UFO Studies...</span></span></span>

Johnson then alerted his parents, Durel and Erma Johnson. They reported seeing a bright object moving away in the distance, although they did not describe observing the close-range object in the same detail as Ronald. A separate report collected by local investigators involved Lester Ensbarger, who said he saw a bright descending or rising light in the Delphos area around the same time, providing a limited additional witness account.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufocasebook.com/Kansas.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufocasebook.com">[UFO Casebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufocasebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Casebook1971, The Delphos Kansas UFO Landing Ring...</span></span></span>

When the Johnson family returned to the location where Ronald said the object had hovered, they found a circular area of pale, glowing material on the ground. The ring was described as approximately eight feet across, with a lighter band of affected soil and a central area that appeared different from the surrounding ground. The family also reported unusual effects when touching the material, including numbness in the fingers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/apro-bulletin-apro-bulletin-1971-11-00-november-december/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

## Why the ground trace made Delphos unusual

Many UFO reports rely primarily on eyewitness testimony, but the Delphos case attracted attention because investigators believed there was something physical to examine. The reported ring was photographed and inspected by local authorities, journalists, and UFO researchers. Investigators noted that the affected soil appeared unusually resistant to absorbing water compared with nearby control soil.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://iccra.org/bystate/Kansas/ICCRA%20-%20KS%20-%20Delphos%2C%20Ottawa%20County%20%28November%202%2C%201971%29.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: iccra.org">[iccra.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">iccra.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Kansas - Delphos, Ottawa County (November 2, 1971)August 10, 2011...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 1971</span></span></span>

Ted Phillips, a physical-trace investigator associated with the Center for UFO Studies (CUFOS), visited the site and collected samples from the ring and surrounding areas. Later analyses compared the affected soil with control samples taken nearby. Reports associated with the investigation described differences in acidity, soluble salts, calcium content, and water resistance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/PDFs/JUFOS/1991_NS_vol3_JUFOS.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesISSN: 0730-5748...</span></span></span>

The most frequently discussed findings included:

* **Hydrophobic soil behaviour:** Water reportedly did not penetrate the ring soil in the same way as ordinary surrounding earth.
* **Chemical differences:** Analyses reported increased calcium and soluble salts compared with control samples.
* **Organic material:** Some investigators examined white fibrous material associated with the ring and proposed biological explanations.
* **No clear evidence of extreme heat:** Later analysis did not find signs that the soil had simply been exposed to a high-temperature event such as a burn or blast.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://iccra.org/bystate/Kansas/ICCRA%20-%20KS%20-%20Delphos%2C%20Ottawa%20County%20%28November%202%2C%201971%29.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: iccra.org">[iccra.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">iccra.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Kansas - Delphos, Ottawa County (November 2, 1971)August 10, 2011...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 1971</span></span></span>

These findings made Delphos more influential among UFO researchers because the debate moved beyond “what did someone see?” to “what caused the measurable changes in the ground?”

## The competing explanations: anomaly or natural process?

The main argument for the UFO interpretation is that several unusual details occurred together: a close-range sighting, multiple [witnesses]({{ 'witnesses-0a727c/' | relative_url }}) seeing lights, a physical ring appearing immediately afterwards, reported physiological effects, and soil differences that were investigated independently. Researchers such as those involved with CUFOS treated the case as a significant close encounter with physical evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cufos.org/types-of-ufos/close-encounters-of-the-second-kind/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cufos.org">[Center for UFO Studies]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cufos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Center for UFO Studies Close Encounters of the Second Kind</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Center for UFO StudiesClose Encounters of the Second Kind - Center for UFO Studies...</span></span></span>

However, the physical evidence has never established an extraterrestrial cause. Later analyses suggested that the unusual soil properties could have resulted from chemical or biological processes. A major alternative interpretation involved naturally occurring organisms capable of producing ring-shaped growth patterns and unusual surface effects. Jacques Vallée and other researchers discussed biological explanations involving actinomycetes, fungus-like organisms that can form circular patterns under some conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://spookyvalley.com/events/ufo-delphos-kansas-ring-1971/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: spookyvalley.com">[Spooky Valley]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">spookyvalley.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Spooky Valley The Delphos Ring &#124; Spooky Valley</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Spooky Valley The Delphos Ring &#124; Spooky Valley</span></span></span>

A later review of preserved soil samples also proposed that chemical compounds such as oxalate-related materials could help explain some reported features. The analysis suggested that naturally occurring substances could account for properties such as water resistance, irritation effects, and possible light-emitting reactions under certain conditions, while also stating that the findings neither proved nor completely ruled out a UFO-related origin.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/analysis-soil-samples-related-delphos-kansas-november-2-1971/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault Analysis of Soil Samples Related to the Delphos, Kansas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultAnalysis of Soil Samples Related to the Delphos, Kansas November 2, 1971 - The Black Vault Case FilesOctober 13, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 1971</span></span></span>

The main difficulty for natural explanations is not necessarily whether individual effects are possible, but whether they explain the timing and combination of events: why a ring appeared at the same location and time as the reported sighting, and whether a natural process could account for all observations together. Conversely, the UFO explanation faces the challenge that no physical analysis has identified material or evidence uniquely requiring an advanced unknown craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/analysis-soil-samples-related-delphos-kansas-november-2-1971/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault Analysis of Soil Samples Related to the Delphos, Kansas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultAnalysis of Soil Samples Related to the Delphos, Kansas November 2, 1971 - The Black Vault Case FilesOctober 13, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 1971</span></span></span>

## Investigations and the status of the evidence

The Delphos incident was not investigated by the US Air Force’s Project Blue Book, which had ended in 1969. Instead, later examination came mainly from private UFO research organisations, journalists, and independent analysts. CUFOS researchers, including astronomer J. Allen Hynek and investigator Ted Phillips, became associated with the case through the collection and study of trace evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.secretsdeclassified.af.mil/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/459832/project-blue-book/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: secretsdeclassified.af.mil">[Secrets Declassified]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">secretsdeclassified.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on af.mil.</span></span></span>

The survival of stored soil samples became one of the case’s most unusual features. Samples preserved after the original investigation were reportedly rediscovered decades later and examined again. Those later studies found that some unusual characteristics remained detectable, although researchers acknowledged that long storage could complicate interpretation because soil chemistry can change over time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/analysis-soil-samples-related-delphos-kansas-november-2-1971/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault Analysis of Soil Samples Related to the Delphos, Kansas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultAnalysis of Soil Samples Related to the Delphos, Kansas November 2, 1971 - The Black Vault Case FilesOctober 13, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 2, 1971</span></span></span>

The evidential picture is therefore mixed:

* **Strong points:** contemporary witness accounts, local documentation, physical samples, and attempts at laboratory analysis.
* **Weak points:** the absence of a uniquely identifiable cause, limited scientific publication in mainstream journals, and uncertainty about how much the soil changed after decades of storage.

  function buildUrl(query, reference, marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
