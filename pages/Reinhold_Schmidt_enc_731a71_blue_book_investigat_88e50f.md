## Introduction

The US Air Force investigation of the Reinhold Schmidt encounter was conducted through Project Blue Book, the official programme created to collect and evaluate reports of unidentified flying objects. In the Kearney, Nebraska, case of November 1957, investigators examined Schmidt’s claim that he had encountered a landed craft and its occupants, but the inquiry did not produce verified evidence of a spacecraft, extraterrestrial material, or a confirmed landing event. The case therefore remained an unresolved personal report rather than an officially validated UFO event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.secretsdeclassified.af.mil/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/459832/project-blue-book/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: secretsdeclassified.af.mil">[Secrets Declassified]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">secretsdeclassified.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on af.mil.</span></span></span>

## Project Blue Book’s role in the investigation

Project Blue Book was the Air Force’s main UFO investigation programme from 1952 until 1969. Its stated purposes were to determine whether UFO reports represented a national security concern and to analyse sightings scientifically. The programme eventually collected more than 12,000 reports, with hundreds remaining classified as unidentified, although the Air Force concluded that no investigated case demonstrated evidence of extraterrestrial vehicles or a threat to national security.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.secretsdeclassified.af.mil/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/459832/project-blue-book/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: secretsdeclassified.af.mil">[Secrets Declassified]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">secretsdeclassified.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on af.mil.</span></span></span>

The Schmidt report reached Blue Book during a period of unusually high public interest in UFO sightings. Unlike a distant observation of an unknown light or object, the Kearney report involved a claimed close encounter with occupants, making the investigation dependent on questions of witness reliability, physical evidence, and whether independent observations supported the account.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AProject_Blue_Book_report_-_1957-11-7230046-Kearney-Nebraska.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: commons.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">commons.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Commons File:Project Blue Book report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wikimedia CommonsFile:Project Blue Book report - 1957-11-7230046-Kearney-Nebraska.pdf - Wikimedia Commons...</span></span></span>

The Blue Book approach required investigators to separate what could be documented from what depended solely on testimony. A detailed witness narrative could make a case significant, but it did not by itself establish that an unusual event had occurred. For the Air Force, confirmation required supporting evidence such as physical traces, reliable measurements, multiple independent witnesses, or data that ruled out ordinary explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Project BLUE BOOK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesProject BLUE BOOK - Unidentified Flying Objects &#124; National ArchivesJune 25, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 25, 2024</span></span></span>

## The reported landing site examination

A central part of the Schmidt investigation was the alleged landing area near Kearney. Schmidt directed attention to a location where he said the craft had been positioned and where unusual traces, including a greenish substance, were reportedly present. The Air Force examination did not verify that a spacecraft had landed there. The available investigation record did not identify a material or physical feature that required an extraterrestrial explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AProject_Blue_Book_report_-_1957-11-7230046-Kearney-Nebraska.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: commons.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">commons.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Commons File:Project Blue Book report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wikimedia CommonsFile:Project Blue Book report - 1957-11-7230046-Kearney-Nebraska.pdf - Wikimedia Commons...</span></span></span>

The reported substance became one of the main points of discussion surrounding the case. Later accounts of the investigation describe the material as having an ordinary explanation involving motor oil rather than an unknown substance connected to a spacecraft. This interpretation weakened the physical-evidence argument because the most concrete claimed trace could be explained without invoking an extraordinary cause.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AProject_Blue_Book_report_-_1957-11-7230046-Kearney-Nebraska.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: commons.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">commons.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Commons File:Project Blue Book report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wikimedia CommonsFile:Project Blue Book report - 1957-11-7230046-Kearney-Nebraska.pdf - Wikimedia Commons...</span></span></span>

The lack of confirmed physical evidence was decisive for Blue Book’s evaluation. Investigators could document that Schmidt reported a remarkable experience, but they could not demonstrate that the site contained evidence of a landed vehicle. In official terms, the case remained an allegation supported primarily by the testimony of one witness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Project BLUE BOOK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesProject BLUE BOOK - Unidentified Flying Objects &#124; National ArchivesJune 25, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 25, 2024</span></span></span>

## Why the case remained unverified

The unresolved status of the Reinhold Schmidt case came from a gap between the strength of the claim and the strength of the evidence. Schmidt described a detailed encounter involving a craft, occupants, conversation, and a landing site, but Blue Book investigators did not find independent confirmation that matched those elements.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AProject_Blue_Book_report_-_1957-11-7230046-Kearney-Nebraska.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: commons.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">commons.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Commons File:Project Blue Book report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wikimedia CommonsFile:Project Blue Book report - 1957-11-7230046-Kearney-Nebraska.pdf - Wikimedia Commons...</span></span></span>

For UFO investigators, the case illustrated a recurring challenge: a witness can provide a sincere and detailed account while the available evidence remains insufficient to establish what actually occurred. Blue Book’s classification system was designed to distinguish between reports that could be identified through investigation and those where the information was incomplete or inconclusive. An inability to verify a report did not automatically mean investigators had explained every detail, but it also did not establish the extraordinary interpretation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.secretsdeclassified.af.mil/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/459832/project-blue-book/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: secretsdeclassified.af.mil">[Secrets Declassified]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">secretsdeclassified.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on af.mil.</span></span></span>

The broader findings of Project Blue Book shaped how the Air Force treated cases like Schmidt’s. After reviewing thousands of reports, the Air Force concluded that no UFO case it investigated provided evidence of extraterrestrial vehicles or technology beyond contemporary scientific understanding. The Schmidt encounter therefore remained historically notable as a claimed contact case, but it did not become an officially confirmed example of a landed unknown craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.secretsdeclassified.af.mil/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/459832/project-blue-book/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: secretsdeclassified.af.mil">[Secrets Declassified]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">secretsdeclassified.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on af.mil.</span></span></span>

## The lasting significance of Blue Book’s conclusion

The Project Blue Book investigation did not resolve every question raised by Schmidt’s account, but it established the official evidentiary position: the Air Force found no verified spacecraft, no confirmed extraterrestrial material, and no physical evidence that demonstrated a non-human origin.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Project BLUE BOOK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesProject BLUE BOOK - Unidentified Flying Objects &#124; National ArchivesJune 25, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 25, 2024</span></span></span>

For later researchers, the case remains useful because it shows the difference between an unexplained report and a proven event.   function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
