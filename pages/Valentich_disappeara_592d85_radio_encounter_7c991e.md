## Introduction

The final radio encounter between Frederick Valentich and Melbourne Flight Service is the strongest surviving record of the 1978 disappearance because it captures the event as it unfolded rather than relying on later recollections. On the evening of 21 October 1978, Valentich reported that an unidentified object was near his Cessna 182L over Bass Strait, described its appearance and movements, and continued communicating with flight service until the transmission ended. The recording does not identify what he encountered or what caused the aircraft to disappear, but it provides a precise timeline of his observations, his changing assessment of the object, and the final moments before contact was lost.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pe2bz.philpem.me.uk/Comm/-%20ELF-VLF/-%20ELF-Theory/Th-112-Ufo-Misc-Odd/valensum.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pe2bz.philpem.me.uk">[PhilPem]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pe2bz.philpem.me.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Phil Pem VUFORS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PhilPemVUFORS - The Frederick Valentich Disappearance...</span></span></span>

The radio exchange remains controversial because it contains both unusually specific observations and significant unanswered questions. It is a genuine aviation record, supported by an official transcript, yet it does not provide independent confirmation that another aircraft or unknown object was present. The importance of the encounter lies less in proving a particular explanation than in preserving the last known sequence of events before the disappearance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/2013/11/the-valentich-disappearance-another-ufo-cold-case-solved/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on skepticalinquirer.org.</span></span></span>

## Flight timeline and recorded conversation

Valentich’s final communication began at approximately 7:06 pm local time while he was flying VH-DSJ south of mainland Victoria towards King Island. He contacted Melbourne Flight Service to ask whether there was any known traffic in the area below 5,000 feet. The controller, Steve Robey, replied that there was no known traffic, after which Valentich reported seeing what appeared to be another aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/2013/11/the-valentich-disappearance-another-ufo-cold-case-solved/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on skepticalinquirer.org.</span></span></span>

The exchange developed over several minutes:

* **7:06:14 pm:** Valentich asked whether any traffic was known to be nearby. After being told there was none, he reported what seemed to be a large aircraft below his altitude.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pe2bz.philpem.me.uk/Comm/-%20ELF-VLF/-%20ELF-Theory/Th-112-Ufo-Misc-Odd/valensum.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pe2bz.philpem.me.uk">[PhilPem]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pe2bz.philpem.me.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Phil Pem VUFORS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PhilPemVUFORS - The Frederick Valentich Disappearance...</span></span></span>
* **7:06–7:07 pm:** When asked to identify the aircraft type, Valentich said he could not confirm what it was and described four bright lights resembling landing lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pe2bz.philpem.me.uk/Comm/-%20ELF-VLF/-%20ELF-Theory/Th-112-Ufo-Misc-Odd/valensum.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pe2bz.philpem.me.uk">[PhilPem]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pe2bz.philpem.me.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Phil Pem VUFORS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PhilPemVUFORS - The Frederick Valentich Disappearance...</span></span></span>
* **7:07 pm:** He reported that the object had passed over him at least 1,000 feet above. He also asked whether any Air Force aircraft were operating nearby, receiving the response that no known aircraft were in the vicinity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pe2bz.philpem.me.uk/Comm/-%20ELF-VLF/-%20ELF-Theory/Th-112-Ufo-Misc-Odd/valensum.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pe2bz.philpem.me.uk">[PhilPem]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pe2bz.philpem.me.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Phil Pem VUFORS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PhilPemVUFORS - The Frederick Valentich Disappearance...</span></span></span>
* **7:08–7:09 pm:** Valentich said the object was approaching again from the east and suggested that it appeared to be moving around him deliberately, describing behaviour he interpreted as unusual.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/2013/11/the-valentich-disappearance-another-ufo-cold-case-solved/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on skepticalinquirer.org.</span></span></span>
* **7:09 pm onward:** He stated that he could not identify the aircraft and later said it was not an aircraft. He continued describing its appearance before the transmission ended.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pe2bz.philpem.me.uk/Comm/-%20ELF-VLF/-%20ELF-Theory/Th-112-Ufo-Misc-Odd/valensum.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pe2bz.philpem.me.uk">[PhilPem]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pe2bz.philpem.me.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Phil Pem VUFORS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PhilPemVUFORS - The Frederick Valentich Disappearance...</span></span></span>

The final section of the recording is the part most often cited in discussions of the case. Valentich described the object as returning and hovering above him before the communication was interrupted. A period of unusual sound followed on the open microphone, after which no further communication from VH-DSJ was received.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/international/findings/au" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Transparency Australia findings · 01 Disclosures · UFO Transparency</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Transparency Australia findings · 01 Disclosures · UFO Transparency</span></span></span>

The timeline matters because it shows that the report was not a single sudden claim of an unknown object. Valentich’s description evolved during a live conversation: he first interpreted what he saw as a possible aircraft, then expressed uncertainty, and finally concluded that it did not appear to be a conventional aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pe2bz.philpem.me.uk/Comm/-%20ELF-VLF/-%20ELF-Theory/Th-112-Ufo-Misc-Odd/valensum.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pe2bz.philpem.me.uk">[PhilPem]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pe2bz.philpem.me.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Phil Pem VUFORS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PhilPemVUFORS - The Frederick Valentich Disappearance...</span></span></span>

## Valentich’s reported object description

The surviving transcript provides the main evidence for what Valentich believed he was observing. His description included several recurring details: bright lights, unusual movement, a metallic appearance, a green light, and a shape that he did not recognise as a normal aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://project1947.com/kbcat/kbvalent.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: project1947.com">[Project 1947]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">project1947.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project 1947PROJECT 1947 - A CATALOGUE OF UAP REPORTS FROM VICTORIA, BASS STRAIT AND TASMANIA AT THE TIME OF THE VALENTICH DISAPPEARANCE...</span></span></span>

According to the transcript preserved from the Department of Transport investigation material, Valentich described:

* a large object near his aircraft;
* several bright lights that resembled landing lights;
* movement that appeared to include passing over him, approaching, and returning;
* a “long shape” with a shiny or metallic exterior;
* a green light associated with the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://project1947.com/kbcat/kbvalent.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: project1947.com">[Project 1947]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">project1947.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project 1947PROJECT 1947 - A CATALOGUE OF UAP REPORTS FROM VICTORIA, BASS STRAIT AND TASMANIA AT THE TIME OF THE VALENTICH DISAPPEARANCE...</span></span></span>

One of the most important details is that Valentich initially used aviation language rather than immediately describing something extraordinary. He referred to the object as an “aircraft” during much of the exchange, asked whether military aircraft were nearby, and sought help identifying it. This has been interpreted in different ways: supporters of an unexplained encounter argue that the uncertainty reflects a pilot trying to describe an unfamiliar object, while sceptical analyses argue that uncertainty and misidentification are common possibilities in night flying.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/2013/11/the-valentich-disappearance-another-ufo-cold-case-solved/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on skepticalinquirer.org.</span></span></span>

The object description is therefore valuable but limited. It records what Valentich perceived, not what was objectively present. The transcript contains his observations, but it does not include radar confirmation of another aircraft, photographs from the flight itself, or recovered material evidence directly linked to the reported object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/2013/11/the-valentich-disappearance-another-ufo-cold-case-solved/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on skepticalinquirer.org.</span></span></span>

## What the transmission can and cannot prove

The radio recording is the strongest evidence in the Valentich disappearance, but its evidential value has clear boundaries. It proves that Valentich made a report, that flight service personnel responded, and that the conversation ended abruptly before the aircraft was recovered. It does not by itself establish the physical identity of the object he described or the cause of the disappearance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/2013/11/the-valentich-disappearance-another-ufo-cold-case-solved/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on skepticalinquirer.org.</span></span></span>

### What remains unresolved

The transmission cannot determine whether Valentich was observing:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* another aircraft that was not identified;
* lights or reflections misinterpreted during night flight;
* an unusual atmospheric or environmental effect;
* or an unknown phenomenon outside conventional explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/2013/11/the-valentich-disappearance-another-ufo-cold-case-solved/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on skepticalinquirer.org.</span></span></span>

</div>

A major difficulty for investigators is the lack of a second independent record of the object. The controller heard Valentich’s description but did not see the object himself, and no confirmed radar evidence demonstrated that another aircraft was present. The audio therefore functions as a record of a pilot’s experience, rather than a complete physical record of the encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/2013/11/the-valentich-disappearance-another-ufo-cold-case-solved/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on skepticalinquirer.org.</span></span></span>

The final radio call remains central to the Valentich mystery because it sits between two kinds of evidence: it is far stronger than a later witness story, yet far weaker than direct physical confirmation. Its lasting significance comes from that unusual position — a detailed, real-time aviation record documenting an unexplained report immediately before an aircraft vanished.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pe2bz.philpem.me.uk/Comm/-%20ELF-VLF/-%20ELF-Theory/Th-112-Ufo-Misc-Odd/valensum.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pe2bz.philpem.me.uk">[PhilPem]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pe2bz.philpem.me.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Phil Pem VUFORS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PhilPemVUFORS - The Frederick Valentich Disappearance...</span></span></span>
