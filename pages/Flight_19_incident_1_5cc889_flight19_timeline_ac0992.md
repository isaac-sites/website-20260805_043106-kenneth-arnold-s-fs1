## Introduction

Flight 19’s disappearance on 5 December 1945 unfolded over only a few hours, but its final mission remains one of the most studied aviation losses of the twentieth century. Five U.S. Navy Grumman TBM Avenger aircraft left Naval Air Station Fort Lauderdale for a routine navigation exercise and vanished after a sequence of position errors, uncertain radio calls, and failed attempts to find a route back to Florida. The surviving records do not show a sudden unexplained event; instead, they document a deteriorating situation in which a trained formation became increasingly unsure of its location over open water.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-057/h-057-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandH-057-4: The Disappearance of Flight 19January 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2021</span></span></span>

The importance of the final flight lies in the unusually detailed record left behind. Radio transmissions, Navy accident reports, and later investigations allow historians to reconstruct the broad timeline even though no confirmed wreckage from the Avengers was recovered. The leading official interpretation has been that navigational confusion, possible compass problems, weather conditions, and eventual fuel exhaustion caused the aircraft to be lost at sea.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-057/h-057-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandH-057-4: The Disappearance of Flight 19January 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2021</span></span></span>

## The Last Mission of Flight 19

### Departure and training route

Flight 19 began as Navigation Problem No. 1, a standard over-water training exercise intended to teach pilots how to navigate using headings, timing, estimated distance, and visual references. The five Avengers departed NAS Fort Lauderdale shortly after 2 p.m. under the command of Lieutenant Charles C. Taylor, an experienced naval aviator who had combat experience in the Pacific.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/browse-by-topic/disasters-and-phenomena/flight-19.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Naval History and Heritage Command The Loss of Flight 19</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage Command The Loss of Flight 19</span></span></span>

The planned route was a triangular course. The aircraft were to fly east from Florida, conduct a bombing exercise at the Hens and Chickens Shoals area of the Bahamas, turn north towards Grand Bahama Island, and then return west towards Fort Lauderdale. In normal conditions, the route was a familiar training pattern rather than an unusual or hazardous mission.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/browse-by-topic/disasters-and-phenomena/flight-19.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Naval History and Heritage Command The Loss of Flight 19</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage Command The Loss of Flight 19</span></span></span>

The first part of the flight appears to have proceeded normally. The aircraft reached the practice area and completed the bombing exercise. The crisis began after the formation moved into the return phase, when uncertainty developed over their exact position and the correct heading back to base.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/browse-by-topic/disasters-and-phenomena/flight-19.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Naval History and Heritage Command The Loss of Flight 19</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage Command The Loss of Flight 19</span></span></span>

### Radio calls and growing confusion

The first signs of trouble came through radio communication between Flight 19 and other aircraft or ground stations listening to the transmissions. Taylor reported that the group was uncertain of its location and that he was having difficulty determining the correct direction to Fort Lauderdale. The Navy’s later analysis identified this growing navigational confusion as a central factor in the loss.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-057/h-057-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandH-057-4: The Disappearance of Flight 19January 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2021</span></span></span>

A critical problem was that Taylor appears to have believed the formation was in a different geographical area from its actual position. He reportedly thought the aircraft were near the Florida Keys, suggesting that he had misidentified the Bahamas and believed the group had travelled much farther west than it had. This assumption influenced later decisions about which direction to fly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-057/h-057-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandH-057-4: The Disappearance of Flight 19January 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2021</span></span></span>

The situation was made more difficult by the limits of 1945 aviation technology. Pilots flying over the ocean depended heavily on magnetic compasses, calculations based on speed and time, weather observations, and recognising landmarks. A mistake in one part of the calculation could produce an increasingly large error as the aircraft continued flying.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-057/h-057-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandH-057-4: The Disappearance of Flight 19January 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2021</span></span></span>

The radio exchanges also reveal disagreement within the group. Some pilots appeared to believe that flying west would be the safest option, while Taylor continued attempting to determine the group’s position and direct its movement. Because Taylor was the flight leader, his judgement carried significant authority even as uncertainty increased.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-057/h-057-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandH-057-4: The Disappearance of Flight 19January 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2021</span></span></span>

Attempts were made from the ground and by other aircraft to assist the formation. However, communication difficulties limited the ability of controllers to obtain a precise position or provide effective guidance. The aircraft also did not successfully resolve the frequency and navigation problems that could have helped rescuers track them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-057/h-057-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandH-057-4: The Disappearance of Flight 19January 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2021</span></span></span>

## Final Hours Over the Atlantic

### The decision to continue flying

As daylight faded, Flight 19 faced a worsening situation: uncertain location, limited fuel, and deteriorating weather. The group’s options became increasingly narrow because returning to land required choosing the correct direction without a reliable position fix. The Navy’s reconstruction concluded that the aircraft likely continued over open water until fuel reserves were exhausted.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-057/h-057-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandH-057-4: The Disappearance of Flight 19January 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2021</span></span></span>

One of the most important moments in the final phase was the decision-making under uncertainty. The pilots were not simply flying blindly; they were attempting to solve a navigation problem with incomplete information. However, once the initial position estimate was wrong, later corrections may have moved the aircraft farther from safety.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-057/h-057-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandH-057-4: The Disappearance of Flight 19January 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2021</span></span></span>

The final radio transmissions became increasingly fragmented. Reports from the period indicate that Taylor and the other pilots recognised the seriousness of their situation and prepared for the possibility of ditching at sea. The last known communications suggested that the group was still attempting to determine its position when contact was lost.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/browse-by-topic/disasters-and-phenomena/flight-19.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Naval History and Heritage Command The Loss of Flight 19</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage Command The Loss of Flight 19</span></span></span>

### The disappearance and rescue failure

After Flight 19 failed to return, the Navy launched a major search operation. Rescue aircraft were dispatched, including PBM Mariner flying boats sent to search for the missing Avengers. One of those rescue aircraft also disappeared, resulting in the loss of an additional 13 crew members.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-057/h-057-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandH-057-4: The Disappearance of Flight 19January 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2021</span></span></span>

The search that followed was one of the largest maritime-air rescue efforts of its time. Navy and Coast Guard forces searched a vast area of the Atlantic, but no confirmed wreckage, life rafts, or survivors from Flight 19 were found. The absence of physical evidence became a major reason the incident later attracted speculation beyond the official investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/browse-by-topic/disasters-and-phenomena/flight-19.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Naval History and Heritage Command The Loss of Flight 19</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage Command The Loss of Flight 19</span></span></span>

The loss of both Flight 19 and the rescue aircraft created the impression of an extraordinary chain of disappearances. However, the two events were investigated separately: the Avengers’ loss was associated primarily with navigation and fuel issues, while the rescue Mariner’s disappearance has generally been linked by investigators to a possible in-flight fire or accident rather than the same cause.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.navy.mil/about-us/leadership/director/directors-corner/h-grams/h-gram-057/h-057-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.navy.mil">[Naval History and Heritage Command]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.navy.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Naval History and Heritage CommandH-057-4: The Disappearance of Flight 19January 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 7, 2021</span></span></span>

## What the Final Timeline Shows


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
