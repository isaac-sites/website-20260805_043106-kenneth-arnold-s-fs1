## Introduction

The Catholic Church’s investigation of the Fátima apparitions was a long process of collecting testimony, reviewing documents, and judging whether public devotion could be permitted. The decisive step came on 13 October 1930, when Bishop José Alves Correia da Silva of Leiria issued a pastoral letter declaring the reported visions at the Cova da Iria “worthy of belief” and authorising the cult of Our Lady of Fátima. This decision did not present the apparitions as a scientifically proven event; rather, it represented a religious judgement that the accounts had passed ecclesiastical examination and that devotion associated with them could proceed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.santuario-fatima.pt/pt/news/documentacao-critica-fatima-selecao-documentos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: santuario-fatima.pt">[Santuário de Fátima]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">santuario-fatima.pt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Santuário de Fátima Documentação Crítica de Fátima</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Santuário de FátimaDocumentação Crítica de Fátima - Seleção de documentos 1917-1930July 20, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 20, 2013</span></span></span>

The Fátima review process became a model example of how the Church handles claims of private revelation: it examined the credibility of witnesses, the consistency of accounts, theological content, and the spiritual effects of the devotion. The investigation also illustrates the limits of such recognition: the Church assessed whether the phenomenon was suitable for belief and worship, not whether every reported physical event could be experimentally verified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19780225_norme-apparizioni_en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vatican.va">[Vatican]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vatican.va</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Norms regarding the manner of proceedings in the discernment of presumed apparitions or revelationsFebruary 25, 1978...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 25, 1978</span></span></span>

## The Leiria inquiry and collected testimony

After the reported events of 1917, Church authorities did not immediately issue an approval. This caution was consistent with Catholic practice for alleged apparitions, where local bishops are expected to investigate before allowing official forms of devotion. In Fátima, the growing number of pilgrims and continuing public interest eventually led the Diocese of Leiria to establish a formal canonical inquiry in 1922.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://fatima.org/approval-by-the-bishop-1930/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.org">[Fatima Center]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">approval by the bishop 1930</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fatima CenterApproval by the Bishop (1930) &#124; The Fatima CenterFebruary 9, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 9, 2017</span></span></span>

The inquiry gathered a wide range of material rather than relying only on the accounts of the three children — Lúcia dos Santos and Francisco and Jacinta Marto. Investigators examined testimonies, written statements, press reports, official correspondence, and interrogations connected with the events. The later publication project *Documentação Crítica de Fátima* preserved many of these materials, including hundreds of documents from the period between 1917 and 1930. The collection includes letters, newspaper articles, official documents, witness statements, and interrogations, showing the documentary scale behind the Church’s review.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fatima.pt/pt/news/documentacao-critica-fatima-selecao-documentos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.pt">[Santuário de Fátima]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.pt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Santuário de Fátima Documentação Crítica de Fátima</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Santuário de FátimaDocumentação Crítica de Fátima - Seleção de documentos 1917-1930July 20, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 20, 2013</span></span></span>

A key element of the investigation was the reliability of the witnesses. Church officials considered factors such as whether the children’s accounts were consistent, whether they showed signs of deception, and whether [the messages]({{ 'the-messages/' | relative_url }}) attributed to the apparitions conflicted with Catholic teaching. These criteria reflected the wider approach used by the Church when examining alleged supernatural phenomena: the character of the witnesses, the content of the claimed revelation, and the fruits produced by the resulting devotion all formed part of the assessment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19780225_norme-apparizioni_en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vatican.va">[Vatican]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vatican.va</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Norms regarding the manner of proceedings in the discernment of presumed apparitions or revelationsFebruary 25, 1978...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 25, 1978</span></span></span>

The investigation also occurred in a difficult social environment. Portugal in the early twentieth century had experienced strong tensions between republican authorities and the Catholic Church, and Fátima developed from a local religious event into a major pilgrimage movement before official recognition was granted. The Church therefore faced two connected questions: whether the reported events were credible and whether the rapidly expanding devotion surrounding them could be accepted responsibly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fatima.pt/pt/news/documentacao-critica-fatima-chega-ao-fim-primeira-fase-edicao-documentos-relacionados-com-os-acontecimentos-fatima-evolucao-santuario-a-expansao-mensagem-em-portugal-no-mundo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.pt">[Santuário de Fátima]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.pt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Santuário de Fátima Documentação Crítica de Fátima</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Santuário de Fátima Documentação Crítica de Fátima</span></span></span>

## The 1930 recognition decision

The final judgement came through Bishop José Alves Correia da Silva’s pastoral letter of 13 October 1930. After considering the findings of the diocesan commission, he declared the visions reported by the children at the Cova da Iria between May and October 1917 “worthy of belief” and permitted official worship of Our Lady of Fátima.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.santuario-fatima.pt/pt/news/documentacao-critica-fatima-selecao-documentos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: santuario-fatima.pt">[Santuário de Fátima]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">santuario-fatima.pt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Santuário de Fátima Documentação Crítica de Fátima</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Santuário de FátimaDocumentação Crítica de Fátima - Seleção de documentos 1917-1930July 20, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 20, 2013</span></span></span>

This wording is important because Catholic recognition of an apparition is not the same as a scientific certification. The phrase “worthy of belief” indicates that Church authorities found sufficient grounds for accepting the devotion within Catholic life, while leaving room for the distinction between religious judgement and empirical proof. Catholics are generally not required to believe in private revelations in the same way they accept central doctrines of faith.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19780225_norme-apparizioni_en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vatican.va">[Vatican]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vatican.va</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Norms regarding the manner of proceedings in the discernment of presumed apparitions or revelationsFebruary 25, 1978...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 25, 1978</span></span></span>

The decision transformed Fátima from a disputed local phenomenon into an officially recognised centre of pilgrimage. The approval provided institutional support for the shrine and allowed public devotion to develop under Church supervision. The recognition also shaped later interpretations of Fátima, including discussions of its messages, the “secrets” associated with the children’s accounts, and the worldwide spread of Fátima devotion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fatima.pt/pt/news/documentacao-critica-fatima-chega-ao-fim-primeira-fase-edicao-documentos-relacionados-com-os-acontecimentos-fatima-evolucao-santuario-a-expansao-mensagem-em-portugal-no-mundo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.pt">[Santuário de Fátima]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.pt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Santuário de Fátima Documentação Crítica de Fátima</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Santuário de Fátima Documentação Crítica de Fátima</span></span></span>

## Belief, investigation, and scientific limits

The Church’s method at Fátima was neither a simple acceptance of eyewitness claims nor a scientific experiment designed to reproduce supernatural events. Instead, it was a theological and historical investigation focused on credibility, consistency, and religious consequences. This distinction explains why the 1930 decision remains significant even for debates about the evidence: it was an institutional judgement about faith and devotion rather than a laboratory conclusion about the cause of the reported phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19780225_norme-apparizioni_en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vatican.va">[Vatican]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vatican.va</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Norms regarding the manner of proceedings in the discernment of presumed apparitions or revelationsFebruary 25, 1978...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 25, 1978</span></span></span>

The strongest documentary foundation for the Church’s decision lies in testimony and records surrounding the events. These include statements from the visionaries and other witnesses, contemporary reporting, and administrative documents collected during the inquiry. Supporters view the scale and continuity of this record as evidence that the events deserved serious consideration. Critics, however, note that testimony-based evidence has inherent limitations because human memory, expectation, and interpretation can influence how extraordinary experiences are reported and preserved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fatima.pt/pt/news/documentacao-critica-fatima-selecao-documentos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fatima.pt">[Santuário de Fátima]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fatima.pt</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Santuário de Fátima Documentação Crítica de Fátima</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Santuário de FátimaDocumentação Crítica de Fátima - Seleção de documentos 1917-1930July 20, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 20, 2013</span></span></span>

The Fátima process also shows how the Church balances openness and caution. Modern Vatican guidelines for alleged supernatural phenomena continue to emphasise investigation of facts, witness credibility, theological consistency, and spiritual outcomes rather than relying only on claims of extraordinary events. The updated norms issued in 2024 further clarified that Church evaluations must distinguish between recognising the value of a devotion and making definitive claims about supernatural origins.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2024/05/17/240517h.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: press.vatican.va">[Vatican Press]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">press.vatican.va</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Vatican PressNorms of the Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith for proceeding in the discernment of alleged supernatural phenomenaMay...</span></span></span>

    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
