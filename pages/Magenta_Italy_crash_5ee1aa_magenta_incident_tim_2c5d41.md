## Introduction

The alleged 1933 Magenta crash is the foundational event behind claims that Fascist Italy recovered an unknown aircraft-like object years before the better-known Roswell case. According to later UFO literature, an unusual object came down near Magenta in Lombardy, was recovered by Italian authorities, and was placed under secret investigation by the Mussolini government. However, the historical record is unusual: the detailed crash narrative does not come from contemporary newspapers, military reports, or publicly verified state archives, but mainly from a set of documents that surfaced decades later and whose authenticity and provenance remain disputed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[newparadigminstitute.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>

## Reported discovery near Magenta

The commonly repeated account places the incident in 1933, with versions differing over the exact date. Some researchers associated with the “Fascist UFO Files” have identified the event with 11 April 1933, while other retellings place the alleged recovery around 13 June 1933. This inconsistency is one of the first challenges in reconstructing the event, because there is no publicly accepted contemporary incident report that fixes the date, location, and circumstances.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/amp/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[theblackvault.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">TH E UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case FilesAugust 10, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 10, 2020</span></span></span>

According to the later narrative, Italian authorities recovered a strange metallic object in the Magenta area, west of Milan. Descriptions vary, but UFO researchers have generally portrayed it as a craft unlike known aircraft of the period, sometimes described as disc-shaped or otherwise unconventional. The alleged object was reportedly treated initially as a possible foreign technological development before becoming the subject of a secret investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.coloradomufon.org/the-magenta-case-italys-1933-ufo-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: coloradomufon.org">[coloradomufon.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">coloradomufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the magenta case italys 1933 ufo mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Magenta Case: First Reported UFO Crash – Colorado MUFONMay 5, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 5, 2026</span></span></span>

The main source for this sequence is the collection of papers later known as the “Fascist UFO Files” or “RS/33 Cabinet papers”. Italian UFO researchers Roberto Pinotti and Alfredo Lissoni said they received these materials anonymously during the 1990s from a source identified as “Mr X”. The documents were presented as Fascist-era material connected to investigations of unusual aerial phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[newparadigminstitute.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>

Supporters of the case argue that the documents contain details consistent with a government response to an unusual event, including references to secrecy, technical examination, and specialised research. Critics argue that the chain of custody is unclear because the papers emerged from an anonymous source rather than from a traceable government archive, making it difficult to establish whether they record a genuine incident or a later-created narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cisu.org/fascist-ufo-files-a-professional-archivists-opinion/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cisu.org">[CISU]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cisu.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fascist UFO Files: a Professional Archivist&#x27;s Opinion</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fascist UFO Files: a Professional Archivist&#x27;s Opinion - CISU - Centro Italiano Studi Ufologici...</span></span></span>

## Government secrecy claims and transport

The later Magenta story expands beyond a simple recovery claim. It alleges that Mussolini’s government attempted to suppress information about the object and organised a formal investigation through a body referred to as Gabinetto RS/33. In this account, the recovered material was moved for examination to facilities connected with Italian aviation research, particularly the SIAI-Marchetti aircraft company at Vergiate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/es/cases/rs33-magenta-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Gabinete RS/33 · Magenta · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Gabinete RS/33 · Magenta · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

The alleged RS/33 group has become one of the most debated parts of the story. Advocates interpret references to “RS/33” in the documents as evidence of a secret state programme studying unexplained aerial technology. Skeptical researchers have questioned whether the documents demonstrate the existence of such an organisation in the form described by modern UFO accounts, noting that archival verification remains incomplete.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cisu.org/fascist-ufo-files-a-professional-archivists-opinion/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cisu.org">[CISU]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cisu.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fascist UFO Files: a Professional Archivist&#x27;s Opinion</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fascist UFO Files: a Professional Archivist&#x27;s Opinion - CISU - Centro Italiano Studi Ufologici...</span></span></span>

A key distinction is between evidence that documents existed and evidence that the documents prove the event occurred. Some of the papers promoted by researchers appear to be physically consistent with older documents, but document age alone does not confirm that their contents are accurate. An authentic 1930s document could still contain a misunderstanding, an unrelated reference, or a later misinterpretation of events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/es/cases/rs33-magenta-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Gabinete RS/33 · Magenta · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Gabinete RS/33 · Magenta · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

The alleged transport of the object also illustrates the difference between early claims and later additions. The basic story concerns a recovery and investigation in Fascist Italy. Later versions add claims about wartime transfers, German awareness, and eventual acquisition by the United States, but these developments rely on additional layers of testimony and interpretation rather than independently verified records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[newparadigminstitute.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>

## Later additions to the story

Over time, the Magenta narrative expanded from an alleged Italian recovery into a broader crash-retrieval story. Later accounts connected the object to secret programmes, wartime intelligence operations, and post-war transfers. These elements helped make the case significant within UFO literature, especially after renewed public attention around modern UAP discussions, but they are less directly tied to the original 1933 claim.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[newparadigminstitute.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>

The weakest point remains the absence of publicly available contemporaneous Italian records independently confirming that a crashed unknown craft was recovered and stored by the Fascist government.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cisu.org/fascist-ufo-files-a-professional-archivists-opinion/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cisu.org">[CISU]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cisu.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fascist UFO Files: a Professional Archivist&#x27;s Opinion</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fascist UFO Files: a Professional Archivist&#x27;s Opinion - CISU - Centro Italiano Studi Ufologici...</span></span></span>
