## Introduction

The Rex Heflin encounter of 3 August 1965 is the personal event behind one of the most discussed UFO photographic cases of the twentieth century. According to Heflin’s account, he was working as an Orange County highway inspector near Myford Road, Santa Ana, California, when he saw a metallic, hat-shaped object hovering near his vehicle. He used a Polaroid camera kept in his work truck to document road conditions and took a rapid sequence of photographs that he said captured the object and a final image of a dark ring-like trace after it departed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/heflinhall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportAugust 3, 1965...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 1965</span></span></span>

The importance of the encounter timeline is that the photographs were not presented as isolated images. Heflin described a sequence involving an unexpected sighting, attempts to use his vehicle radio, several camera exposures, and later interactions with investigators. ## The August 1965 sighting timeline

### A roadside observation during routine work

On the afternoon of 3 August 1965, Rex Heflin was carrying out his normal duties as a highway inspector in the Santa Ana area. His work involved inspecting roads and documenting conditions, which explains why he had a Polaroid camera available in his vehicle rather than needing to retrieve a camera specifically for the sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/heflinhall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportAugust 3, 1965...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 1965</span></span></span>

Heflin reported that while stopped on Myford Road he noticed an unusual flash and then saw a disc-shaped object with a raised upper section, which he described as resembling a hat or dome-topped craft. He estimated that the object was relatively close, hovering above the roadway. Accounts based on his testimony describe the object as metallic in appearance, with a darker central area and a rotating light-like feature underneath.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/heflinhall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportAugust 3, 1965...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 1965</span></span></span>

The location mattered to later investigators because the roadside environment provided fixed references such as trees, utility lines, and the road itself. These details allowed later analysts to examine questions of distance, scale, and whether the photographed object behaved like a suspended model, a thrown object, or something moving independently.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/rex-heflin-1965/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Rex Heflin · Santa Ana · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Rex Heflin · Santa Ana · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

### The camera sequence

Heflin’s account centred on four photographs. He said he first photographed the object while it was near his truck, then continued photographing as it moved away. The first three images were said to show the object, while the fourth captured what he interpreted as a smoke-like ring remaining after the object’s departure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://searchufos.com/cases/rex-heflin-ufo-photos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: searchufos.com">[SearchUFOs]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">searchufos.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Search UFOs Rex Heflin UFO Photos — UFO Case File</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Search UFOs Rex Heflin UFO Photos — UFO Case File</span></span></span>

The sequence became a key feature of the case because it created a visual timeline:

For supporters of Heflin’s account, this progression suggested an unfolding event rather than a single accidental image. For sceptical investigators, however, a sequence of photographs could also be produced through other means, including a carefully arranged object photographed from different positions. The disagreement has never centred only on whether an object appears in the images, but on what process produced the sequence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoexplorations.com/ufos-that-never-were-classic-photos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoexplorations.com">[ufoexplorations]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoexplorations.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Explorations &#124; The UFOs That Never Were: Classic Photos Exposed</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Explorations &#124; The UFOs That Never Were: Classic Photos Exposed...</span></span></span>

## Heflin’s testimony and camera sequence

### The reported radio failure

One of the most frequently discussed details of Heflin’s testimony was his claim that his vehicle radio stopped working while the object was nearby. He said he attempted to contact his office during the encounter but could not transmit, and that the radio returned to normal after the object departed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/heflinhall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportAugust 3, 1965...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 1965</span></span></span>

This detail increased interest among investigators because temporary equipment failures were sometimes reported in UFO cases of the period. However, the radio claim remained a supporting part of Heflin’s story rather than independent physical evidence. The photographic record and the witness testimony remained the central elements of the investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/heflinhall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportAugust 3, 1965...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 1965</span></span></span>

### How Heflin described the object’s movement

Heflin’s description was unusually specific for a brief roadside encounter. He reported that the object hovered, moved across his field of view, tilted or changed position, and then accelerated away. He also described a rotating feature underneath the object, which he compared to a sweeping light.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/heflinhall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportAugust 3, 1965...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 1965</span></span></span>

These details shaped later photographic interpretations. Some analysts attempted to match the apparent movement in the photographs with Heflin’s description, while others argued that apparent changes in position could result from ordinary photographic effects or a nearby object moving relative to the camera.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/rex-heflin-1965/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Rex Heflin · Santa Ana · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Rex Heflin · Santa Ana · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

The encounter therefore became a test of how much weight should be given to a witness narrative when the physical evidence is unusual but incomplete. Heflin’s account provided a chronological framework, but the photographs themselves remained open to competing interpretations.

## Questions about witness reliability

### Why investigators took Heflin seriously

Heflin’s background was an important factor in early investigations. He was a government employee performing routine highway work rather than someone publicly seeking attention through UFO claims. Civilian investigators, including members of the Los Angeles branch of the National Investigations Committee on Aerial Phenomena (NICAP), interviewed him, examined his background, and investigated the circumstances surrounding the photographs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/heflinhall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportAugust 3, 1965...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 1965</span></span></span>

Supporters of the case argued that Heflin’s occupation, the ordinary circumstances of the sighting, and his willingness to cooperate with investigators strengthened his credibility. The fact that he had a camera available for work purposes also made the event appear less like a planned photographic demonstration.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/heflinhall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportAugust 3, 1965...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 1965</span></span></span>

However, witness credibility does not by itself establish what was photographed. A reliable witness can accurately report what they believed they saw while still being mistaken about the identity or nature of an object. This distinction became central to later debate.

### The disputed handling of the photographs

The credibility question became more complicated after the photographs attracted attention. Heflin later reported that men claiming to represent NORAD obtained the original photographs for examination and did not return them for many years. NORAD and the Air Force denied that an authorised representative had taken the photographs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/heflin65.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: The Rex Heflin photographs case, Santa Ana, Ca., USA, 1965...</span></span></span>

The missing originals affected later evaluation because photographic analysis is strongest when researchers can examine first-generation material and a clear chain of custody. ### The lasting dispute over Heflin’s account

The encounter remains controversial because the witness account contains both strengths and limitations. On one side, Heflin described a specific event in a consistent sequence, produced multiple photographs, and was investigated by several groups. On the other, there was no independent witness who confirmed seeing the object itself, and later analysts disagreed about whether the photographs required an extraordinary explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/heflinhall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportAugust 3, 1965...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 1965</span></span></span>

The Heflin encounter is therefore best understood as a documented historical report with a strong personal narrative but unresolved interpretation. The timeline explains why the photographs became influential: they were tied to a real person, a real workplace setting, and a claimed chain of events rather than appearing as unexplained images without context. The central question remains not whether Rex Heflin reported an unusual roadside encounter — he did — but what the photographs and circumstances ultimately reveal about what he experienced.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/heflinhall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO ReportAugust 3, 1965...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 3, 1965</span></span></span>

    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
