## What happened at Dechmont Woods in 1979?

Robert Taylor was a forester employed by the Livingston Development Corporation, working in the newly developing Livingston area. On the morning of 9 November 1979, he drove near Dechmont Law to inspect forestry work. According to his account, he left his vehicle and walked through woodland with his dog before entering a clearing, where he saw a large, dark grey, dome-like object hovering close to the ground.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.undiscoveredscotland.co.uk/livingston/livingstonincident/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: undiscoveredscotland.co.uk">[Undiscovered Scotland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">undiscoveredscotland.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on undiscoveredscotland.co.uk.</span></span></span>

Taylor described the object as roughly 20 feet (about six metres) across, with a flange around its edge and protruding structures that he compared to propellers. He said two smaller spherical objects approached him from the craft. These objects allegedly attached themselves to his trousers with spike-like projections and dragged him towards the larger object. Taylor then remembered little until he found himself lying in the clearing, after which he returned home in a distressed state.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://history.scot/the-livingston-ufo-incident/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.scot">[History Scot]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.scot</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on history.scot.</span></span></span>

When Taylor arrived home, his wife noticed that he was injured and that his clothing had been torn. Police were contacted, and the matter was investigated as a possible assault rather than simply recorded as a UFO report. This unusual legal framing contributed to the case’s reputation as one of the few UFO incidents in Britain to receive a conventional criminal investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Taylor_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Robert Taylor incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Robert Taylor incident</span></span></span>

## Why did the case attract serious attention?

### The witness and his background

Supporters of the case often emphasise Taylor’s background. He was an older forestry worker with no established public interest in UFOs before the incident, and early investigators described him as a reliable and straightforward witness. His supporters argue that his ordinary occupation and lack of obvious motive to seek publicity make deliberate fabrication less likely.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/bufora-1979-1989-journal-of-transient-aerial-phenomena-jtap-vol-1-no-2-mar-1980/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

Taylor maintained his account for the rest of his life, including after the incident became widely discussed. His consistency is frequently cited by UFO researchers as an important factor in the case’s credibility. However, consistency alone cannot establish that an unusual perception or event had an extraordinary cause; a sincere witness can still misunderstand an event or remember details inaccurately.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Taylor_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Robert Taylor incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Robert Taylor incident</span></span></span>

### The reported physical evidence

The strongest part of the case for many investigators was the presence of physical evidence. ## How was the investigation interpreted?

Police treated the incident as a possible assault because Taylor returned with injuries and damaged clothing. Investigators collected statements and examined the location, but they did not reach a publicly confirmed explanation. The official record therefore sits in an unusual position: the event was treated seriously as a real incident involving a person, while the cause remained unidentified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://history.scot/the-livingston-ufo-incident/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.scot">[History Scot]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.scot</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on history.scot.</span></span></span>

UFO researchers have argued that the case is significant because it contains several elements rarely found together: a named witness, a detailed close encounter report, physical traces, and contemporary investigation. Some have ranked it among the more compelling British UFO cases because the witness was not a professional UFO claimant and because the incident involved alleged physical interaction rather than only a distant sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://alienresearch.cn/top-100-ufo-cases/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: alienresearch.cn">[ALIENRESEARCH 地外文明研究]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">alienresearch.cn</span><span class="citation-popover-title">地外文明研究TOP 100 UFO CASEs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ALIENRESEARCH 地外文明研究TOP 100 UFO CASEs - ALIENRESEARCHJuly 14, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 14, 2022</span></span></span>

Sceptical analyses focus on the gap between an unexplained event and an extraterrestrial explanation. They note that the investigation established injuries and unusual circumstances, but did not establish what caused them. Alternative possibilities discussed by critics include misidentification, an accident involving an ordinary object, a medical episode affecting perception or memory, or an unknown but non-extraterrestrial event. None of these explanations has achieved universal acceptance among commentators.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Taylor_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Robert Taylor incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Robert Taylor incident</span></span></span>

## The main explanations for the Livingston incident

The extraterrestrial interpretation remains the most famous explanation because Taylor described an object with apparently engineered features and a physical encounter. For believers, the combination of his testimony and the site evidence suggests that the event involved something beyond conventional technology. The case is particularly influential in UFO literature because it presents a rare “close encounter” narrative involving alleged physical effects on a witness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.undiscoveredscotland.co.uk/livingston/livingstonincident/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: undiscoveredscotland.co.uk">[Undiscovered Scotland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">undiscoveredscotland.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on undiscoveredscotland.co.uk.</span></span></span>

A more cautious interpretation is that the incident represents an unidentified event rather than evidence of alien visitation. Under this view, the important facts are that Taylor experienced something distressing, his injuries and damaged clothing were real, and investigators could not determine the cause. The absence of a confirmed explanation does not automatically favour the most extraordinary hypothesis.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Taylor_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Robert Taylor incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Robert Taylor incident</span></span></span>

Other proposed explanations have included conventional machinery, wildlife-related events, environmental effects, or a misunderstanding of a natural occurrence. The enduring debate around the Livingston incident comes from a tension between two facts.     return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
