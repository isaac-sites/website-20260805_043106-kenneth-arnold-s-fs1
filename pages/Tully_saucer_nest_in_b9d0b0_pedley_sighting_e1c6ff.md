## Introduction

George Pedley’s sighting on 19 January 1966 became the defining eyewitness account behind the Tully “saucer nest” incident. A 28-year-old banana grower near Tully, Queensland, Pedley reported seeing a grey, disc-shaped object rise from the vicinity of Horseshoe Lagoon before accelerating away. His account gained lasting attention because it was followed by the discovery of an unusual circular disturbance in the lagoon reeds, creating the combination of witness testimony and physical trace evidence that made the case internationally known.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uforq.org/the-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uforq.org">[UFO Research Queensland - Australia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uforq.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Research Queensland</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AustraliaThe Tully Saucer Nest 1966 – UFO Research Queensland – AustraliaOctober 7, 2023</span></span></span>

Pedley’s report is important because it is unusually specific for a single-witness UFO case: he described the object’s shape, apparent size, movement, sound, and direction of travel, then promptly brought others to the location. At the same time, his testimony remains the main human source for the sighting itself, meaning the interpretation of the event depends heavily on how his reliability, memory, and later statements are assessed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thinkaboutitdocs.com/the-1966-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thinkaboutitdocs.com">[Think About It Docs]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thinkaboutitdocs.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the 1966 tully saucer nest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Think About It Docs1966: The Tully Saucer NestApril 8, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 8, 2013</span></span></span>

## The morning sighting near Horseshoe Lagoon

On the morning of 19 January 1966, Pedley was driving a tractor along a farm track on land owned by Albert Pennisi near Euramo, close to Tully in northern Queensland. As he approached Horseshoe Lagoon at about 9:00 am, he reported hearing a loud hissing sound above the noise of the tractor. At first, he thought the sound might be caused by a leaking tyre or a problem with the machinery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uforq.org/the-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uforq.org">[UFO Research Queensland - Australia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uforq.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Research Queensland</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AustraliaThe Tully Saucer Nest 1966 – UFO Research Queensland – AustraliaOctober 7, 2023</span></span></span>

According to the account later recorded in UFO literature and linked to the contemporary investigation record, Pedley then looked towards the lagoon and saw what he described as a large grey or silver-grey object rising from the marsh area. He estimated it was about 25 feet (around 7.5 metres) across and several feet thick, with a shape resembling “two saucers, face to face”. He reported that the object climbed vertically, tilted slightly, and then departed rapidly towards the south-west.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/blog/truth-out-there-queensland-ufo-related-periodicals" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: slq.qld.gov.au">[State Library of Queensland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">slq.qld.gov.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">State Library of Queensland The Truth Is Out There</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">State Library of QueenslandThe Truth Is Out There - Queensland UFO related periodicals &#124; State Library of QueenslandJuly 2, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2015</span></span></span>

The sighting itself was extremely brief. Later descriptions vary slightly, but the observation was generally measured in seconds rather than minutes. This short duration became one of the central issues in later assessments: supporters argued that Pedley’s immediate reaction and consistent description supported a genuine observation, while sceptics noted that a fleeting view of an unusual object provides limited opportunity for accurate identification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thinkaboutitdocs.com/the-1966-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thinkaboutitdocs.com">[Think About It Docs]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thinkaboutitdocs.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the 1966 tully saucer nest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Think About It Docs1966: The Tully Saucer NestApril 8, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 8, 2013</span></span></span>

### What Pedley said he saw

Pedley’s description focused on movement and general appearance rather than fine details. He did not report windows, occupants, markings, or visible technology. In later recollections, he emphasised that he had seen an object rising and moving away, rather than claiming certainty about its nature. This distinction became significant because early newspaper coverage often framed the event as a “spaceship” encounter, while Pedley himself was more cautious about what he could actually identify.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thinkaboutitdocs.com/the-1966-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thinkaboutitdocs.com">[Think About It Docs]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thinkaboutitdocs.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the 1966 tully saucer nest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Think About It Docs1966: The Tully Saucer NestApril 8, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 8, 2013</span></span></span>

The reported features of the object were:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* A dull grey or grey-blue appearance rather than a highly reflective metallic surface.
* A disc or saucer-like form.
* An estimated width of about 25 feet.
* A vertical rise followed by rapid departure.
* A hissing sound that appeared to diminish as the object moved away.
* No visible occupants or obvious external structures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uforq.org/the-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uforq.org">[UFO Research Queensland - Australia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uforq.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Research Queensland</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AustraliaThe Tully Saucer Nest 1966 – UFO Research Queensland – AustraliaOctober 7, 2023</span></span></span>

These details gave investigators a structured account to examine, but they did not by themselves establish what the object was. The description could document what Pedley experienced without resolving whether the cause was an aircraft, atmospheric phenomenon, misidentification, or something unexplained.

## Pedley’s description and later testimony

A key feature of Pedley’s account is that his wording changed in emphasis over time. The earliest reports presented him as having seen a “saucer” or “spaceship”, but later interviews showed a more careful interpretation. In a later discussion with UFO researcher Bill Chalker, Pedley reportedly objected to the stronger wording, explaining that he had seen “an object” rather than knowing it was a spaceship.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thinkaboutitdocs.com/the-1966-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thinkaboutitdocs.com">[Think About It Docs]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thinkaboutitdocs.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the 1966 tully saucer nest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Think About It Docs1966: The Tully Saucer NestApril 8, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 8, 2013</span></span></span>

This later caution is significant when evaluating witness credibility. Rather than repeatedly expanding the claim, Pedley appears to have narrowed it: he maintained that he had seen something unusual, but separated that observation from assumptions about its origin.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thinkaboutitdocs.com/the-1966-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thinkaboutitdocs.com">[Think About It Docs]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thinkaboutitdocs.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the 1966 tully saucer nest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Think About It Docs1966: The Tully Saucer NestApril 8, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 8, 2013</span></span></span>

The circumstances surrounding his report also affected how investigators viewed him. Pedley did not initially appear to be seeking publicity; accounts state that he reported the incident to Tully Police later that day, after discussing it with others, and then accompanied police to the site the following morning. The police and Royal Australian Air Force (RAAF) investigation records became part of the official documentation of the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thinkaboutitdocs.com/the-1966-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thinkaboutitdocs.com">[Think About It Docs]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thinkaboutitdocs.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the 1966 tully saucer nest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Think About It Docs1966: The Tully Saucer NestApril 8, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 8, 2013</span></span></span>

Sergeant A. V. Moylan of Tully Police inspected the location with Pedley on 20 January and contacted Townsville RAAF Base. The RAAF requested further information through its UFO reporting procedures and sought samples from the disturbed vegetation. This created an unusually detailed paper trail compared with many contemporary UFO reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thinkaboutitdocs.com/the-1966-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thinkaboutitdocs.com">[Think About It Docs]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thinkaboutitdocs.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the 1966 tully saucer nest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Think About It Docs1966: The Tully Saucer NestApril 8, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 8, 2013</span></span></span>

## The witness account and the “saucer nest” evidence

Pedley’s sighting became famous because he linked the object’s departure point with an unusual formation in the lagoon. After the object disappeared, he went to investigate and reported finding a circular area of flattened reeds. Later accounts described the feature as a depression or “nest” in the floating vegetation, with the reeds arranged in a radial pattern.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uforq.org/the-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uforq.org">[UFO Research Queensland - Australia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uforq.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Research Queensland</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AustraliaThe Tully Saucer Nest 1966 – UFO Research Queensland – AustraliaOctober 7, 2023</span></span></span>

The relationship between the sighting and the physical mark is central to Pedley’s account. He believed the two events were connected: the object he saw had risen from the lagoon and left the disturbance behind. However, the physical trace did not independently prove the cause of the event. Investigators considered conventional explanations, including weather-related effects and mechanical possibilities, while UFO researchers argued that the unusual shape of the disturbance supported Pedley’s interpretation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/blog/truth-out-there-queensland-ufo-related-periodicals" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: slq.qld.gov.au">[State Library of Queensland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">slq.qld.gov.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">State Library of Queensland The Truth Is Out There</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">State Library of QueenslandThe Truth Is Out There - Queensland UFO related periodicals &#124; State Library of QueenslandJuly 2, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2015</span></span></span>

The strongest point in favour of Pedley’s testimony is therefore not that it proves an extraordinary explanation, but that it combines a detailed observation with a contemporaneous report of a physical feature at the same location. The strongest limitation is that the sighting itself remained a single-person observation, with no other confirmed witness seeing the object in flight.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/tully-saucer-nests-1966/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Tully Saucer Nests · RAAF investigation · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Tully Saucer Nests · RAAF investigation · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

## Limits of single-witness evidence

Pedley’s account occupies an unusual position in UFO investigations: it is neither a simple unsupported story nor a verified identification of an unknown craft. The report was made by a named witness, investigated by authorities, and connected to a documented physical site. These factors distinguish it from many anecdotal sightings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thinkaboutitdocs.com/the-1966-tully-saucer-nest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thinkaboutitdocs.com">[Think About It Docs]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thinkaboutitdocs.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the 1966 tully saucer nest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Think About It Docs1966: The Tully Saucer NestApril 8, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 8, 2013</span></span></span>

However, a single eyewitness account has inherent limits. Human perception is affected by viewing time, distance, lighting, expectation, and the difficulty of judging size and speed without reference points. In Pedley’s case, the object was seen for only a short period, and the observer was attempting to interpret an unfamiliar event while operating farm machinery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapcodex.org/cases/tully-saucer-nests-1966/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapcodex.org">[UAP Codex]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapcodex.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UAP Codex Tully Saucer Nests · RAAF investigation · UAP Codex</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Codex Tully Saucer Nests · RAAF investigation · UAP Codex</span></span></span>

The later history of the case illustrates this tension. Supporters of Pedley’s account point to his consistency, his reputation among locals, and the existence of the lagoon disturbance. Sceptical interpretations focus on the absence of independent observers of the object, the possibility of misidentification, and uncertainty over how the physical trace was produced.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/blog/truth-out-there-queensland-ufo-related-periodicals" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: slq.qld.gov.au">[State Library of Queensland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">slq.qld.gov.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">State Library of Queensland The Truth Is Out There</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">State Library of QueenslandThe Truth Is Out There - Queensland UFO related periodicals &#124; State Library of QueenslandJuly 2, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2015</span></span></span>

George Pedley’s importance in the Tully case therefore rests on a narrower but enduring point: he was the person whose observation transformed an unusual patch of disturbed lagoon vegetation into one of Australia’s most debated UFO incidents. His testimony remains the foundation of the case, while the question of what he actually saw remains unresolved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-12-16/reporting-on-taboo-topics-of-ufos-and-crop-circles/104711618" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.net.au">[ABC News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.net.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ABC News Reporting on the taboo topics of UFOs and crop circles</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC NewsReporting on the taboo topics of UFOs and crop circles - ABC News...</span></span></span>
