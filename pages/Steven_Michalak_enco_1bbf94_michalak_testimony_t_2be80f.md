## Introduction

Stefan (often recorded as Steven) Michalak’s account of the Falcon Lake encounter centres on a short sequence of events on 20 May 1967: a prospecting trip near Falcon Lake, Manitoba; the sighting of two unusual objects; his approach to one that appeared to land; and a reported blast that left him injured. The importance of the testimony lies in the combination of a detailed first-person narrative and a later investigation involving police, military officials, medical examinations, and site inspections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://library-archives.canada.ca/eng/collection/engage-learn/podcast/Pages/ufo-falcon-lake-incident.aspx?wbdisable=true" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: library-archives.canada.ca">[Library and Archives Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">library-archives.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Library and Archives Canada UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Library and Archives CanadaUFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident - Part 1May 15, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 15, 2019</span></span></span>

Michalak’s story has remained controversial because the core event is clear — he was in the wilderness, he reported seeing something unusual, and he returned with documented injuries — while the interpretation of what caused those injuries and what he encountered remains disputed. ## The reported sighting on 20 May 1967

Michalak was an industrial mechanic and amateur prospector who regularly searched the Falcon Lake area for minerals. According to his account, he began the day prospecting for quartz and silver deposits in the bush near Falcon Lake in Manitoba’s Whiteshell Provincial Park. After working through the morning, he was examining a quartz vein when nearby geese suddenly became agitated, drawing his attention upward. He reported seeing two glowing objects descending or moving in the area.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://library-archives.canada.ca/eng/collection/engage-learn/podcast/Pages/ufo-falcon-lake-incident.aspx?wbdisable=true" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: library-archives.canada.ca">[Library and Archives Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">library-archives.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Library and Archives Canada UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Library and Archives CanadaUFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident - Part 1May 15, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 15, 2019</span></span></span>

In Michalak’s description, one object remained airborne while the other descended and appeared to land nearby. He initially considered the possibility that the object might be a conventional aircraft or experimental technology rather than immediately identifying it as something extraterrestrial. His later descriptions portrayed the object as metallic, changing in appearance from a glowing form into a more solid-looking craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://library-archives.canada.ca/eng/collection/engage-learn/podcast/Pages/ufo-falcon-lake-incident.aspx?wbdisable=true" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: library-archives.canada.ca">[Library and Archives Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">library-archives.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Library and Archives Canada UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Library and Archives CanadaUFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident - Part 1May 15, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 15, 2019</span></span></span>

The timing of the encounter became an important part of the case. Michalak said the sighting occurred during daylight, in an area where he was familiar with the landscape. Unlike many UFO reports based on distant observations, his claim involved a close approach over a period of time, allowing him to describe details of the object’s shape, surface, and apparent movements.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://umtoday-wordpress.ad.umanitoba.ca/the-falcon-lake-ufo-files/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: umtoday-wordpress.ad.umanitoba.ca">[UM Today]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">umtoday-wordpress.ad.umanitoba.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UM TodayUM Today &#124; Libraries &#124; The Falcon Lake UFO FilesOctober 31, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 31, 2019</span></span></span>

### The first moments of contact

Michalak said he spent time observing the landed object before approaching it. In his account, he watched the object closely, made sketches, and attempted to understand what he was seeing. He reported that the surface appeared smooth and without obvious markings or seams. As he moved closer, he described an opening in the side of the object and bright light coming from within.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.experiencersupport.org/declassified/dnd-ufo-report-summary-regarding-ufo-at-falcon-beach-manitoba-may" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: experiencersupport.org">[Experiencer Support]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">experiencersupport.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Experiencer Support DND UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Experiencer SupportDND UFO Report Summary: Regarding UFO at Falcon Beach, Manitoba – May 20 1967 &#124; The Experiencer Support Association...</span></span></span>

According to his testimony, he attempted to communicate with whoever might be inside, using languages he knew, but received no clear response. He later said he heard sounds that he interpreted as voices or activity inside the object, although he did not see any occupants. The Canadian Department of National Defence summary of the report recorded that Michalak claimed an opening appeared, that he tried to communicate, and that the opening later closed as the object began to move.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.experiencersupport.org/declassified/dnd-ufo-report-summary-regarding-ufo-at-falcon-beach-manitoba-may" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: experiencersupport.org">[Experiencer Support]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">experiencersupport.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Experiencer Support DND UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Experiencer SupportDND UFO Report Summary: Regarding UFO at Falcon Beach, Manitoba – May 20 1967 &#124; The Experiencer Support Association...</span></span></span>

This part of the testimony became one of the most debated elements of the case. Supporters argue that the consistency of the basic sequence — approach, observation of an opening, attempted communication, and sudden departure — makes the report distinctive. Critics point out that some details became more elaborate in later retellings and that a single witness account is difficult to verify independently.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falcon_Lake_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Falcon Lake Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Falcon Lake Incident</span></span></span>

## Approaching the landed object

The most significant moment in Michalak’s narrative was his decision to walk toward the object. He said that as he approached, he was close enough to examine the surface and attempt to look inside. He described intense light from the opening that prevented him from clearly seeing the interior.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.experiencersupport.org/declassified/dnd-ufo-report-summary-regarding-ufo-at-falcon-beach-manitoba-may" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: experiencersupport.org">[Experiencer Support]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">experiencersupport.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Experiencer Support DND UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Experiencer SupportDND UFO Report Summary: Regarding UFO at Falcon Beach, Manitoba – May 20 1967 &#124; The Experiencer Support Association...</span></span></span>

Michalak’s reported actions at this stage are central to why the Falcon Lake case became known as a “close encounter” rather than simply a sighting. He was not describing an object seen briefly in the sky; he claimed to have been within a few feet of a physical structure and close enough to touch it. He said the object felt extremely hot, and that contact with it damaged his glove.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://library-archives.canada.ca/eng/collection/engage-learn/podcast/Pages/ufo-falcon-lake-incident.aspx?wbdisable=true" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: library-archives.canada.ca">[Library and Archives Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">library-archives.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Library and Archives Canada UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Library and Archives CanadaUFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident - Part 1May 15, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 15, 2019</span></span></span>

After this interaction, Michalak said the object began changing position. In his account, the opening closed, the craft rotated, and a section containing what appeared to be openings or vents turned toward him. He reported hearing mechanical sounds and feeling a force of hot air or gas directed at him.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.experiencersupport.org/declassified/dnd-ufo-report-summary-regarding-ufo-at-falcon-beach-manitoba-may" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: experiencersupport.org">[Experiencer Support]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">experiencersupport.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Experiencer Support DND UFO Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Experiencer SupportDND UFO Report Summary: Regarding UFO at Falcon Beach, Manitoba – May 20 1967 &#124; The Experiencer Support Association...</span></span></span>

He removed damaged clothing and began leaving the area, feeling ill and disoriented. ## The departure blast and aftermath

After the object departed, Michalak described experiencing nausea, weakness, and a burning sensation. He left the site and eventually reached a road, where he encountered a highway patrol officer. His condition attracted attention because he had visible burns and appeared unwell.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://library-archives.canada.ca/eng/collection/engage-learn/podcast/Pages/ufo-falcon-lake-incident.aspx?wbdisable=true" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: library-archives.canada.ca">[Library and Archives Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">library-archives.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Library and Archives Canada UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Library and Archives CanadaUFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident - Part 1May 15, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 15, 2019</span></span></span>

The following days transformed the encounter from a private report into a national controversy. The Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP) and Royal Canadian Air Force (RCAF) became involved, with investigators taking statements from Michalak and examining aspects of the reported event. The RCMP interviewed him on 23 and 24 May 1967, only days after the incident. Investigators recorded that he appeared to be suffering from an illness at the time and was unable to immediately guide them to the site.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/engage-learn/podcasts/discover/episode-054.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canada.ca">[Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake incident, part 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake incident, part 2 - Canada.ca...</span></span></span>

## Why Michalak’s timeline remains disputed

The strength of the Falcon Lake case comes from the fact that the encounter was described in detail and followed by immediate reports, but those same details created points of disagreement. Investigators and later researchers examined whether Michalak’s statements remained consistent across interviews, written accounts, and later public retellings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/engage-learn/podcasts/discover/episode-054.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canada.ca">[Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake incident, part 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake incident, part 2 - Canada.ca...</span></span></span>

Supporters of Michalak’s account emphasise that he reported the event before the story became widely public, that he experienced injuries that required attention, and that official agencies investigated rather than dismissing the report immediately.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/engage-learn/podcasts/discover/episode-054.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: canada.ca">[Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake incident, part 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake incident, part 2 - Canada.ca...</span></span></span>

Sceptical analyses focus on issues such as changing descriptions, questions about how some evidence was obtained, and alternative explanations for the injuries and other physical findings. Some researchers have argued that the event may have resulted from an ordinary accident or misinterpretation rather than an unknown craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cliffsnotes.com/study-notes/21251781" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cliffsnotes.com">[CliffsNotes]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cliffsnotes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cliffs Notes Skeptical Reactions to Falcon Lake UFO Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skeptical Reactions to Falcon Lake UFO Incident - CliffsNotesSeptember 4, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 4, 2024</span></span></span>

The lasting significance of Michalak’s testimony is therefore not that it provides a universally accepted explanation, but that it created a rare UFO case where a detailed personal account, a precise date and location, reported physical effects, and official investigation records all became part of the same debate. The timeline of 20 May 1967 remains the foundation of the Falcon Lake controversy because every later interpretation depends on how that original encounter is understood.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://library-archives.canada.ca/eng/collection/engage-learn/podcast/Pages/ufo-falcon-lake-incident.aspx?wbdisable=true" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: library-archives.canada.ca">[Library and Archives Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">library-archives.canada.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Library and Archives Canada UFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Library and Archives CanadaUFOs at LAC: The Falcon Lake Incident - Part 1May 15, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 15, 2019</span></span></span>
