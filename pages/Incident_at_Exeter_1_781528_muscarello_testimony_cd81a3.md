## Introduction

Norman Muscarello’s roadside encounter was the event that transformed the Exeter UFO reports from a local rumour into a nationally discussed investigation. In the early hours of 3 September 1965, the 18-year-old Exeter resident reported seeing a large, silent object with intense red lights while walking along Route 150 near Kensington, New Hampshire. His frightened arrival at the Exeter Police Department led officers Eugene Bertrand and David Hunt to investigate the location, where they reported seeing a similar object. The importance of Muscarello’s testimony lies less in proving what the object was than in how it created a chain of independently recorded observations involving a civilian witness and two police officers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seacoastnh.com/famous-people/link-free-or-die/norman-muscarello-recalls-his-ufo-incident-at-exeter/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seacoastnh.com">[seacoastnh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seacoastnh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Norman Muscarello Recalls His UFO Incident at Exeter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Norman Muscarello Recalls His UFO Incident at Exeter</span></span></span>

## Route 150 encounter and first report

### A frightened witness reaches the police

Muscarello’s sighting began while he was travelling home after visiting his girlfriend in Amesbury, Massachusetts. Because he had recently sold his car, he was hitchhiking and walking portions of the journey. According to later accounts, he was near Kensington on Route 150 at around 2:00 a.m. when he noticed unusual red lights ahead. At first, he reportedly considered the possibility that they belonged to an ordinary emergency vehicle, but he soon concluded that the lights were suspended above the ground rather than attached to a vehicle on the road.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Exeter incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Exeter incident</span></span></span>

Muscarello described seeing a large dark object associated with several bright, pulsating red lights. He later said the object appeared close to the trees and seemed to move towards his position, causing him to drop down beside the road in fear. He then ran to a nearby house seeking help before stopping a passing car that took him to the Exeter Police Department. Contemporary retellings and later interviews describe him as visibly shaken when he arrived, a detail that became important to investigators because his immediate reaction was treated as evidence that he believed he had experienced something frightening rather than a story invented for attention.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seacoastnh.com/famous-people/link-free-or-die/norman-muscarello-recalls-his-ufo-incident-at-exeter/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seacoastnh.com">[seacoastnh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seacoastnh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Norman Muscarello Recalls His UFO Incident at Exeter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Norman Muscarello Recalls His UFO Incident at Exeter</span></span></span>

At the police station, Muscarello reported the event to Officer Reginald “Scratch” Toland, who contacted Officer Eugene Bertrand. Bertrand initially approached the report with caution, but decided to return to the location with Muscarello to determine whether there was an ordinary explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seacoastnh.com/Places-and-Events/NH-History/officer-eugene-bertrand-on-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seacoastnh.com">[seacoastnh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seacoastnh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span></span></span>

### The return to the scene

The decision to revisit the location became the turning point in the Exeter case. Muscarello’s original claim was no longer only a single witness report: Bertrand’s investigation created the opportunity for another observer to evaluate the same location under similar conditions. Bertrand later recalled that Muscarello’s description sounded unusual, but that he would have been inclined to dismiss it had he not subsequently seen something himself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seacoastnh.com/Places-and-Events/NH-History/officer-eugene-bertrand-on-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seacoastnh.com">[seacoastnh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seacoastnh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span></span></span>

Bertrand and Muscarello returned to the area near the reported location, where they said they observed a large object with red lights. Bertrand’s later recollections differed in some details from Muscarello’s description, particularly regarding the object’s apparent size, but both accounts included the same broad features: a dark form, intense red lights, unusual movement, and a lack of obvious engine noise.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seacoastnh.com/Places-and-Events/NH-History/officer-eugene-bertrand-on-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seacoastnh.com">[seacoastnh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seacoastnh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span></span></span>

This transition from a frightened teenager’s account to a report involving a police officer is one of the main reasons Muscarello’s testimony remains significant in discussions of the Exeter incident. The case was not based solely on an isolated anonymous claim; it developed into a multi-witness report within a short period.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/650903exeter_schuessler.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">650903exeter schuessler</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">US09031965-0001THE EXETER, N.H. ## Descriptions of the object and lights

### The core details of Muscarello’s account

Muscarello’s description remained consistent around several key elements:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* A large object near the tree line outside Kensington.
* Several bright red lights, often described in later accounts as flashing or pulsating.
* Very little or no sound despite the object’s apparent size and proximity.
* A hovering or slow-moving appearance rather than normal aircraft behaviour.
* A strong feeling that the object was unusually close.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Exeter incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Exeter incident</span></span></span>

</div>

Later versions of the story often include an estimate that the object was roughly 80 to 90 feet across, although size estimates from night-time observations are inherently uncertain because witnesses must judge distance without clear reference points. Muscarello’s description of the object’s closeness was therefore one of the most memorable aspects of his testimony, but also one of the areas most vulnerable to disagreement between eyewitness interpretation and objective measurement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.incidentatexeter.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: incidentatexeter.com">[Incident at Exeter]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">incidentatexeter.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on incidentatexeter.com.</span></span></span>

The red lights became the defining feature of the sighting. Supporters of the case have argued that the unusual lighting pattern and apparent movement separated the object from conventional aircraft. Skeptical analyses have noted that bright lights viewed at night can create misleading impressions of size, speed, and distance, particularly when the observer is surprised or frightened.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Exeter incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Exeter incident</span></span></span>

### Emotional state and witness reliability

Muscarello’s emotional reaction has been interpreted in two different ways. Those who regard the case as compelling argue that his fear, immediate police report, and willingness to return to the location support his sincerity. The fact that Bertrand, a police officer, became another witness is viewed as strengthening the credibility of the original report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seacoastnh.com/Places-and-Events/NH-History/officer-eugene-bertrand-on-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seacoastnh.com">[seacoastnh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seacoastnh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span></span></span>

A more cautious interpretation separates sincerity from accuracy. A witness can honestly describe an experience while still misjudging what they saw. Night-time observations, especially involving unusual lights, are affected by uncertainty over distance, scale, movement, and background reference points. For this reason, Muscarello’s testimony is often considered strong evidence that he experienced something unusual, but not by itself definitive evidence of the object’s identity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Exeter incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Exeter incident</span></span></span>

## How later witnesses supported the account

### Bertrand and Hunt’s corroboration

The strongest feature of Muscarello’s testimony is that it was followed by reports from two Exeter police officers. Officer Eugene Bertrand returned to the site with Muscarello and reported seeing a similar object. Officer David Hunt later arrived and also reported observing the phenomenon. Their involvement gave the case unusual weight because the central witnesses were not simply repeating Muscarello’s story; they reported their own observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seacoastnh.com/Places-and-Events/NH-History/officer-eugene-bertrand-on-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seacoastnh.com">[seacoastnh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seacoastnh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span></span></span>

In a recorded interview years later, he described initially doubting the teenager’s story but acknowledged that he and other officers could not arrive at a satisfactory explanation for what they reported seeing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seacoastnh.com/Places-and-Events/NH-History/officer-eugene-bertrand-on-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seacoastnh.com">[seacoastnh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seacoastnh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span></span></span>

The police testimony also introduced complexity. The officers’ descriptions were not identical to Muscarello’s. Differences appeared in estimates of size, distance, and the object’s exact appearance. Rather than eliminating the value of the testimony, these variations illustrate a central problem in eyewitness investigations: independent observers often agree on broad features while differing on measurements and interpretation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seacoastnh.com/Places-and-Events/NH-History/officer-eugene-bertrand-on-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seacoastnh.com">[seacoastnh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seacoastnh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Officer Eugene Bertrand on UFOs</span></span></span>

### From local report to famous case file

Muscarello’s account became the foundation for wider investigations by journalists, UFO researchers, and the U.S. Air Force’s Project Blue Book. Author John G. Fuller’s reporting and later book *Incident at Exeter* helped make Muscarello one of the best-known witnesses associated with the case. Decades later, Muscarello continued to discuss the event in interviews and maintained that he did not know what he had seen, while still believing the experience was genuine.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seacoastnh.com/famous-people/link-free-or-die/norman-muscarello-recalls-his-ufo-incident-at-exeter/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seacoastnh.com">[seacoastnh.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seacoastnh.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Norman Muscarello Recalls His UFO Incident at Exeter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Norman Muscarello Recalls His UFO Incident at Exeter</span></span></span>

The lasting importance of Muscarello’s testimony is therefore not simply the description of a strange object on a New Hampshire roadside. His report created the evidential structure that made Exeter famous: an initial civilian observation, an immediate attempt at verification, and later testimony from police witnesses. The unresolved question is not whether Muscarello reported an experience—records show that he did—but how that experience should be interpreted when strong witness testimony exists without physical evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.incidentatexeter.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: incidentatexeter.com">[Incident at Exeter]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">incidentatexeter.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on incidentatexeter.com.</span></span></span>

    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
