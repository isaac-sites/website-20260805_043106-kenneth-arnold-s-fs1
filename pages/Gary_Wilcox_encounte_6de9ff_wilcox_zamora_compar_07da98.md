## Introduction

The reported encounters of Gary Wilcox and Lonnie Zamora became linked in UFO literature because they occurred on the same date — 24 April 1964 — and shared several striking features: an egg-shaped or oval craft, small humanoid figures, and a close-range encounter with a landed object. The similarities made some researchers argue that the two reports represented a significant pattern, while critics have pointed out that there is no confirmed evidence connecting the witnesses, locations, or events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/occupants_hall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report...</span></span></span>

## The two reported encounters on 24 April 1964

Gary Wilcox’s account placed his encounter in Newark Valley, New York, during the morning of 24 April 1964. He reported seeing a smooth, egg-shaped object in a field and encountering two small figures associated with it. Later accounts of his testimony described the beings as around four feet tall, wearing shiny clothing, and showing interest in agricultural matters. UFO researchers often highlighted the unusual farming-related details of the report, including the alleged discussion of fertiliser and plant material.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/occupants_hall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report...</span></span></span>

Several hours later, in Socorro, New Mexico, police officer Lonnie Zamora reported encountering an oval metallic object near the ground while responding to what he initially believed might be an accident. Zamora described two small figures in white clothing near the object. After he approached, the object allegedly produced a loud noise, rose from the area, and departed. The incident was investigated by local authorities and the U.S. Air Force’s Project Blue Book, which documented the case as one of its notable unidentified reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bluebook.one/case/62-HQ-83894/serial/438" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bluebook.one">[BlueBook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bluebook.one</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FBI Case 62-HQ-83894 Serial 438: Socorro, New Mexico UFO Sighting, Officer Lonnie Zamora, April 24, 1964 &#124; BlueBook...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 24, 1964</span></span></span>

The timing is one of the main reasons the cases are repeatedly discussed together. Both reports came from 24 April 1964, but they were separated by roughly 2,000 miles and by several hours. No documentary evidence has established communication between the witnesses or a common source for their descriptions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/occupants_hall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report...</span></span></span>

## Similarities and differences between the accounts

The strongest similarity is the combination of an unusual craft shape and reported occupants. UFO catalogues from the period grouped both cases among 1964 occupant reports, noting that Wilcox described an egg-shaped craft with two four-foot-tall beings, while Zamora described an egg-shaped object with two similarly sized figures nearby.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/occupants_hall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report...</span></span></span>

The visual overlap attracted attention because the details were more specific than a typical distant UFO sighting. Both witnesses described:

* Two small humanoid figures associated with the craft.

However, the differences are equally important. Wilcox’s report centred on communication and an apparent exchange of information, with the figures portrayed as calm and interested in agriculture. Zamora’s account involved a sudden encounter with a police officer who observed the figures briefly before they moved back to the craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/occupants_hall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report...</span></span></span>

The [physical evidence]({{ 'evidence-debate-4fe7b7/' | relative_url }}) also separates the cases. Wilcox’s account is known mainly through his testimony and later investigations, with no widely accepted physical trace from the alleged landing. Zamora’s case received additional attention because investigators examined reported landing impressions, scorched vegetation, and other site details after the event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://decur.org/documents/socorro-blue-book-investigation-1964" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: decur.org">[DECUR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">decur.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Socorro / Zamora Blue Book Investigation Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Socorro / Zamora Blue Book Investigation Report - DECUR...</span></span></span>

Another difference is the witnesses’ circumstances. Wilcox was a farmer reporting an unusual private encounter in a rural setting. Zamora was an on-duty police officer whose report was quickly communicated to other officials, creating a chain of contemporary documentation. This difference has led many researchers, including some who accept that Zamora had a genuine unexplained experience, to treat the two cases as similar in theme but unequal in evidential strength.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bluebook.one/case/62-HQ-83894/serial/438" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bluebook.one">[BlueBook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bluebook.one</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FBI Case 62-HQ-83894 Serial 438: Socorro, New Mexico UFO Sighting, Officer Lonnie Zamora, April 24, 1964 &#124; BlueBook...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 24, 1964</span></span></span>

## Why the connection remains debated

The same-day coincidence is the main reason the Wilcox and Zamora reports continue to be paired. For supporters of the connection, the combination of date, craft shape, and occupant descriptions appears unusual. They argue that two independent witnesses describing similar objects and beings within hours of each other deserves attention.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/occupants_hall.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Alien Encounters, UFO Occupants, Humanoid Report...</span></span></span>

Sceptical interpretations focus on the limits of the evidence. Similar descriptions do not necessarily demonstrate a shared event, especially when the reports lack independent confirmation linking them together. A resemblance between accounts can result from many factors, including common cultural imagery, later retellings, or the way unusual experiences are interpreted and recorded.
