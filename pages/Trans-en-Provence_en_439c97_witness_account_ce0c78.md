## Introduction

Renato Nicolai’s account is the human centre of the [Trans-en-Provence]({{ 'trans-en-provence-encounter-1981/' | relative_url }}) encounter of 8 January 1981. He reported seeing a small, oval, metallic-looking object descend into his garden, remain on or just above the ground for a short time, and then depart rapidly. The importance of his testimony came from what followed: investigators did not rely on the sighting alone, but compared his description with physical traces found at the site and later laboratory analyses. The French space agency’s investigation did not establish what the object was, but it treated Nicolai’s testimony as a serious account associated with a documented physical event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/48917" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TRANS-EN-PROVENCE (83) 08.01.1981 &#124; GEIPANJuly 16, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 16, 2018</span></span></span>

Nicolai’s report remains significant because it is unusually specific for a single-witness case. His statement included the object’s shape, estimated dimensions, movement, sound, distance from him, and the sequence of its arrival and departure. ## Nicolai’s reported sighting and timeline

### The afternoon encounter on 8 January 1981

According to Nicolai’s testimony recorded during the investigation, the event occurred at around 17:00 on 8 January 1981 at his property in Trans-en-Provence, in southern France. He was carrying out work in his garden when his attention was drawn by a sound he described as a whistling noise. Turning towards the source, he saw an object descending from the air near the lower part of his property.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/48917" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TRANS-EN-PROVENCE (83) 08.01.1981 &#124; GEIPANJuly 16, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 16, 2018</span></span></span>

Nicolai described the object as having an oval or disc-like form, similar to two plates placed together edge-to-edge. In his account, it had a lead-grey appearance, a raised rim around its circumference, and visible features underneath that he interpreted as possible supports or mechanisms. The investigation record later summarised his estimated dimensions as roughly 1.5 metres in height, with the object observed from a distance of around 30 metres.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.geipan.fr/en/node/48917" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: geipan.fr">[Geipan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">geipan.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TRANS-EN-PROVENCE (83) 08.01.1981 &#124; GEIPANJuly 16, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 16, 2018</span></span></span>

The reported sequence was brief. Nicolai said the object descended to the lower terrace area of the garden, remained there only for a short period, then rose vertically before moving away rapidly. His observation was later reconstructed by investigators as lasting on the order of several tens of seconds rather than minutes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-fr-gepan-tn16-trans-en-provence-1983-gepan-technical-note-16-trans-en-provence-1983" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyGEPAN Technical Note 16, Trans-en-Provence Investigation (Enquête 81/01), GEPAN / CNES (Centre national d&#x27;études spatiale...</span></span></span>

The witness did not initially describe the event as an extraterrestrial encounter. According to the investigation record, he considered the possibility that it might have been some type of experimental military device, partly because of the region’s military activity. This detail has been noted as part of his initial interpretation: he attempted to fit the experience into a conventional framework rather than immediately presenting it as a mystery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-fr-gepan-tn16-trans-en-provence-1983-gepan-technical-note-16-trans-en-provence-1983" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyGEPAN Technical Note 16, Trans-en-Provence Investigation (Enquête 81/01), GEPAN / CNES (Centre national d&#x27;études spatiale...</span></span></span>

### From personal observation to formal investigation

The transition from private observation to official inquiry began the following day. Nicolai showed the area to others, and local gendarmes became involved. On 9 January 1981, investigators visited the site, photographed the marks, interviewed Nicolai, and collected initial samples. The case was later transferred to GEPAN (Groupe d’Étude des Phénomènes Aérospatiaux Non-Identifiés), the French national space agency CNES’s specialised study group for unidentified aerospace phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-fr-gepan-tn16-trans-en-provence-1983-gepan-technical-note-16-trans-en-provence-1983" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyGEPAN Technical Note 16, Trans-en-Provence Investigation (Enquête 81/01), GEPAN / CNES (Centre national d&#x27;études spatiale...</span></span></span>

The timing of this response became one of the case’s notable features. Because investigators arrived relatively soon after the reported event, they were able to document the location and gather material samples before the site had substantially changed. The physical investigation was therefore built around a direct comparison: whether Nicolai’s description of a brief landing or contact event matched the marks and environmental effects found in the garden.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-fr-gepan-tn16-trans-en-provence-1983-gepan-technical-note-16-trans-en-provence-1983" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyGEPAN Technical Note 16, Trans-en-Provence Investigation (Enquête 81/01), GEPAN / CNES (Centre national d&#x27;études spatiale...</span></span></span>

## Credibility questions and corroboration limits

Nicolai’s account has often been discussed in terms of credibility rather than proof. Investigators considered several factors when assessing his testimony: the consistency of his descriptions, his willingness to report the event, the absence of obvious contradictions, and the relationship between his account and the physical traces examined afterwards. GEPAN’s approach was not to treat the testimony as conclusive evidence, but as one element in a wider investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-fr-gepan-tn16-trans-en-provence-1983-gepan-technical-note-16-trans-en-provence-1983" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyGEPAN Technical Note 16, Trans-en-Provence Investigation (Enquête 81/01), GEPAN / CNES (Centre national d&#x27;études spatiale...</span></span></span>

A factor supporting the seriousness of the account was that Nicolai did not appear to seek immediate publicity. The initial report was handled through local authorities rather than through UFO organisations or media channels. Later coverage and interviews brought wider attention to him, but the original investigation began with a conventional police-style response.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-fr-gepan-tn16-trans-en-provence-1983-gepan-technical-note-16-trans-en-provence-1983" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyGEPAN Technical Note 16, Trans-en-Provence Investigation (Enquête 81/01), GEPAN / CNES (Centre national d&#x27;études spatiale...</span></span></span>

However, the testimony also has important limitations. Nicolai was the only person who claimed to have seen the object. Neighbours and investigators could examine the aftermath, but no second observer independently confirmed the object’s appearance, movement, or departure. This means the physical evidence could be compared with his description, but the sighting itself remained a single-witness report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-fr-gepan-tn16-trans-en-provence-1983-gepan-technical-note-16-trans-en-provence-1983" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyGEPAN Technical Note 16, Trans-en-Provence Investigation (Enquête 81/01), GEPAN / CNES (Centre national d&#x27;études spatiale...</span></span></span>

## How investigators assessed the testimony

The GEPAN investigation treated Nicolai’s statement as a structured data source rather than simply as a narrative. Investigators separated different parts of the report: the witness’s position, the object’s estimated dimensions, its trajectory, the sounds reported, the duration of observation, and the claimed interaction with the ground. This allowed the testimony to be compared with photographs, measurements, environmental information, and laboratory results.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-fr-gepan-tn16-trans-en-provence-1983-gepan-technical-note-16-trans-en-provence-1983" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyGEPAN Technical Note 16, Trans-en-Provence Investigation (Enquête 81/01), GEPAN / CNES (Centre national d&#x27;études spatiale...</span></span></span>

The physical traces shaped how Nicolai’s testimony was judged. Investigators found a disturbed area of ground and carried out soil and vegetation analyses, creating a rare situation in which a witness account could be tested against measurable effects. Critics argue that physical traces do not automatically prove the witness’s interpretation of what produced them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-fr-gepan-tn16-trans-en-provence-1983-gepan-technical-note-16-trans-en-provence-1983" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyGEPAN Technical Note 16, Trans-en-Provence Investigation (Enquête 81/01), GEPAN / CNES (Centre national d&#x27;études spatiale...</span></span></span>
