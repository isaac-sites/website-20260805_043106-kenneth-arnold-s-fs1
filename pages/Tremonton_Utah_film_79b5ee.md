## Introduction

The central historical question is not whether the film exists or whether Newhouse genuinely recorded something unusual; those points are well established. ## The 1952 sighting and the film

### Delbert Newhouse’s observation

On the morning of 2 July 1952, Newhouse was driving with his wife and two children on State Highway 30, approximately seven miles from Tremonton in northern Utah. His wife first noticed a group of bright objects in the sky. Newhouse stopped the car, observed the objects, and retrieved his 16mm Bell & Howell Automaster camera with a 3-inch telephoto lens. He filmed approximately 30 feet of film, capturing about 1,200 frames over roughly 75 seconds.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.ncas.org/condon/text/case49.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.ncas.org">[NCAS Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.ncas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Files Condon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NCAS FilesCondon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah - Movie FilmJuly 2, 1952...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 1952</span></span></span>

Newhouse described seeing around ten or twelve objects moving in a loose formation. He reported that they appeared bright and metallic, and later described their appearance as similar to two saucers joined together. He also stated that he could not determine their exact size, distance, or speed because there was no fixed reference point in the sky. This limitation became one of the most important issues in later analysis: without reliable distance information, apparent speed and size calculations become highly uncertain.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.ncas.org/condon/text/case49.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.ncas.org">[NCAS Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.ncas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Files Condon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NCAS FilesCondon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah - Movie FilmJuly 2, 1952...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 1952</span></span></span>

Newhouse’s background added weight to the report. He was a Navy photographic specialist with extensive experience in aerial photography, and investigators considered him a credible witness. His professional familiarity with cameras and aircraft photography was one reason the film received serious attention from military analysts rather than being dismissed as a simple civilian report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/tremonton.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Tremonton, Utah, UFO Color Film of July 2, 1952...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 1952</span></span></span>

## How the Air Force investigated the footage

The film was submitted through Navy channels and eventually examined by the US Air Force’s Air Technical Intelligence Center as part of Project Blue Book, the Air Force programme investigating UFO reports. Analysts studied the footage for months, examining possible explanations including aircraft, balloons, reflections, and birds.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.ncas.org/condon/text/case49.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.ncas.org">[NCAS Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.ncas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Files Condon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NCAS FilesCondon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah - Movie FilmJuly 2, 1952...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 1952</span></span></span>

Early investigators found some aspects difficult to reconcile with ordinary aircraft. The objects appeared to move without visible propulsion, and witnesses reported no sound. Some analysts also questioned whether birds could produce the apparent brightness and motion seen in the film. However, the absence of a clear identification did not prove that the objects were extraordinary; it reflected the limitations of the available imagery and measurements.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.ncas.org/condon/text/case49.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.ncas.org">[NCAS Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.ncas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Files Condon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NCAS FilesCondon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah - Movie FilmJuly 2, 1952...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 1952</span></span></span>

The case was also reviewed by the Robertson Panel in 1953, a group of scientists convened with CIA involvement to examine UFO reports of possible national-security significance. The panel considered the Tremonton film one of the notable cases available at the time. It concluded that a conventional explanation, particularly birds reflecting sunlight, was more likely than an unknown technological craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/how-to-investigate-a-flying-saucer/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cia.gov">[CIA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cia.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">How To Investigate a Flying Saucer</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">How To Investigate a Flying Saucer - CIA...</span></span></span>

## The bird explanation and later analysis

The most influential reassessment came from the University of Colorado UFO project, commonly associated with the Condon Report published in 1968. Astronomer William K. Hartmann examined the Tremonton footage and concluded that the objects were most likely white birds, probably gulls. His analysis argued that the movements, brightness changes, and apparent group behaviour matched birds viewed at a distance through a telephoto lens.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.ncas.org/condon/text/case49.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.ncas.org">[NCAS Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.ncas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Files Condon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NCAS FilesCondon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah - Movie FilmJuly 2, 1952...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 1952</span></span></span>

Several details supported this interpretation:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* White birds in sunlight can appear as bright, featureless points when photographed from a distance.
* A flock of birds can appear to move unpredictably as individuals turn, bank, and change position.
* The film lacked reliable scale information, making estimates of speed and distance highly uncertain.
* The Tremonton area contained suitable environments for large numbers of birds.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.ncas.org/condon/text/case49.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.ncas.org">[NCAS Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.ncas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Files Condon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NCAS FilesCondon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah - Movie FilmJuly 2, 1952...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 1952</span></span></span>

The Condon Report stated that the film itself was consistent with birds and that the case did not establish the existence of extraordinary aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.ncas.org/condon/text/case49.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.ncas.org">[NCAS Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.ncas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Files Condon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NCAS FilesCondon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah - Movie FilmJuly 2, 1952...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 1952</span></span></span>

## Why the film remains debated

The continuing interest in the Tremonton film comes from the tension between witness credibility and photographic ambiguity. Newhouse was considered a reliable observer, and he genuinely recorded something he could not identify at the time. That does not mean the objects were necessarily unknown in a broader scientific sense; it means the available evidence was insufficient for a definitive identification in 1952.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/tremonton.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs at close sight: the Tremonton, Utah, UFO Color Film of July 2, 1952...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 1952</span></span></span>

Supporters of an unexplained interpretation have pointed to the fact that military and civilian analysts spent significant effort studying the film and that some early reviewers did not immediately identify the objects. They argue that the footage remains unusual because it shows apparent manoeuvring and brightness changes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/reports/utah6.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Tremonton, Utah UFO Color Film</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Tremonton, Utah UFO Color Film - NICAP Synopsis...</span></span></span>

Sceptical investigators counter that the film’s weaknesses are exactly what make misidentification likely. The objects were filmed against a featureless sky, without landmarks, radar data, or independent measurements of distance and velocity. ## Historical significance of the Tremonton film

The Tremonton, Utah film became one of the classic cases of the early flying-saucer era because it represented a rare combination of documentation and official attention. Unlike many UFO reports based only on eyewitness testimony, it provided physical media that could be repeatedly examined. This made it an important example in debates about how photographic evidence should be evaluated.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/how-to-investigate-a-flying-saucer/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cia.gov">[CIA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cia.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">How To Investigate a Flying Saucer</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">How To Investigate a Flying Saucer - CIA...</span></span></span>

Its legacy is less about proving or disproving extraterrestrial visitation and more about demonstrating the challenges of interpreting distant aerial imagery. The case shows how a credible witness, a genuine recording, and extensive investigation can still leave room for competing explanations when key measurements are missing. The prevailing scientific assessment today is that the Tremonton film most likely shows birds, while the event remains a landmark example of how unidentified does not necessarily mean unexplained.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.ncas.org/condon/text/case49.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.ncas.org">[NCAS Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.ncas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Files Condon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NCAS FilesCondon Report, Case 49: Tremonton, Utah - Movie FilmJuly 2, 1952...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 1952</span></span></span>

    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
