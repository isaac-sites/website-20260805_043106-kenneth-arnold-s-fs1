## Introduction

The [Greenhaw encounter]({{ 'greenhaw-encounter-1973/' | relative_url }}) of 1973 became one of the best-known “humanoid” UFO stories of the 1970s because its central witness was not a private enthusiast but Jeff Greenhaw, the police chief of Falkville, Alabama. The reported sequence was straightforward: a late-night call about a possible UFO landing led Greenhaw to investigate, he said he encountered a reflective, human-shaped figure on a rural road, and he took Polaroid photographs that later became the defining evidence of the case. The lasting controversy has never been only about what Greenhaw saw, but about how to interpret a police officer’s testimony, the surviving images, and the circumstances surrounding a brief roadside encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://appalachianhistorian.org/the-falkville-metal-man-jeff-greenhaw-and-alabamas-silver-suited-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: appalachianhistorian.org">[Appalachianhistorian.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">appalachianhistorian.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Falkville Metal Man: Jeff Greenhaw and Alabama’s Silver-Suited Mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">24, 2026...</span></span></span>

The event took place on 17 October 1973 during a period of intense UFO reporting in the United States. Within hours, the story moved from a small Alabama community into regional newspapers and later UFO literature, where it became known as the “Falkville Metal Man” case. The timeline of that night and Greenhaw’s own account remain the foundation for understanding why the incident became a UFO legend.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://appalachianhistorian.org/the-falkville-metal-man-jeff-greenhaw-and-alabamas-silver-suited-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: appalachianhistorian.org">[Appalachianhistorian.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">appalachianhistorian.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Falkville Metal Man: Jeff Greenhaw and Alabama’s Silver-Suited Mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">24, 2026...</span></span></span>

## The October 1973 report and response

### The call that started the investigation

According to later accounts of the incident, Greenhaw was at home when he received a report of unusual activity near Falkville. A caller claimed that a UFO had landed in a field outside town, prompting the young police chief to investigate. Sources differ on details of the caller’s identity and the exact wording of the original report, but the consistent element is that Greenhaw responded in his official capacity rather than setting out as a UFO investigator.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://appalachianhistorian.org/the-falkville-metal-man-jeff-greenhaw-and-alabamas-silver-suited-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: appalachianhistorian.org">[Appalachianhistorian.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">appalachianhistorian.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Falkville Metal Man: Jeff Greenhaw and Alabama’s Silver-Suited Mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">24, 2026...</span></span></span>

Greenhaw travelled to the area in a patrol vehicle and searched the reported location. He later said that he found no obvious sign of a landed object, lights, or other evidence of a spacecraft. After spending time checking the area, he was reportedly close to concluding that the call had been a prank when he encountered something unexpected on a nearby road.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://appalachianhistorian.org/the-falkville-metal-man-jeff-greenhaw-and-alabamas-silver-suited-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: appalachianhistorian.org">[Appalachianhistorian.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">appalachianhistorian.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Falkville Metal Man: Jeff Greenhaw and Alabama’s Silver-Suited Mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">24, 2026...</span></span></span>

This transition — from a routine police response to an extraordinary personal encounter — is central to the case’s appeal. The story was not built around a distant light in the sky but around a sequence in which a local officer followed a normal investigative procedure and then claimed to have confronted something he could not identify.

### The roadside encounter

Greenhaw’s account describes seeing a figure standing in or near the roadway. He said the figure appeared humanoid but was covered in a highly reflective material that gave it a metallic appearance. Later descriptions attributed to Greenhaw compared the brightness of the surface to a polished substance, with the figure appearing smooth and unusual under vehicle lights. Some accounts also describe a head and neck that seemed merged together and an antenna-like feature, although these details come primarily from later retellings of his testimony.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.paranormalcatalog.net/ufos/the-falkville-metal-man" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: paranormalcatalog.net">[PARANORMAL CATALOG]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">paranormalcatalog.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">PARANORMAL CATALOGThe Falkville Metal Man</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">February 3, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 3, 2023</span></span></span>

Greenhaw said he initially approached the figure as he might approach any person needing assistance. According to his later statements, the encounter quickly became stranger when he realised the figure did not appear to be an ordinary person. He reported taking several Polaroid photographs before the figure moved away.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://thehiddentruth.co/1973-police-chief-photographs-spaceman-from-ufo-va-109/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thehiddentruth.co">[thehiddentruth.co]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thehiddentruth.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1973 police chief photographs spaceman from ufo va 109</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">1973: Police Chief Photographs Spaceman From &#124; The Hidden Truth...</span></span></span>

The reported escape became another memorable part of the legend. Greenhaw stated that the figure moved away at unusual speed and that he attempted to follow it in his vehicle but could not keep pace. Supporters of the account have treated this as one of the more unusual aspects of the testimony, while sceptics have argued that darkness, distance, and confusion could have affected the perception of movement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://thehiddentruth.co/1973-police-chief-photographs-spaceman-from-ufo-va-109/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thehiddentruth.co">[thehiddentruth.co]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thehiddentruth.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1973 police chief photographs spaceman from ufo va 109</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">1973: Police Chief Photographs Spaceman From &#124; The Hidden Truth...</span></span></span>

## Greenhaw’s account as police chief

Greenhaw’s role as a police officer strongly influenced how the story was received. A report from an anonymous civilian might have attracted curiosity, but a serving police chief claiming to have photographed a strange figure gave the event a different level of public attention. The combination of official position and unusual claim helped the story spread beyond Falkville.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://appalachianhistorian.org/the-falkville-metal-man-jeff-greenhaw-and-alabamas-silver-suited-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: appalachianhistorian.org">[Appalachianhistorian.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">appalachianhistorian.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Falkville Metal Man: Jeff Greenhaw and Alabama’s Silver-Suited Mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">24, 2026...</span></span></span>

At the same time, his position also placed him under greater scrutiny. The case depended heavily on Greenhaw’s personal description because the photographs did not provide a clear identification of what the figure was. The images showed a bright, reflective form in darkness, but they did not reveal enough detail to independently determine whether the object was an unknown entity, a costume, protective clothing, or another ordinary explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cryptopia.us/site/2010/03/metal-man-of-falkville-alabama-usa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cryptopia.us">[Cryptopia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cryptopia.us</span><span class="citation-popover-title">FALKVILLE METAL MAN (ALABAMA, USA) &#124; Cryptopia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FALKVILLE METAL MAN (ALABAMA, USA) &#124; Cryptopia - Exploring The Hidden World...</span></span></span>

The police-chief element therefore created a double effect: it increased the credibility of the initial report for many readers, but it also raised the stakes of any challenge to Greenhaw’s interpretation. Once the story became public, his reputation became closely tied to the case.

### The photographs and the limits of the evidence

The Polaroid photographs became the main physical record of the encounter. Their importance came from the fact that they appeared to preserve an image of the figure rather than leaving the case as a purely verbal report. ## The case’s place in UFO history

The Falkville encounter became memorable because it arrived at a moment when reports of strange beings and close encounters were receiving unusually high attention. The year 1973 included several widely discussed UFO reports, including the Pascagoula incident in Mississippi earlier in October. Falkville’s story appeared within that wider atmosphere of public fascination with humanoid encounters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://appalachianhistorian.org/the-falkville-metal-man-jeff-greenhaw-and-alabamas-silver-suited-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: appalachianhistorian.org">[Appalachianhistorian.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">appalachianhistorian.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Falkville Metal Man: Jeff Greenhaw and Alabama’s Silver-Suited Mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">24, 2026...</span></span></span>

This gave it a stronger historical footprint than many similar claims, even though the central mystery remained unresolved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://appalachianhistorian.org/the-falkville-metal-man-jeff-greenhaw-and-alabamas-silver-suited-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: appalachianhistorian.org">[Appalachianhistorian.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">appalachianhistorian.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Falkville Metal Man: Jeff Greenhaw and Alabama’s Silver-Suited Mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">24, 2026...</span></span></span>

The encounter’s endurance comes from the tension between a familiar setting and an extraordinary claim. A rural police call, a patrol car, a roadside meeting, and instant-camera photographs are ordinary elements of a local incident. The interpretation of what appeared in those photographs is what transformed a short October night in Alabama into a lasting UFO legend.

Today, the Greenhaw encounter is best understood as a case built around competing readings of the same event. The known timeline shows that Greenhaw investigated a reported sighting, claimed to encounter a metallic-looking figure, and produced photographs that attracted decades of debate. What remains disputed is not whether the story became part of UFO history, but whether the evidence points to an unexplained encounter or a more ordinary event remembered through extraordinary circumstances.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://appalachianhistorian.org/the-falkville-metal-man-jeff-greenhaw-and-alabamas-silver-suited-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: appalachianhistorian.org">[Appalachianhistorian.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">appalachianhistorian.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Falkville Metal Man: Jeff Greenhaw and Alabama’s Silver-Suited Mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">24, 2026...</span></span></span>

    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
