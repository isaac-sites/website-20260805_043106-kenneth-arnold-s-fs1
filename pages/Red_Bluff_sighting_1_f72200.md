## What happened near Red Bluff on 13 August 1960?

### The police encounter on Hoag Road

Shortly before midnight, Officers Carson and Scott were travelling in a California Highway Patrol vehicle near Corning, south-east of Red Bluff, while searching for a speeding motorcyclist. They reported seeing what first appeared to be an aircraft descending towards the ground, leading them to believe they might be witnessing a crash. According to their account, the object descended to a low altitude, then abruptly reversed direction and climbed before stopping and hovering.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/600813.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Red Bluff UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Red Bluff UFO...</span></span></span>

The officers described the object as round or oval, surrounded by a glow, with red lights at either end and several white lights visible between them. They reported that it moved silently and appeared capable of rapid changes in direction. Carson later stated that the object approached their patrol car closely on more than one occasion and that a red beam of light appeared to sweep the area.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/600813.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Red Bluff UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Red Bluff UFO...</span></span></span>

After watching the object move away, the officers reportedly followed it. They said a second similar object later appeared, and both objects were observed hovering and displaying lights before disappearing towards the eastern horizon. The event was reported to other law-enforcement personnel, some of whom said they had also seen unusual lights that night.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/600813dir.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Red Bluff UFO Case Directory</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Red Bluff UFO Case Directory...</span></span></span>

## Why the witnesses made the case unusual

The Red Bluff report attracted attention because the primary witnesses were police officers rather than casual observers. Carson and Scott produced a formal account through law-enforcement channels, and later investigators interviewed them about their observations. UFO researcher Richard Hall and astronomer J. Allen Hynek both discussed the case in later UFO literature, with Hynek examining it as part of his work connected to Air Force investigations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/600813dir.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Red Bluff UFO Case Directory</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Red Bluff UFO Case Directory...</span></span></span>

A second important feature was the claim of radar confirmation. According to accounts collected by the National Investigations Committee on Aerial Phenomena (NICAP), local personnel initially reported that a radar station had detected an unidentified object, but the Air Force later denied that radar had tracked the sighting. This disagreement became one of the central controversies surrounding the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/600813.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Red Bluff UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Red Bluff UFO...</span></span></span>

However, the radar evidence is difficult to verify today. The surviving public record contains conflicting descriptions of what was seen or reported by radar personnel, and there is no publicly available radar data that independently confirms the object’s identity, location, speed, or movement. As a result, the radar claim remains an important part of the story but not a conclusive physical measurement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.govweird.com/topics/ufo/project-blue-book/red-bluff-area-ca-august-1960" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: govweird.com">[govweird]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">govweird.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">red bluff area ca august 1960</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project Blue Book: Red Bluff Area, California, August 1960 · govweird...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: august 1960</span></span></span>

## The Air Force investigation and its explanation

The Red Bluff case was examined within the framework of Project Blue Book, the United States Air Force programme that investigated UFO reports from 1952 to 1969. The Air Force maintained records of thousands of sightings and classified cases according to whether they were identified, insufficiently documented, or remained unexplained. The project’s files were later transferred to the National Archives.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Project BLUE BOOK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesProject BLUE BOOK - Unidentified Flying Objects &#124; National Archives...</span></span></span>

The Air Force conclusion for the Red Bluff-area reports was that atmospheric effects were responsible. Investigators proposed that temperature inversions — layers of air with unusual temperature patterns that can bend light — combined with smoke and other atmospheric conditions to create a mirage-like effect. The proposed light sources included bright astronomical objects whose images could have been distorted by the atmosphere.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.govweird.com/topics/ufo/project-blue-book/red-bluff-area-ca-august-1960" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: govweird.com">[govweird]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">govweird.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">red bluff area ca august 1960</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project Blue Book: Red Bluff Area, California, August 1960 · govweird...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: august 1960</span></span></span>

This explanation was controversial among UFO researchers because they argued that it did not adequately account for all aspects of the officers’ testimony, particularly the reported movement, proximity, and apparent interaction with the patrol car. NICAP investigators challenged the astronomical explanation and argued that some details of the official account did not match the witnesses’ descriptions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/600813mc.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mc Donald&#x27;s Comments On The Red Bluff Case</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">McDonald&#x27;s Comments On The Red Bluff Case...</span></span></span>

## The main evidence for and against the UFO interpretation

The Red Bluff case is often discussed because it contains both strong-looking evidence and significant limitations.

**Points that supporters consider significant:**


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Experienced witnesses:** Carson and Scott were trained police officers accustomed to observing events in the field. Their account was recorded close to the time of the incident rather than created decades later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/600813.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Red Bluff UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Red Bluff UFO...</span></span></span>
* **Multiple observers:** Other people in the Red Bluff area reportedly saw unusual lights during the same period, creating a broader local sighting pattern.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/ufoe/section_7.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Section 7</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Section 7...</span></span></span>
* **Reported physical effects:** Claims of radio interference and a radar observation, if accurate, would suggest something more than a simple visual misidentification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/600813dir.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[NICAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Red Bluff UFO Case Directory</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Red Bluff UFO Case Directory...</span></span></span>

</div>

**Points that limit the case as proof of an unknown craft:**

* **No recovered object or physical evidence:** There were no materials, photographs, recordings, or measurable traces linked to the sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.govweird.com/topics/ufo/project-blue-book/red-bluff-area-ca-august-1960" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: govweird.com">[govweird]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">govweird.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">red bluff area ca august 1960</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project Blue Book: Red Bluff Area, California, August 1960 · govweird...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: august 1960</span></span></span>

## Why the Red Bluff case still attracts attention

The lasting interest in the Red Bluff sighting comes less from a single piece of decisive evidence and more from the tension between witness credibility and evidential limits. A pair of police officers reported an extraordinary event, and their account was detailed enough to remain memorable. At the same time, extraordinary claims require strong independent evidence, and the case lacks the kind of physical measurements that could settle the question.

The incident also reflects a wider issue in the history of UFO investigations: many famous cases sit between two categories. They are not easily dismissed as simple mistakes because credible people may genuinely have seen something unusual, but they are also not proven examples of unknown technology because observation alone cannot establish what caused the event.     var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
